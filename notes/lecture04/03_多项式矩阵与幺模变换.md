## 一、两种等价写法 / Two descriptions of a polynomial matrix

**中文：** 多项式矩阵既可以看成“元素是多项式的矩阵”，也可以按幂次整理为“系数是常数矩阵的多项式”。非零矩阵的次数是所有元素次数的最大值，最高次系数只要求非零，不要求可逆。

**English:** A polynomial matrix is both a matrix whose entries are polynomials and a polynomial whose coefficients are constant matrices. Its degree is the maximum entry degree. The leading coefficient matrix must be nonzero, but need not be invertible.

$$
A(\lambda)=A_0+A_1\lambda+\cdots+A_d\lambda^d,\quad
A_k\in\mathbb F^{m\times n},\quad A_d\ne0.
$$

**补充例题 / Supplementary example：**

$$
\begin{aligned}
A(\lambda)&=\begin{bmatrix}1+\lambda&\lambda^2\\0&2\end{bmatrix}\\
&=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\0&2\end{bmatrix}
+\lambda\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{bmatrix}
+\lambda^2\begin{bmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{bmatrix}.
\end{aligned}
$$

**中文：** 该矩阵次数为 $2$，最高次系数矩阵奇异。固定形状的全体多项式矩阵在加法、数乘下构成线性空间。乘法按系数卷积，保留矩阵因子的次序。方阵多项式在 $n\ge2$ 时构成非交换的有单位元代数。

**English:** This matrix has degree two and a singular leading coefficient. Polynomial matrices of a fixed size form a vector space under coefficientwise addition and scalar multiplication. Multiplication uses coefficient convolution while retaining matrix-factor order. Square polynomial matrices form a noncommutative unital algebra for $n\ge2$.

$$
\left(\sum_i A_i\lambda^i\right)\left(\sum_j B_j\lambda^j\right)
=\sum_k\left(\sum_{i+j=k}A_iB_j\right)\lambda^k.
$$

**中文：** 对非零乘积，只有 $\deg(AB)\le\deg A+\deg B$，不一定取等号，因为最高次系数的乘积可能为零矩阵。标量多项式环无零因子的结论，也不能直接推广到方阵多项式代数。

**English:** For a nonzero product, only $\deg(AB)\le\deg A+\deg B$ is guaranteed: leading coefficient matrices can have zero product. The absence of zero divisors for scalar polynomials does not extend to the square-matrix polynomial algebra.

## 二、课件 regular 条件与左右除法 / The lecture's regularity condition and division

**中文：** 本节按课件第 32 页的用法：非零方阵多项式 $B(\lambda)$ 的最高次系数 $B_d$ 可逆时，称其满足 regular 条件。于是 $\deg\det B=nd$。还要分别判断 $\det B$ 是否恒零、是否为非零常数，这些条件的含义不同。

**English:** Following slide 32, the lecture's regularity condition requires the leading coefficient $B_d$ of a nonzero square polynomial matrix to be invertible. Then $\deg\det B=nd$. Whether $\det B$ is identically zero or is a nonzero constant must be assessed separately; these are different conditions.

**中文：** 在这个最高次系数可逆的条件下，矩阵长除法可以分别消去左右的最高次项，得到唯一的商与余式：

**English:** With this invertible-leading-coefficient condition, matrix long division cancels leading terms on the appropriate side, giving unique quotients and remainders:

$$
\begin{gathered}
A=Q_rB+R_r,\qquad A=BQ_l+R_l,\\
R_s=0\ \text{or}\ \deg R_s<\deg B\quad(s\in\{r,l\}).
\end{gathered}
$$

**中文：** 最后一条应分别理解为每个余式要么为零、要么次数小于除式次数。课件第 34 页写成 $\deg A$ 是笔误。若只要求小于被除式次数，唯一性会失败，例如 $A=\lambda^2I$、$B=\lambda I$ 既可取 $Q=\lambda I,R=0$，也可取 $Q=(\lambda+1)I,R=-\lambda I$。

**English:** Each remainder must be zero or have degree below the divisor's degree. The bound $\deg A$ on slide 34 is a typo. A bound below the dividend's degree would not ensure uniqueness: for $A=\lambda^2I$ and $B=\lambda I$, both $Q=\lambda I,R=0$ and $Q=(\lambda+1)I,R=-\lambda I$ would qualify.

**中文：** 若当前最高次系数为 $A_m$，右侧除式形式 $Q_rB$ 需要系数 $A_mB_d^{-1}$；左侧除式形式 $BQ_l$ 需要 $B_d^{-1}A_m$。矩阵不交换，因此左右结果一般不同。余式次数界加上 $B_d$ 可逆，也保证了唯一性。

**English:** With current leading coefficient $A_m$, the form $Q_rB$ uses $A_mB_d^{-1}$, while $BQ_l$ uses $B_d^{-1}A_m$. Noncommutativity can therefore give different results. The remainder-degree bound together with invertibility of $B_d$ also proves uniqueness.

**补充例题 / Supplementary example：**

$$
D=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\0&2\end{bmatrix},\quad
C=\begin{bmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{bmatrix},\quad
B=\lambda I-D,\quad A=\lambda C.
$$

$$
A=CB+CD=BC+DC,\qquad
CD=\begin{bmatrix}0&2\\0&0\end{bmatrix},\quad
DC=\begin{bmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** 两种商都是 $C$，但两个常数余式不同；它们的次数都小于 $\deg B=1$。

**English:** Both quotients are $C$, but the constant remainders differ. Both have degree below $\deg B=1$.

## 三、多项式矩阵的秩 / Rank of a polynomial matrix

**中文：** 秩是其非零多项式子式的最高阶数，零矩阵的秩为零。这也等于把元素看作有理函数后，在域 $\mathbb F(\lambda)$ 上求得的秩。不能用某一个数值代入后的秩代替它。

**English:** Rank is the largest order of a minor that is not the zero polynomial; the zero matrix has rank zero. Equivalently, it is rank over the rational-function field $\mathbb F(\lambda)$. The rank at one numerical substitution need not equal it.

**补充例题 / Supplementary example：**

$$
H(\lambda)=\begin{bmatrix}\lambda&0\\0&1\end{bmatrix},\quad
\det H=\lambda\not\equiv0,\quad
\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F(\lambda)}H=2,\quad
\operatorname{rank}H(0)=1.
$$

**中文：** 代入某点可能使原本非零的子式取零值，所以数值代入可使秩下降。

**English:** Substitution can make a nonzero polynomial minor evaluate to zero, so specialized rank can drop.

## 四、幺模：逆仍是多项式矩阵 / Unimodularity: the inverse remains polynomial

**中文：** 幺模方阵多项式 $U$ 必须存在多项式矩阵逆。课件定理 2.20 给出判据：行列式是非零常数。必要性来自 $\det U\det U^{-1}=1$；充分性由伴随矩阵公式得到。

**English:** A unimodular square polynomial matrix must have a polynomial inverse. Theorem 2.20 characterizes this by a nonzero constant determinant. Necessity follows from $\det U\det U^{-1}=1$, and sufficiency from the adjugate formula.

$$
\boxed{U\text{ unimodular}\iff\det U=c\in\mathbb F\setminus\{0\}},\qquad
U^{-1}=\frac{\operatorname{adj}U}{c}.
$$

**补充例题 / Supplementary example：**

$$
U=\begin{bmatrix}1&\lambda\\0&1\end{bmatrix},\quad
U^{-1}=\begin{bmatrix}1&-\lambda\\0&1\end{bmatrix},\quad \det U=1.
$$

**中文：** 前面的 $H=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda,1)$ 有有理函数矩阵逆 $\operatorname{diag}(1/\lambda,1)$，却没有多项式矩阵逆，因此不是幺模。$U$ 是幺模，但其最高次系数奇异，说明幺模不要求课件的 regular 条件。

**English:** The earlier $H=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda,1)$ has rational inverse $\operatorname{diag}(1/\lambda,1)$ but no polynomial inverse, so it is not unimodular. Conversely, $U$ is unimodular despite its singular leading coefficient, showing that unimodularity does not require the lecture's regularity condition.

**中文：** 仅检查所有实数代入后可逆也不充分：一阶矩阵 $[\lambda^2+1]$ 对每个实数取值均非零，但逆 $[1/(\lambda^2+1)]$ 不是多项式。

**English:** Invertibility at every real substitution is insufficient: the one-by-one matrix $[\lambda^2+1]$ is nonzero for every real value, but its inverse $[1/(\lambda^2+1)]$ is not polynomial.

## 五、初等变换与多项式矩阵等价 / Elementary operations and equivalence

**中文：** 允许三种行变换及对应列变换：交换；乘非零常数；把一行的任意多项式倍数加到另一行。非恒定多项式不能直接作为整行倍乘因子，因为它不是多项式环的单位。

**English:** Allowed row operations, and their column analogues, are swapping; multiplication by a nonzero constant; and adding a polynomial multiple of one row to another. Multiplying an entire row by a nonconstant polynomial is not an invertible elementary operation over the polynomial ring.

**中文：** 行变换对应左乘，列变换对应右乘。对应初等矩阵都幺模，逆操作仍有多项式系数。同型多项式矩阵的等价关系为

**English:** Row operations correspond to left multiplication, column operations to right multiplication. The elementary matrices are unimodular and their inverse operations remain polynomial. Equivalence of matrices of the same size is characterized by

$$
\boxed{B(\lambda)=P(\lambda)A(\lambda)Q(\lambda)},\qquad P,Q\text{ unimodular}.
$$

**补充例题 / Supplementary example：**

$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}\lambda&1\\1&0\end{bmatrix}
\xrightarrow{r_1\leftarrow r_1-\lambda r_2}
\begin{bmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{bmatrix}
\xrightarrow{r_1\leftrightarrow r_2}I.
$$

**中文：** 两步都合法，且 $\det A=-1$，所以它等价于单位矩阵，逆为 $\begin{bmatrix}0&1\\1&-\lambda\end{bmatrix}$。同阶幺模矩阵都等价于单位矩阵，因而彼此等价。

**English:** Both steps are allowed, and $\det A=-1$. It is equivalent to the identity and has inverse $\begin{bmatrix}0&1\\1&-\lambda\end{bmatrix}$. Every unimodular matrix of the same size is equivalent to the identity, so all are mutually equivalent.

**中文：** 多项式矩阵不能仅按秩判等价。例如一阶矩阵 $[\lambda]$ 与 $[1]$ 同秩，但一阶幺模因子只能是非零常数，无法将 $\lambda$ 变成 $1$。必须检查允许的乘法因子属于哪个环。

**English:** Equal rank does not characterize polynomial-matrix equivalence. The one-by-one matrices $[\lambda]$ and $[1]$ have equal rank, but one-by-one unimodular factors are only nonzero constants and cannot turn $\lambda$ into $1$. Always specify the ring in which transformation factors must be invertible.
