## 一、给线性空间增加乘法 / Adding multiplication to a vector space

**中文：** 这里的“线性代数”指一种代数结构：线性空间上另有一个封闭、结合且双线性的内部乘法。它要求向量之间能够相乘，并与原加法、数乘相容。单位元和交换律是额外条件。

**English:** Here a linear algebra is an algebraic structure: a vector space with a closed, associative, bilinear internal multiplication. Vectors can be multiplied compatibly with addition and scalar multiplication. A multiplicative identity and commutativity are additional properties.

$$
a(b+c)=ab+ac,\quad(a+b)c=ac+bc,\quad
(ka)b=a(kb)=k(ab),\quad a(bc)=(ab)c.
$$

**中文：** 方阵在通常乘法下构成有单位元的代数，矩阵阶数至少为 $2$ 时一般不交换；多项式环是交换的有单位元代数。次数至多 $d$ 的多项式空间在 $d\ge1$ 时不对乘法封闭，因为 $\lambda^d\lambda^d=\lambda^{2d}$。

**English:** Square matrices form a unital algebra under ordinary multiplication and are generally noncommutative for matrix size at least two. The polynomial ring is a commutative unital algebra. For $d\ge1$, polynomials of degree at most $d$ are not closed under ordinary multiplication, since $\lambda^d\lambda^d=\lambda^{2d}$.

**中文：** 代数同构既是线性空间同构，还必须保持乘法：$\Phi(ab)=\Phi(a)\Phi(b)$。仅有相同维数不足以证明两个代数同构。

**English:** An algebra isomorphism is a vector-space isomorphism that also preserves multiplication: $\Phi(ab)=\Phi(a)\Phi(b)$. Equal vector-space dimensions alone do not establish algebra isomorphism.

## 二、在代数元素处代入多项式 / Evaluating a polynomial at an algebra element

**中文：** 在有单位元 $e$ 的代数中，定义 $\alpha^0=e$，并令标量多项式 $p(\lambda)=\sum p_i\lambda^i$ 对应 $p(\alpha)=\sum p_i\alpha^i$。因此常数项应写成 $p_0e$；当 $\alpha$ 是矩阵时，常数项为 $p_0I$。

**English:** In an algebra with identity $e$, set $\alpha^0=e$ and evaluate a scalar polynomial by $p(\alpha)=\sum p_i\alpha^i$. Its constant term is $p_0e$, or $p_0I$ when evaluating at a matrix.

$$
(p+q)(\alpha)=p(\alpha)+q(\alpha),\qquad
(pq)(\alpha)=p(\alpha)q(\alpha).
$$

**中文：** 即使整个代数不交换，同一个元素的幂仍满足 $\alpha^i\alpha^j=\alpha^{i+j}$，所以标量多项式的上述代入规则成立。这里系数是标量；不要直接把结论推广到任意不交换的矩阵系数。

**English:** Even in a noncommutative algebra, powers of the same element satisfy $\alpha^i\alpha^j=\alpha^{i+j}$, so these evaluation identities hold for scalar polynomials. The coefficients here are scalars; the conclusion does not automatically extend to arbitrary noncommuting matrix coefficients.

**补充例题 / Supplementary example：**

$$
N=\begin{bmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{bmatrix},\quad N^2=0,\quad
p(\lambda)=(\lambda+1)^2
\Longrightarrow p(N)=I+2N=\begin{bmatrix}1&2\\0&1\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** 代入映射不一定单射：$\lambda^2$ 不是零多项式，但在这个 $N$ 处取值为零矩阵。

**English:** Evaluation need not be injective: $\lambda^2$ is a nonzero polynomial but evaluates to zero at this $N$.

## 三、多项式环为什么是整环 / Why the polynomial ring is an integral domain

**中文：** 域上两个非零多项式的首项系数之积非零，因此乘积非零、次数相加，并且非零因子可消去。和的次数至多为两者次数较大值，首项相消时会下降。

**English:** Over a field, the product of two nonzero leading coefficients is nonzero. Thus nonzero polynomials have nonzero product, degrees add, and cancellation holds. The degree of a nonzero sum is at most the larger degree and can drop through leading-term cancellation.

$$
\deg(pq)=\deg p+\deg q,\qquad
a\ne0,\ ab=ac\Longrightarrow b=c.
$$

**中文：** 多项式环中的可逆元素恰是非零常数。若 $pq=1$，次数相加为零，迫使二者次数都为零。首一多项式的乘积仍首一。

**English:** The units of the polynomial ring are exactly the nonzero constants. If $pq=1$, degree addition forces both degrees to be zero. Products of monic polynomials are monic.

## 四、欧氏除法与唯一性 / Euclidean division and uniqueness

**中文：** 欧氏整环允许使用一个非负整数值函数衡量非零元素，使每次除法的非零余式都比除式“小”。课件还要求对非零 $a,b$ 有 $\nu(a)\le\nu(ab)$。多项式可取 $\nu(p)=\deg p$。

**English:** A Euclidean domain has a nonnegative integer-valued function on nonzero elements such that division leaves a zero remainder or a smaller one. The lecture also requires $\nu(a)\le\nu(ab)$ for nonzero $a,b$. For polynomials, use $\nu(p)=\deg p$.

$$
b\ne0\Longrightarrow a=bq+r,\qquad r=0\ \text{or}\ \deg r<\deg b.
$$

**中文：** 在域上的多项式环中，商与余式唯一。若 $a=bq+r=bq'+r'$，则 $b(q-q')=r'-r$；若 $q\ne q'$，左边次数至少为 $\deg b$，右边却小于它，矛盾。存在性由反复消去被除式的最高次项得到，依赖除式首项系数可逆。

**English:** In a polynomial ring over a field, quotient and remainder are unique. If $b(q-q')=r'-r$ and $q\ne q'$, the left side has degree at least $\deg b$, while the right side has smaller degree, a contradiction. Existence follows by repeatedly canceling the dividend's leading term, using the invertible leading coefficient of the divisor.

**补充例题 / Supplementary example：**

$$
\lambda^3+2\lambda^2+\lambda+1
=(\lambda^2+1)(\lambda+2)-1.
$$

**中文：** 商为 $\lambda+2$，余式为 $-1$，其次数 $0<2$。若余式为零，写 $b\mid a$，表示 $b$ 整除 $a$。

**English:** The quotient is $\lambda+2$ and the remainder is $-1$, whose degree satisfies $0<2$. A zero remainder is written $b\mid a$: $b$ divides $a$.

**中文：** 欧氏整环的存在性公理本身不保证商、余式唯一。例如若整数余式只要求 $|r|<|b|$，则 $5=2\cdot2+1=2\cdot3-1$。通常加上 $0\le r<|b|$ 才得到整数除法的唯一余式。

**English:** The Euclidean-domain existence axiom alone does not guarantee uniqueness. If integer remainders only satisfy $|r|<|b|$, then $5=2\cdot2+1=2\cdot3-1$. The conventional restriction $0\le r<|b|$ gives the unique integer remainder.

## 五、辗转相除 / The Euclidean algorithm

**中文：** 每次用除式替换被除式、用余式替换除式，非零余式的次数严格下降，算法必停止。最后的非零余式首一化后就是最大公因式；反向代入可得到贝祖等式。

**English:** Replace the dividend by the divisor and the divisor by the remainder. Nonzero remainder degrees strictly decrease, so the algorithm terminates. The last nonzero remainder, normalized to be monic, is the gcd. Back-substitution gives a Bezout identity.

**补充例题 / Supplementary example：**

$$
\lambda^3-1=\lambda(\lambda^2-1)+(\lambda-1),\qquad
\lambda^2-1=(\lambda+1)(\lambda-1).
$$

$$
\gcd(\lambda^3-1,\lambda^2-1)=\lambda-1
=(\lambda^3-1)-\lambda(\lambda^2-1).
$$

**中文：** 下一章将这个线性组合与“理想的和”联系起来。

**English:** The next chapter interprets this polynomial combination as a generator of a sum of ideals.
