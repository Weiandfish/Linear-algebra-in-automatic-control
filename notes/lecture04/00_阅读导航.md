# 第四讲中英文教学笔记 / Lecture 4 Bilingual Study Notes

## 阅读方式 / How to study

**中文：** 本套笔记覆盖第四讲全部 40 页，把第 2.1—2.6 节合并成 3 个正文大章，再附导航与练习速查，共 5 个文件。中文后紧跟英文，公式共用；例题保留题设、关键计算和结论。标为“教学补充”的证明与例题用于解释原讲义。

**English:** These notes cover all 40 slides of Lecture 4, grouping Sections 2.1-2.6 into three main chapters plus this guide and an exercise/reference file. Chinese paragraphs are followed by English, with shared formulas. Examples retain their data, essential calculations, and conclusions. Proofs and examples marked “Teaching supplement” explain the lecture material.

**中文：** 一般定义在域 $\mathbb F$ 上讨论；没有特别说明的具体计算取 $\mathbb R$ 或 $\mathbb C$。理想记作 $\langle a\rangle=\{aq:q\in\mathbb F[\lambda]\}$，非零最大公因式和最小公倍式统一取首一形式。多项式矩阵的非零子式，指它作为多项式不恒为零。

**English:** General definitions use a field $\mathbb F$; concrete calculations use $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$ unless stated otherwise. Write $\langle a\rangle=\{aq:q\in\mathbb F[\lambda]\}$, and normalize nonzero greatest common divisors and least common multiples to be monic. A nonzero polynomial-matrix minor means a polynomial that is not identically zero.
