## 一、理想比子空间多一个条件 / An ideal has an absorption property

**中文：** 多项式理想 $I\subseteq\mathbb F[\lambda]$ 首先是线性子空间，还要求：任意 $b\in I$ 乘任意多项式 $a\in\mathbb F[\lambda]$ 后仍在 $I$。这是对整个多项式环的乘法吸收性。

**English:** A polynomial ideal $I\subseteq\mathbb F[\lambda]$ is a linear subspace with an extra property: multiplying any $b\in I$ by any polynomial $a\in\mathbb F[\lambda]$ stays in $I$. This is absorption under multiplication by the whole polynomial ring.

$$
b\in I,\ a\in\mathbb F[\lambda]\Longrightarrow ab\in I.
$$

**中文：** 主理想 $\langle d\rangle$ 是 $d$ 的全部多项式倍数，不是仅由常数倍组成的线性生成空间。零理想为 $\langle0\rangle=\{0\}$；单位理想为 $\langle1\rangle=\mathbb F[\lambda]$，任何非零常数都可生成它。

**English:** The principal ideal $\langle d\rangle$ contains all polynomial multiples of $d$, not merely its scalar multiples. The zero ideal is $\langle0\rangle=\{0\}$; the unit ideal is $\langle1\rangle=\mathbb F[\lambda]$, generated by any nonzero constant.

**补充例题 / Supplementary example：**

$$
\langle\lambda\rangle=\{p:p(0)=0\},\qquad
\langle\lambda^2\rangle\subsetneq\langle\lambda\rangle.
$$

**中文：** 常数多项式构成子空间，却不是理想：$1$ 属于其中，乘 $\lambda$ 后离开它。主理想的包含方向与整除方向相反：$\langle a\rangle\subseteq\langle b\rangle$ 当且仅当 $b\mid a$。

**English:** Constant polynomials form a subspace but not an ideal: they contain $1$, but multiplying by $\lambda$ leaves the subspace. Inclusion of principal ideals reverses divisibility: $\langle a\rangle\subseteq\langle b\rangle$ exactly when $b\mid a$.

## 二、每个多项式理想都是主理想 / Every polynomial ideal is principal

**中文：** 对非零理想 $I$，取其中非零、次数最小的多项式 $d$，再首一化。对任意 $a\in I$ 作除法 $a=dq+r$。由吸收性，$r=a-dq\in I$；若 $r\ne0$，它的次数小于 $d$，矛盾。因此 $I=\langle d\rangle$。

**English:** For a nonzero ideal $I$, choose a nonzero element $d$ of least degree and normalize it to be monic. Divide any $a\in I$ as $a=dq+r$. Absorption gives $r=a-dq\in I$. A nonzero remainder would have smaller degree than $d$, a contradiction. Hence $I=\langle d\rangle$.

**中文：** 两个生成元互为非零常数倍；要求首一后生成元唯一。零理想另由 $0$ 生成。课件定理 2.8 将同一“最小欧氏函数值加除法”的证明推广到欧氏整环。

**English:** Two nonzero generators differ by a nonzero scalar, so requiring monicity makes the generator unique. The zero ideal is generated by zero. Theorem 2.8 extends the same minimum-Euclidean-value and division argument to Euclidean domains.

## 三、和取最大公因式，交取最小公倍式 / Sums use gcd; intersections use lcm

**中文：** 理想的和与交仍是理想。若 $I_1=\langle a\rangle$、$I_2=\langle b\rangle$，则和由全部多项式组合 $ua+vb$ 构成，交由全部共同倍式构成。

**English:** Sums and intersections of ideals remain ideals. For $I_1=\langle a\rangle$ and $I_2=\langle b\rangle$, their sum consists of polynomial combinations $ua+vb$, while their intersection consists of common multiples.

$$
\boxed{\langle a\rangle+\langle b\rangle=\langle\gcd(a,b)\rangle},\qquad
\boxed{\langle a\rangle\cap\langle b\rangle=\langle\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)\rangle}.
$$

**中文：** 和的生成元既整除 $a,b$，又可写成它们的多项式组合，所以任何公因式都整除它。交的生成元是共同倍式，并整除每个共同倍式。这里“最大”“最小”按整除关系理解，不是比较多项式在某点的数值。

**English:** The sum's generator divides $a,b$ and is a polynomial combination of them, so every common divisor divides it. The intersection's generator is a common multiple that divides every common multiple. “Greatest” and “least” refer to divisibility, not values at an evaluation point.

**中文：** 对非零首一 $a,b$，有 $\gcd(a,b)\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)=ab$。分解成相同的一套首一不可约因子后，最大公因式取各重数的最小值，最小公倍式取最大值；缺失因子的重数按 $0$ 计。

**English:** For nonzero monic $a,b$, their monic gcd and lcm have product $ab$. Expressing both in the same monic irreducible factors, the gcd takes minimum exponents and the lcm maximum exponents; an absent factor has exponent zero.

**补充例题：第三次作业第 2 题 / Supplementary example: Homework 3, Problem 2.**

$$
a=(\lambda+1)(\lambda+2)^2,\qquad
b=(\lambda+2)(\lambda+3)(\lambda+5).
$$

$$
\gcd(a,b)=\lambda+2,\qquad
\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)=(\lambda+1)(\lambda+2)^2(\lambda+3)(\lambda+5).
$$

**中文：** 把这两个生成元分别放入尖括号，就得到理想的和与交。两者重数不同，不能把共有因子的平方也放进最大公因式。

**English:** Enclosing these generators in ideal brackets gives the sum and intersection. Their common factor has different multiplicities, so its square does not belong in the gcd.

## 四、互素与贝祖等式 / Coprimality and Bezout identities

**中文：** 课件定理 2.11 给出三个等价条件：一组多项式的最大公因式为 $1$；它们生成单位理想；存在多项式系数使其组合等于 $1$。

**English:** Theorem 2.11 gives three equivalent conditions: the polynomials have gcd one; they generate the unit ideal; and a polynomial combination of them equals one.

$$
\gcd(a_1,\ldots,a_r)=1
\iff\langle a_1\rangle+\cdots+\langle a_r\rangle=\langle1\rangle
\iff\exists u_i:\ \sum_i u_i a_i=1.
$$

**补充例题 / Supplementary example：**

$$
\lambda^2+1=(\lambda+1)(\lambda-1)+2
\Longrightarrow
1=\tfrac12(\lambda^2+1)-\tfrac12(\lambda+1)(\lambda-1).
$$

**中文：** 因此 $\lambda^2+1$ 与 $\lambda-1$ 互素。辗转相除的反向代入给出这样的系数，比只宣布“互素”提供了可直接检验的证据。

**English:** Hence $\lambda^2+1$ and $\lambda-1$ are coprime. Back-substitution in the Euclidean algorithm gives these coefficients, providing a directly checkable certificate.

## 五、不可约分解与互素的层次 / Irreducible factorization and levels of coprimality

**中文：** 正次数多项式不可约，是指不能分成两个正次数因子的乘积。非零常数是单位，不是这里讨论的素多项式。在域上的一元多项式环中，不可约多项式满足素性：$p\mid ab$ 推出 $p\mid a$ 或 $p\mid b$。

**English:** A positive-degree polynomial is irreducible when it cannot be factored into two positive-degree polynomials. Nonzero constants are units, not the prime polynomials under discussion. In a univariate polynomial ring over a field, irreducibles are prime: $p\mid ab$ implies $p\mid a$ or $p\mid b$.

**中文：** 证明要点：若 $p\nmid a$，不可约性给出 $\gcd(p,a)=1$。将贝祖等式 $up+va=1$ 乘以 $b$，由 $p\mid ab$ 得 $p\mid b$。由此得到唯一分解：非零多项式可写成非零常数乘各首一不可约因子的正整数次幂，除因子次序外唯一。

**English:** If $p\nmid a$, irreducibility gives $\gcd(p,a)=1$. Multiply a Bezout identity $up+va=1$ by $b$; since $p\mid ab$, it follows that $p\mid b$. This leads to unique factorization into a nonzero constant times powers of distinct monic irreducibles, unique up to ordering.

$$
a=c\,p_1^{m_1}\cdots p_s^{m_s},\qquad c\ne0,\quad m_i\ge1.
$$

**中文：** 定理 2.14 中，将每个完整素因子幂从 $a$ 中分别除去，所得余因式的整体最大公因式为 $1$。整体互素不等于两两互素。

**English:** Theorem 2.14 says that the cofactors obtained by removing each complete prime-power factor from $a$ have collective gcd one. Collective coprimality is weaker than pairwise coprimality.

**补充反例 / Supplementary counterexample：**

$$
\lambda(\lambda-1),\quad\lambda(\lambda+1),\quad(\lambda-1)(\lambda+1).
$$

**中文：** 三者整体最大公因式为 $1$，但每一对都有非平凡公因式。对于三个及以上多项式，不要把这两种条件混用。

**English:** These three have collective gcd one, but every pair shares a nonconstant factor. For three or more polynomials, the two conditions must be distinguished.

## 六、系数域、根与重数 / Coefficient fields, roots, and multiplicity

**中文：** 代数闭域中每个正次数多项式都有根，进而完全分解为一次因子。代数学基本定理说明 $\mathbb C$ 代数闭。次数为 $n$ 的复多项式有 $n$ 个根是按重数计数，不保证有 $n$ 个不同的根。

**English:** In an algebraically closed field, every positive-degree polynomial has a root and factors into linear factors. The fundamental theorem of algebra states that $\mathbb C$ is algebraically closed. A degree-$n$ complex polynomial has $n$ roots counting multiplicity, not necessarily $n$ distinct roots.

**中文：** 实数域上的首一不可约因子是一元一次式，或判别式为负的二次式；非实根以共轭对出现，重数相同。不可约性依赖系数域。

**English:** Monic irreducibles over $\mathbb R$ are linear factors or quadratics with negative discriminant. Nonreal roots occur in conjugate pairs with equal multiplicities. Irreducibility depends on the coefficient field.

**补充例题：第三次作业第 3 题 / Supplementary example: Homework 3, Problem 3.**

$$
\begin{aligned}
q&=\lambda^2+\lambda+1,\\
a_1&=\lambda^4+\lambda^3-\lambda-1=(\lambda^2-1)q,\\
a_2&=\lambda^4+2\lambda^3+3\lambda^2+2\lambda+1=q^2.
\end{aligned}
$$

**中文：** $q$ 的判别式为 $-3$，在实数域不可约；在复数域可分成两个一次因子。无论使用实或复系数，因 $q(1)=3$、$q(-1)=1$，它都与 $\lambda^2-1$ 互素。因此

**English:** The discriminant of $q$ is $-3$, so it is irreducible over the reals and splits into two linear factors over the complex numbers. Over either field, $q(1)=3$ and $q(-1)=1$ make it coprime to $\lambda^2-1$. Thus

$$
\gcd(a_1,a_2)=q,\qquad
\operatorname{lcm}(a_1,a_2)=(\lambda^2-1)q^2.
$$

**中文：** 求理想和与交时，先做结构化因式分解，再取重数，通常比直接对高次式长除更简洁。

**English:** For ideal sums and intersections, structural factorization followed by exponent comparison is often shorter than long division on the expanded high-degree polynomials.
