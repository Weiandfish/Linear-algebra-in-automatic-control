## 1.5.1 线性映射：保持线性组合 / Linear mappings preserve linear combinations

> 对应 / Source: L2, pp. 21–22；Definition 1.21。

### 定义 / Definition

**中文：** 设 $U,V$ 是同一域 $\mathbb F$ 上的线性空间。映射 $T:U\to V$ 线性，是指它同时保持加法与数乘。讲义对两个不同空间之间的映射也使用 linear transformation；一些教材把这个词专用于 $U=V$ 的情形。本文按讲义使用，并用 $T$ 代替花体映射符号。

**English:** Let $U,V$ be vector spaces over the same field $\mathbb F$. A map $T:U\to V$ is linear if it preserves addition and scalar multiplication. The slides also use “linear transformation” between different spaces, although some texts reserve that term for $U=V$. These notes follow the slides and write $T$ in place of their calligraphic map symbol.

$$
T(u+v)=T(u)+T(v),\qquad T(au)=aT(u).
$$

$$
\boxed{T(au+bv)=aT(u)+bT(v).}
$$

**中文：** 直观上，先在输入空间组合向量再映射，与先分别映射再按同样系数组合，得到相同输出。两个步骤可以交换，这就是“保持线性组合”。

**English:** Combining input vectors and then applying the map gives the same result as mapping them separately and combining the outputs with the same coefficients. This interchange of the two steps is preservation of linear combinations.

### 定义域、陪域、像与值域 / Domain, codomain, image, and range

$$
T:U\to V,\qquad
\operatorname{im}T=T(U)=\{T(u):u\in U\}\subseteq V.
$$

**中文：** $U$ 是定义域，$V$ 是陪域；$T(u)$ 是单个向量的像，$T(U)$ 是所有实际输出构成的像空间或值域。像空间可能只是陪域的一部分；只有 $\operatorname{im}T=V$ 时，映射才是满射。

**English:** The space $U$ is the domain and $V$ is the codomain. The vector $T(u)$ is the image of one input, while $T(U)$ is the image space or range of the map. The image may be only part of the codomain; the map is surjective precisely when $\operatorname{im}T=V$.

### 恒等映射和零映射 / Identity and zero mappings

$$
I:V\to V,\quad I(v)=v;
\qquad O:U\to V,\quad O(u)=0_V.
$$

**中文：** 两者都线性。恒等映射的像是 $V$，核是 $\{0\}$；零映射的像是 $\{0\}$，核是整个定义域 $U$。这两个极端例子有助于检查一般说法是否合理。

**English:** Both are linear. The identity has image $V$ and kernel $\{0\}$; the zero map has image $\{0\}$ and kernel equal to its entire domain $U$. These extreme cases are useful checks on general claims.

### 讲义例子：矩阵乘法产生线性映射 / Lecture example: a matrix defines a linear map

> 对应 / Source: L2, p. 24。

$$
A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n},\qquad T:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^m,\quad T(x)=Ax.
$$

**中文：** 由矩阵分配律和数乘规则，$A(ax+by)=aAx+bAy$，所以 $T$ 线性。$n$ 是输入坐标数，$m$ 是输出坐标数；线性映射的矩阵不必是方阵。

**English:** Matrix distributivity and scalar multiplication give $A(ax+by)=aAx+bAy$, so $T$ is linear. The number $n$ counts input coordinates and $m$ counts output coordinates; the matrix need not be square.

### 如何迅速否定线性？ / How to quickly disprove linearity

**教学补充 / Teaching supplement：**

| 映射 / Map | 判断 / Verdict | 理由 / Reason |
|---|---|---|
| $T(x)=Ax$ | 线性 / Linear | 保持所有线性组合 / Preserves linear combinations |
| $T(x)=Ax+b$，$b\ne0$ | 非线性，属于仿射映射 / Not linear; affine | $T(0)=b\ne0$ |
| $T:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$，$T(x)=x^2$ | 非线性 / Not linear | $T(2)=4\ne2T(1)$ |
| 多项式求导 $D(p)=p'$ / Polynomial differentiation | 线性 / Linear | $(ap+bq)'=ap'+bq'$ |

**中文：** $T(0)=0$ 是必要条件，不是充分条件。平方映射就是满足 $T(0)=0$ 但不线性的反例。要证明线性，最好直接验证任意线性组合；要否定，只需找一组输入与系数使等式失败。

**English:** The condition $T(0)=0$ is necessary but not sufficient. Squaring satisfies it but is not linear. To prove linearity, verify arbitrary linear combinations; to disprove it, find one choice of inputs and coefficients for which the identity fails.

**中文：** 教学补充：线性还依赖标量域。复共轭 $T(z)=\overline z$ 对实标量线性，但对复标量不线性，因为 $T(i\cdot1)=-i\ne iT(1)$。

**English:** Teaching supplement: linearity also depends on the scalar field. Complex conjugation $T(z)=\overline z$ is linear over real scalars but not over complex scalars, since $T(i\cdot1)=-i\ne iT(1)$.

## 1.5.2 基本性质、核与像 / Basic properties, kernel, and image

> 讲义性质对应 / Lecture properties: L2, pp. 23–25。
> 核、秩–零度和单射判定为教学补充 / Kernel, rank–nullity, and injectivity tests are teaching supplements; related null-space material is on p. 11。

### 性质 1：零向量映到零向量 / Property 1: zero maps to zero

$$
T(0)=T(0+0)=T(0)+T(0)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad T(0)=0.
$$

**中文：** 仍然是在输出空间的加法群中相减。线性映射保持原点，不能平移原点。

**English:** Subtract in the additive group of the output space. A linear map preserves the origin; it cannot translate it.

### 性质 2：保持有限线性组合 / Property 2: preservation of finite linear combinations

$$
T\left(\sum_{i=1}^s k_iv_i\right)=\sum_{i=1}^s k_iT(v_i).
$$

**中文：** 因此知道一组基的像，就知道所有向量的像。后面构造矩阵只需要处理基向量，这一性质是原因。

**English:** Knowing the images of a basis determines the images of all vectors. This property is why matrix construction only requires basis vectors.

### 性质 3：相关性会保留，无关性未必 / Property 3: dependence is preserved; independence need not be

**中文：** 若 $\sum k_iv_i=0$ 且系数不全为零，应用 $T$ 后仍有 $\sum k_iT(v_i)=0$，使用同一组非平凡系数。因此相关向量的像一定相关。但无关向量的像可能相关，因为映射可能把不同方向压到同一方向，甚至压成零。

**English:** If $\sum k_iv_i=0$ with coefficients not all zero, applying $T$ gives $\sum k_iT(v_i)=0$ with the same nontrivial coefficients. Dependent vectors therefore have dependent images. Independent vectors may have dependent images because the map can collapse distinct directions together or send them to zero.

**教学补充反例 / Supplementary counterexample：**

$$
T\big((x_1,x_2)^T\big)=(x_1,0)^T,\qquad T(e_1)=e_1,\quad T(e_2)=0.
$$

**中文：** 输入 $e_1,e_2$ 无关，输出含零向量，因而相关。讲义也用零映射提示这一问题。

**English:** The inputs $e_1,e_2$ are independent, but the outputs are dependent because one is zero. The slides also use the zero map to illustrate this issue.

### 核与像都是子空间 / Kernel and image are subspaces

$$
\ker T=\{u\in U:T(u)=0\},\qquad
\operatorname{im}T=\{T(u):u\in U\}.
$$

**中文：** 核在输入空间里，记录被映成零的方向；像在输出空间里，记录能够实现的输出。核的封闭性由 $T(au+bv)=0$ 得到；像的封闭性由 $aT(u)+bT(v)=T(au+bv)$ 得到。

**English:** The kernel lies in the input space and records directions sent to zero. The image lies in the output space and records achievable outputs. Kernel closure follows from $T(au+bv)=0$; image closure follows from $aT(u)+bT(v)=T(au+bv)$.

**中文：** 当 $T(x)=Ax$ 时，核是 $Ax=0$ 的解空间，像是 $A$ 的列空间，因为 $Ax$ 是各列的线性组合。因此“抽象映射的核与像”对应熟悉的“矩阵零空间与列空间”。

**English:** For $T(x)=Ax$, the kernel is the solution space of $Ax=0$, and the image is the column space of $A$, since $Ax$ is a linear combination of its columns. Thus abstract kernel and image correspond to matrix null space and column space.

### 秩–零度公式与单射 / Rank–nullity and injectivity

**中文：** 教学补充：若输入空间 $U$ 有限维，则输入的维数等于像的维数加核的维数。映射的秩是像的维数，零度是核的维数。这里右边等于输入空间的维数，不是输出空间的维数。

**English:** Teaching supplement: for a finite-dimensional domain $U$, its dimension equals the dimension of the image plus the dimension of the kernel. The map’s rank is its image dimension; its nullity is its kernel dimension. Their sum equals the domain dimension, not the codomain dimension.

$$
\boxed{\dim U=\dim\operatorname{im}T+\dim\ker T},\qquad
\boxed{n=\operatorname{rank}A+\dim\mathcal N(A)}.
$$

**中文：** 证明思路是把核的一组基扩成输入空间的基。核基向量的像为零；新增向量的像生成整个像空间，而且无关。因此输入基中的两部分分别贡献零度和秩。对[线性子空间文件 1.4.1 节](04_线性子空间.md#section-1-4-1)的 $2\times3$ 矩阵，秩为 2、零度为 1，正好 $3=2+1$。

**English:** Extend a basis of the kernel to a basis of the domain. Kernel basis vectors map to zero; the images of the added vectors span the image and are independent. These two parts contribute nullity and rank. For the $2\times3$ matrix in [Section 1.4.1 of Linear Subspaces](04_线性子空间.md#section-1-4-1), the rank is two and the nullity is one, giving $3=2+1$.

**中文：** 映射单射，当且仅当核只有零向量。因为 $T(u)=T(v)$ 等价于 $T(u-v)=0$。单射也保证无关向量的像仍无关；否则对像的相关关系应用核为零的条件，就会得到原向量的相关关系。

**English:** A linear map is injective exactly when its kernel is trivial, because $T(u)=T(v)$ is equivalent to $T(u-v)=0$. An injective map also preserves independence: a dependence relation among images would, by the trivial-kernel condition, imply a dependence relation among the original vectors.

## 1.5.3 线性映射的矩阵表示 / Matrix representation of a linear mapping

> 对应 / Source: L2, pp. 26–29。

### 一列对应一个输入基向量 / One column for each input basis vector

**中文：** 设 $T:U\to V$，输入基 $B=(b_1,\ldots,b_n)$，输出基 $C=(c_1,\ldots,c_m)$。分别计算 $T(b_j)$，再把它写成输出基 $C$ 的线性组合；这些输出坐标就是矩阵第 $j$ 列。讲义用 $\mathcal L(U\to V)$ 表示所有这类线性映射的集合。

**English:** Let $T:U\to V$ with input basis $B=(b_1,\ldots,b_n)$ and output basis $C=(c_1,\ldots,c_m)$. Compute each $T(b_j)$ and express it in the output basis $C$. Those coordinates form column $j$ of the matrix. The slides denote the set of such linear mappings by $\mathcal L(U\to V)$.

$$
T(b_j)=\sum_{i=1}^m a_{ij}c_i,\qquad
[T]_{C\leftarrow B}
=A=\big[[T(b_1)]_C\ \cdots\ [T(b_n)]_C\big]\in\mathbb F^{m\times n}.
$$

**中文：** 箭头 $C\leftarrow B$ 表示“输入使用 $B$ 坐标，输出使用 $C$ 坐标”。行数等于输出空间维数，列数等于输入空间维数。不是把输入基向量本身放进矩阵，也不是直接把像的标准坐标放进去；必须使用指定输出基的坐标。

**English:** The arrow $C\leftarrow B$ means that input coordinates use $B$ and output coordinates use $C$. Rows count output dimensions and columns count input dimensions. The columns are not the input basis vectors themselves, and the standard coordinates of the images are valid only when the specified output basis is standard.

### 为什么得到 $y=Ax$？ / Why does this give $y=Ax$?

**中文：** 写 $u=\sum_{j=1}^n x_jb_j$。线性性允许把 $T$ 分配到这组线性组合上，再将每个 $T(b_j)$ 展开到输出基，得到以下推导。

**English:** Write $u=\sum_{j=1}^n x_jb_j$. Linearity distributes $T$ over this combination. Expanding each $T(b_j)$ in the output basis gives the following derivation.

$$
\begin{aligned}
T(u)
&=\sum_{j=1}^n x_jT(b_j)\\
&=\sum_{j=1}^n x_j\sum_{i=1}^m a_{ij}c_i\\
&=\sum_{i=1}^m\left(\sum_{j=1}^n a_{ij}x_j\right)c_i.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
y_i=\sum_{j=1}^n a_{ij}x_j,\qquad
\boxed{[T(u)]_C=A[u]_B},\qquad\boxed{y=Ax}.
$$

**中文：** 这是第二讲最后两页的关键结论，第 29 页再次呈现同一推导。映射作用于抽象向量；矩阵作用于该向量的坐标。两者通过选定的基联系起来。

**English:** This is the key conclusion on the last two slides; slide 29 repeats the derivation. The map acts on abstract vectors, while the matrix acts on their coordinates. The chosen bases connect the two descriptions.

### 完整例题 A：输入、输出都使用非标准基 / Worked example A: nonstandard bases on both sides

**教学补充 / Teaching supplement：**

$$
T:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R^2,\qquad
T\begin{pmatrix}u_1\\u_2\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}u_1+2u_2\\3u_1+4u_2\end{pmatrix}.
$$

$$
B=\left(\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix},\begin{bmatrix}1\\-1\end{bmatrix}\right),\qquad
C=\left(\begin{bmatrix}1\\0\end{bmatrix},\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix}\right).
$$

**中文：** 第一步，求输入基的像：$T(b_1)=(3,7)^T$，$T(b_2)=(-1,-1)^T$。第二步，用输出基表示这些像。因为 $r c_1+s c_2=(r+s,s)^T$，向量 $(a,b)^T$ 在 $C$ 下的坐标是 $(a-b,b)^T$。

**English:** First compute the input basis images: $T(b_1)=(3,7)^T$ and $T(b_2)=(-1,-1)^T$. Then express them in the output basis. Since $r c_1+s c_2=(r+s,s)^T$, the $C$-coordinates of $(a,b)^T$ are $(a-b,b)^T$.

$$
[T(b_1)]_C=\begin{bmatrix}-4\\7\end{bmatrix},\qquad
[T(b_2)]_C=\begin{bmatrix}0\\-1\end{bmatrix},\qquad
A=\begin{bmatrix}-4&0\\7&-1\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** 第三步，测试 $u=(4,2)^T$。根据第一讲换基例子，$[u]_B=(3,1)^T$，所以输出的 $C$ 坐标是 $A(3,1)^T=(-12,20)^T$。最后还原成标准坐标并与直接计算比较。

**English:** Third, test $u=(4,2)^T$. The change-of-basis example in Lecture 1 gives $[u]_B=(3,1)^T$, so its output coordinates are $A(3,1)^T=(-12,20)^T$. Reconstruct the standard-coordinate output and compare it with direct calculation.

$$
T(u)=-12c_1+20c_2=\begin{bmatrix}8\\20\end{bmatrix},\qquad
T\begin{pmatrix}4\\2\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}4+2\cdot2\\3\cdot4+4\cdot2\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}8\\20\end{pmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** 两种计算一致。映射在标准基下的矩阵是 $\begin{bmatrix}1&2\\3&4\end{bmatrix}$，在这里指定的两组基下却是 $\begin{bmatrix}-4&0\\7&-1\end{bmatrix}$。映射相同，坐标描述不同。

**English:** The two computations agree. The standard-basis matrix is $\begin{bmatrix}1&2\\3&4\end{bmatrix}$, whereas the matrix in the specified bases is $\begin{bmatrix}-4&0\\7&-1\end{bmatrix}$. The mapping is the same; its coordinate description differs.

### 完整例题 B：多项式求导的矩阵 / Worked example B: a matrix for differentiation

**教学补充 / Teaching supplement：**

$$
D:\mathbb R_2[\lambda]\to\mathbb R_1[\lambda],\qquad D(p)=p',
\quad B=(1,\lambda,\lambda^2),\quad C=(1,\lambda).
$$

$$
D(1)=0,\quad D(\lambda)=1,\quad D(\lambda^2)=2\lambda
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
[D]_{C\leftarrow B}=\begin{bmatrix}0&1&0\\0&0&2\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** 输入空间维数是 3，输出空间维数是 2，因此矩阵是 $2\times3$。对 $p=2+3\lambda+\lambda^2$，输入坐标为 $(2,3,1)^T$，矩阵给出输出坐标 $(3,2)^T$，对应 $p'=3+2\lambda$。

**English:** The input dimension is three and the output dimension is two, so the matrix is $2\times3$. For $p=2+3\lambda+\lambda^2$, the input coordinates are $(2,3,1)^T$, and the output coordinates are $(3,2)^T$, representing $p'=3+2\lambda$.

$$
\begin{bmatrix}0&1&0\\0&0&2\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}2\\3\\1\end{bmatrix}
=\begin{bmatrix}3\\2\end{bmatrix},\qquad
\ker D=\operatorname{span}\{1\},\qquad
\operatorname{im}D=\mathbb R_1[\lambda].
$$

**中文：** 核由常数多项式组成，维数为 1；像维数为 2，满足 $3=1+2$。这个例子说明抽象对象之间的映射，也可以用熟悉的矩阵乘法计算。

**English:** The kernel consists of constant polynomials and has dimension one. The image has dimension two, so $3=1+2$. This example shows how mappings between abstract objects can be calculated using ordinary matrix multiplication.

## 1.5.4 映射矩阵换基：与向量坐标换基连接 / Changing bases for a mapping matrix

> 教学补充：由 L1, pp. 43–44 与 L2, pp. 26–29 的公式推导 / Teaching supplement derived from the two lectures’ coordinate formulas。

### 一般输入、输出基都改变 / Change input and output bases independently

**中文：** 已知旧基下 $y=Ax$，把输入基从 $B$ 改为 $B'=BP$，输出基从 $C$ 改为 $C'=CQ$。由第一讲，旧输入坐标 $x=Px'$，旧输出坐标 $y=Qy'$。代入并整理即可得到新矩阵。

**English:** Suppose $y=Ax$ in the old bases. Change the input basis from $B$ to $B'=BP$ and the output basis from $C$ to $C'=CQ$. Lecture 1 gives $x=Px'$ and $y=Qy'$. Substitute and rearrange to obtain the new matrix.

$$
Qy'=APx'\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
y'=Q^{-1}APx',\qquad
\boxed{A'=Q^{-1}AP}.
$$

**中文：** 右侧 $P$ 将新输入坐标转为旧输入坐标；$A$ 执行旧坐标中的映射；左侧 $Q^{-1}$ 再把旧输出坐标转为新输出坐标。按这个操作次序理解，就不用孤立背公式。

**English:** Reading from right to left, $P$ converts new input coordinates to old input coordinates; $A$ applies the mapping in old coordinates; and $Q^{-1}$ converts old output coordinates to new output coordinates. This order explains the formula.

### 同一空间的变换：相似矩阵 / A transformation on one space: similar matrices

**中文：** 若 $T:V\to V$，输入输出都从同一旧基换到同一新基，则 $Q=P$，得到相似变换。这是之后学习典范形式的基础，此处只把它看作坐标变换的直接结果。

**English:** For $T:V\to V$, if input and output use the same basis change, then $Q=P$ and the result is a similarity transformation. This underlies later canonical forms; here it is simply a consequence of coordinate changes.

$$
\boxed{A'=P^{-1}AP}.
$$

**教学补充例题 / Supplementary example：**

$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\0&2\end{bmatrix},\quad
P=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{bmatrix}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
A'=\begin{bmatrix}3/2&-1/2\\-1/2&3/2\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** 直接求新基向量的像也能检验：$(1,1)^T$ 的像是 $(1,2)^T$，新坐标为 $(3/2,-1/2)^T$；$(1,-1)^T$ 的像是 $(1,-2)^T$，新坐标为 $(-1/2,3/2)^T$，恰好是 $A'$ 的两列。

**English:** Check by mapping the new basis vectors directly. The image of $(1,1)^T$ is $(1,2)^T$, whose new coordinates are $(3/2,-1/2)^T$. The image of $(1,-1)^T$ is $(1,-2)^T$, whose new coordinates are $(-1/2,3/2)^T$. These are exactly the columns of $A'$.
