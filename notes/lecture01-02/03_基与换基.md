## 1.3.1 基、维数与坐标 / Bases, dimension, and coordinates

> 对应 / Source: L1, pp. 38–42；Definition 1.15；L2, pp. 3–7。

### 基需要同时满足两件事 / A basis has two requirements

**中文：** 有序向量组 $B=(b_1,\ldots,b_n)$ 是 $V$ 的基，当且仅当它线性无关，并且能表示 $V$ 中每个向量。前一个条件排除冗余，后一个条件保证没有遗漏方向。

**English:** An ordered list $B=(b_1,\ldots,b_n)$ is a basis of $V$ exactly when it is linearly independent and spans every vector in $V$. Independence excludes redundancy; spanning excludes missing directions.

$$
\boxed{\text{基 / Basis}=\text{线性无关 / Independent}+\text{生成整个空间 / Spans the space}}
$$

**中文：** 仅有无关性不够，例如 $(e_1)$ 不能成为 $\mathbb R^2$ 的基。仅能生成也不够，例如 $(e_1,e_2,e_1+e_2)$ 虽然生成 $\mathbb R^2$，但有冗余，不能成为基。基的顺序影响坐标。

**English:** Independence alone is insufficient: $(e_1)$ is not a basis of $\mathbb R^2$. Spanning alone is insufficient: $(e_1,e_2,e_1+e_2)$ spans $\mathbb R^2$ but is redundant. The order of basis vectors affects coordinates.

### 为什么基也叫极大无关组？ / Why is a basis a maximal independent set?

**中文：** “极大”表示不能再加入空间中的其他向量而保持无关。如果某个 $v$ 不在已有向量的生成空间中，加入它仍会无关。因此一个极大无关组必须生成整个空间，成为基。这里讨论的是整个 $V$ 中的极大无关组；某个有限向量列表的极大无关组，只保证是该列表生成空间的基。

**English:** “Maximal” means no additional vector from the space can be added while retaining independence. If a vector $v$ were outside the current span, adding it would preserve independence. Thus a maximal independent set in $V$ must span $V$ and is a basis. A maximal independent subset of a particular finite list is only guaranteed to be a basis of that list’s span.

### 维数与坐标的唯一性 / Dimension and unique coordinates

**中文：** 有限维空间的任意两组基具有相同的向量数，这个数称为维数 $\dim V$。每个向量关于固定基都有唯一坐标：存在性来自生成性，唯一性来自无关性。讲义还写 $\operatorname{rank}V=n$；本文主要使用更常见的 $\dim V=n$ 表示空间维数。

**English:** Any two bases of a finite-dimensional space contain the same number of vectors; this number is its dimension $\dim V$. Coordinates in a fixed basis exist because it spans and are unique because it is independent. The slides also write $\operatorname{rank}V=n$; these notes mainly use the more common notation $\dim V=n$ for space dimension.

$$
v=x_1b_1+\cdots+x_nb_n,
\qquad [v]_B=\begin{bmatrix}x_1\\\vdots\\x_n\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** $v$ 是对象本身，$[v]_B$ 是对象在某组基下的数字描述。换一组基，$v$ 没变，坐标通常会变。一个多项式的坐标列，也要先说明使用哪组多项式基。

**English:** The vector $v$ is the object; $[v]_B$ is its numerical description in a particular basis. Changing the basis does not change $v$, but usually changes its coordinates. A polynomial coordinate column is meaningful only after specifying a polynomial basis.

### 讲义例子：多项式的标准基 / Lecture example: the monomial basis

$$
B=(1,\lambda,\lambda^2,\ldots,\lambda^{n-1})
\quad\text{in }\mathbb F_{n-1}[\lambda].
$$

**中文：** 任意多项式 $p=a_0+\cdots+a_{n-1}\lambda^{n-1}$ 都是这组向量的线性组合，因此生成性成立。若 $k_0+k_1\lambda+\cdots+k_{n-1}\lambda^{n-1}=0$，比较各项系数得到所有 $k_i=0$，因此无关性成立。基包含 $n$ 个向量，所以维数为 $n$。

**English:** Every polynomial $p=a_0+\cdots+a_{n-1}\lambda^{n-1}$ is a linear combination of these vectors, proving spanning. If $k_0+k_1\lambda+\cdots+k_{n-1}\lambda^{n-1}=0$, comparison of coefficients gives $k_i=0$ for every $i$, proving independence. There are $n$ basis vectors, so the dimension is $n$.

$$
\dim\mathbb F_{n-1}[\lambda]=n,\qquad
[p]_B=(a_0,a_1,\ldots,a_{n-1})^T.
$$

**中文：** 注意下标是 $n-1$，维数却是 $n$，因为常数项也占一个独立方向。

**English:** The degree bound is $n-1$, but the dimension is $n$, because the constant term contributes an independent direction.

### 讲义例子：平移后的多项式基 / Lecture example: a shifted polynomial basis

$$
C=(1,\lambda-a,(\lambda-a)^2,\ldots,(\lambda-a)^{n-1}).
$$

**中文：** 对实或复多项式，关于点 $a$ 的有限 Taylor 展开给出这组基下的坐标。因为 $p$ 的次数至多为 $n-1$，展开式到 $n-1$ 阶就结束，没有余项。这个导数公式在本课程的 $\mathbb R$ 或 $\mathbb C$ 上使用。

**English:** For real or complex polynomials, the finite Taylor expansion around $a$ gives coordinates in this shifted basis. Since $p$ has degree at most $n-1$, the expansion ends at order $n-1$ with no remainder. This derivative formula is used over $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$ in this course.

$$
p(\lambda)=\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}\frac{p^{(j)}(a)}{j!}(\lambda-a)^j,
\qquad
[p]_C=\begin{bmatrix}
p(a)\\p'(a)\\p''(a)/2!\\\vdots\\p^{(n-1)}(a)/(n-1)!
\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**教学补充例题 / Supplementary worked example：**

$$
p(\lambda)=2+3\lambda+\lambda^2,\quad
B=(1,\lambda,\lambda^2),\quad C=(1,\lambda-1,(\lambda-1)^2).
$$

**中文：** 标准基下坐标为 $(2,3,1)^T$。在点 1，$p(1)=6$、$p'(1)=5$、$p''(1)/2=1$，所以新坐标为 $(6,5,1)^T$。展开检验如下；两个坐标列描述同一个多项式。

**English:** The monomial coordinates are $(2,3,1)^T$. At 1, $p(1)=6$, $p'(1)=5$, and $p''(1)/2=1$, giving shifted coordinates $(6,5,1)^T$. Expanding verifies that both coordinate columns describe the same polynomial.

$$
6+5(\lambda-1)+(\lambda-1)^2
=6+5\lambda-5+\lambda^2-2\lambda+1
=2+3\lambda+\lambda^2.
$$

## 1.3.2 过渡矩阵：基怎样变，坐标怎样变？ / Transition matrices: bases versus coordinates

> 对应 / Source: L1, pp. 43–44；Theorem 1.4；L2, pp. 8–9。

### 先固定等式，再谈方向 / Fix the defining equation first

**中文：** 令旧基 $B=(b_1,\ldots,b_n)$，新基 $C=(c_1,\ldots,c_n)$。将每个新基向量用旧基表示，把坐标作为矩阵的列，得到讲义的过渡矩阵 $P$。最可靠的记忆方式是记住定义等式 $C=BP$。

**English:** Let $B=(b_1,\ldots,b_n)$ be the old basis and $C=(c_1,\ldots,c_n)$ the new basis. Express each new basis vector in the old basis and place its coordinates in a column to obtain the slides’ transition matrix $P$. The safest convention is its defining equation, $C=BP$.

$$
P=\big[[c_1]_B\ \cdots\ [c_n]_B\big],\qquad \boxed{C=BP}.
$$

**中文：** 讲义称 $P$ 为“从基 $B$ 到基 $C$ 的过渡矩阵”，但它乘坐标时却把 $C$ 坐标转为 $B$ 坐标。不同教材的方向命名可能不同，所以做题时写等式比只写“从旧到新”更准确。

**English:** The slides call $P$ the transition matrix “from basis $B$ to basis $C$,” but when acting on coordinates it converts $C$-coordinates into $B$-coordinates. Direction labels vary across texts; the defining equation is more reliable than the phrase “old to new.”

### 从同一个向量推导公式 / Derive the formula from the same vector

$$
x=[v]_B,\quad y=[v]_C,
\qquad v=Bx=Cy=BPy.
$$

**中文：** 基 $B$ 下坐标唯一，因此 $x=Py$。由于两边都是基，$P$ 可逆，进而 $y=P^{-1}x$。基矩阵乘 $P$，从旧基得到新基；旧坐标乘 $P^{-1}$，得到新坐标。

**English:** Uniqueness of coordinates in $B$ gives $x=Py$. Since both lists are bases, $P$ is invertible, so $y=P^{-1}x$. Multiplying the old basis by $P$ gives the new basis; multiplying the old coordinates by $P^{-1}$ gives the new coordinates.

$$
\boxed{[v]_B=P[v]_C},\qquad
\boxed{[v]_C=P^{-1}[v]_B}.
$$

### 为什么 $P$ 可逆？ / Why is $P$ invertible?

**中文：** 若 $Pz=0$，则 $Cz=BPz=0$。因为 $C$ 是基，只有 $z=0$。因此方阵 $P$ 的零空间只有零向量，故可逆。这就是讲义 Theorem 1.4 的核心。

**English:** If $Pz=0$, then $Cz=BPz=0$. Since $C$ is a basis, this forces $z=0$. Thus the square matrix $P$ has a trivial null space and is invertible. This is the core of Theorem 1.4.

### 数值例题 / Numerical worked example

**教学补充 / Teaching supplement：**

$$
B=(e_1,e_2),\quad
C=\left(\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix},\begin{bmatrix}1\\-1\end{bmatrix}\right),\quad
P=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{bmatrix},\quad
P^{-1}=\frac12\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{bmatrix}.
$$

$$
v=\begin{bmatrix}4\\2\end{bmatrix},\quad
[v]_B=\begin{bmatrix}4\\2\end{bmatrix},\quad
[v]_C=P^{-1}[v]_B=\begin{bmatrix}3\\1\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** 检验：$3(1,1)^T+1(1,-1)^T=(4,2)^T$。新坐标 $(3,1)^T$ 表示沿新基方向的系数，不是把原向量“改成了另一个向量”。

**English:** Check: $3(1,1)^T+1(1,-1)^T=(4,2)^T$. The new coordinates $(3,1)^T$ are coefficients along the new basis directions; the underlying vector has not changed.

### 多项式换基例题 / Polynomial change-of-basis example

**中文：** 沿用 1.3.1 节的 $B=(1,\lambda,\lambda^2)$ 和 $C=(1,\lambda-1,(\lambda-1)^2)$。将三个新基向量展开，得到 $P$ 的三列。请特别注意：不是把三个坐标横着放成三行。

**English:** Use $B=(1,\lambda,\lambda^2)$ and $C=(1,\lambda-1,(\lambda-1)^2)$ from Section 1.3.1. Expanding the three new basis vectors gives the three columns of $P$. Place their coordinates in columns, not rows.

$$
[1]_B=\begin{bmatrix}1\\0\\0\end{bmatrix},\quad
[\lambda-1]_B=\begin{bmatrix}-1\\1\\0\end{bmatrix},\quad
[(\lambda-1)^2]_B=\begin{bmatrix}1\\-2\\1\end{bmatrix}.
$$

$$
P=\begin{bmatrix}1&-1&1\\0&1&-2\\0&0&1\end{bmatrix},\qquad
P\begin{bmatrix}6\\5\\1\end{bmatrix}
=\begin{bmatrix}2\\3\\1\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** 这与 Taylor 展开的坐标完全一致。做题时可用“重新组成原向量”检查方向是否写反。

**English:** This agrees with the Taylor-expansion coordinates. Reconstructing the original vector is a useful check against reversing the conversion direction.
