## 1.1.1 二元运算与代数系统 / Binary operations and algebraic systems

> 对应 / Source: L1, pp. 10–13；Definitions 1.1–1.3。

### 定义与封闭性 / Definition and closure

**中文：** 二元运算把两个输入唯一地对应到一个输出。讲义先使用一般形式 $U\times V\to W$；研究群、环时，我们主要使用同一集合上的运算 $S\times S\to S$。输出始终留在 $S$ 中，称为对该运算封闭。

**English:** A binary operation assigns a unique output to each pair of inputs. The slides first use the general form $U\times V\to W$; for groups and rings, we mainly use operations $S\times S\to S$. The operation is closed on $S$ when its output always remains in $S$.

$$
\star:U\times V\to W,\qquad (u,v)\mapsto u\star v.
$$

**中文：** 矩阵乘法是讲义的例子：$n\times m$ 矩阵乘 $m\times p$ 矩阵，得到 $n\times p$ 矩阵。必须检查相邻维数；乘法次序不可随意交换。

**English:** Matrix multiplication is the example in the slides: an $n\times m$ matrix multiplied by an $m\times p$ matrix produces an $n\times p$ matrix. The inner dimensions must agree, and the order of multiplication matters.

$$
\mathbb C^{n\times m}\times\mathbb C^{m\times p}
\longrightarrow\mathbb C^{n\times p},\qquad (A,B)\mapsto AB.
$$

**中文：** 若 $A$ 为 $2\times3$，$B$ 为 $3\times4$，则 $AB$ 有定义，但 $BA$ 没有定义。即使都是方阵、两个方向都有定义，也不保证 $AB=BA$。

**English:** If $A$ is $2\times3$ and $B$ is $3\times4$, then $AB$ is defined but $BA$ is not. Even for square matrices, where both products exist, $AB=BA$ need not hold.

### 交换律与结合律 / Commutativity and associativity

$$
\underbrace{a\star b=b\star a}_{\text{交换律 / Commutativity}},\qquad
\underbrace{a\star(b\star c)=(a\star b)\star c}_{\text{结合律 / Associativity}}.
$$

**中文：** 交换律改变顺序；结合律只改变括号。矩阵乘法满足结合律，但一般不满足交换律。代数系统就是“集合、规定好的运算及其满足的性质”这一整体；不能只看集合而忽略运算。

**English:** Commutativity changes the order; associativity changes only the grouping. Matrix multiplication is associative but generally not commutative. An algebraic system consists of a set, specified operations, and the properties they satisfy; the set alone is insufficient.

**教学补充例题 / Supplementary example:**

$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{bmatrix},\quad
B=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\1&1\end{bmatrix},\quad
AB=\begin{bmatrix}2&1\\1&1\end{bmatrix},\quad
BA=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\1&2\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** 这直接给出了 $AB\ne BA$ 的反例。否定“所有对象都满足某性质”，只需要一个有效反例。

**English:** This directly gives a counterexample to $AB=BA$. A single valid counterexample disproves a claim that a property holds for all objects.

## 1.1.2 群：一种能够撤销的运算 / Groups: an operation that can be undone

> 对应 / Source: L1, pp. 14–15；Definition 1.4, Theorem 1.1。

### 定义 / Definition

**中文：** 群 $(G,\star)$ 由一个非空集合和一个封闭的运算组成，还满足结合律、存在双侧单位元、每个元素存在双侧逆元。交换律不是一般群的必要条件；满足交换律的群称为交换群或 Abelian group。

**English:** A group $(G,\star)$ consists of a nonempty set and a closed operation satisfying associativity, the existence of a two-sided identity, and the existence of a two-sided inverse for every element. Commutativity is not required for a general group; a commutative group is called an Abelian group.

| 条件 / Condition | 数学表达 / Formula | 直观含义 / Intuition |
|---|---|---|
| 封闭 / Closure | $a\star b\in G$ | 运算不离开集合 / Stay inside the set |
| 结合 / Associativity | $(a\star b)\star c=a\star(b\star c)$ | 可以重新加括号 / Regroup operations |
| 单位元 / Identity | $e\star a=a\star e=a$ | 什么也不改变 / Change nothing |
| 逆元 / Inverse | $a^{-1}\star a=a\star a^{-1}=e$ | 撤销一个元素的作用 / Undo an element |

**中文：** 对加法群，单位元通常写成 $0$，逆元写成 $-a$；对乘法群，单位元通常写成 $1$ 或 $I$，逆元写成 $a^{-1}$。符号随运算改变。这里的“单位元”不表示它的长度是 1。

**English:** In an additive group, the identity is usually written $0$ and the inverse of $a$ is $-a$. In a multiplicative group, the identity is usually $1$ or $I$, and the inverse is $a^{-1}$. The notation depends on the operation. An identity element is not an element whose length is one.

### 判断例子 / Examples to classify

| 集合与运算 / Set and operation | 是否为群 / Group? | 原因 / Reason |
|---|---|---|
| $(\mathbb Z,+)$ | 是，交换群 / Yes, Abelian | $0$ 是单位元，$-a\in\mathbb Z$ / Identity and negatives exist |
| $(\mathbb R,+)$ | 是，交换群 / Yes, Abelian | 同上 / Same reasoning |
| $(\mathbb R,\times)$ | 否 / No | $0$ 无乘法逆元 / Zero has no multiplicative inverse |
| $(\mathbb R\setminus\{0\},\times)$ | 是，交换群 / Yes, Abelian | 非零实数有倒数 / Nonzero real numbers have reciprocals |
| 所有 $n\times n$ 实矩阵，乘法 / All square real matrices, multiplication | 否 / No | 奇异矩阵不可逆 / Singular matrices are not invertible |
| $GL(n,\mathbb R)$，乘法 / Invertible real matrices, multiplication | 是 / Yes | 乘积和逆矩阵仍可逆 / Products and inverses remain invertible |

### 单位元和逆元为什么唯一？ / Why are identity and inverse unique?

**中文：** 若 $e$ 和 $f$ 都是单位元，则利用各自的单位元性质，有 $e=e\star f=f$。若 $b,c$ 都是 $a$ 的逆元，利用结合律可以把同一个乘积分别化简成 $b$ 与 $c$。

**English:** If $e$ and $f$ are both identities, their identity properties give $e=e\star f=f$. If $b$ and $c$ are both inverses of $a$, associativity allows the same product to simplify to both $b$ and $c$.

$$
b=b\star e=b\star(a\star c)=(b\star a)\star c=e\star c=c.
$$

**中文：** 这是常用的唯一性证明方法：先假设有两个满足条件的对象，再证明它们相等。证明逆元唯一不需要交换律。

**English:** This is a standard uniqueness proof: assume two objects satisfy the required conditions, then show that they are equal. Uniqueness of inverses does not require commutativity.

**自测 / Self-check：** 非负整数 $\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$ 在普通加法下是群吗？/ Do the nonnegative integers form a group under ordinary addition?

**答案 / Answer：** 不是：$1$ 的加法逆元 $-1$ 不在集合中。/ No: the additive inverse of $1$, namely $-1$, is outside the set.

## 1.1.3 多项式加法群 / The additive group of polynomials

> 对应 / Source: L1, pp. 16–23；Definitions 1.5–1.7 及多项式加法。

### 多项式、相等与次数 / Polynomials, equality, and degree

**中文：** 域 $\mathbb F$ 上的形式多项式是有限个系数与 $\lambda$ 的幂组成的表达式。$\lambda$ 是不定元；判断两个多项式相等，要比较每个幂次的系数，而不是只检查某一个点的数值。

**English:** A formal polynomial over $\mathbb F$ is an expression with finitely many coefficients and powers of the indeterminate $\lambda$. Equality of polynomials means equality of the coefficient of every power, not merely equality of their values at one point.

$$
p(\lambda)=a_0+a_1\lambda+\cdots+a_n\lambda^n,\qquad a_i\in\mathbb F.
$$

**中文：** 当 $a_n\ne0$ 时，$\deg p=n$，$a_n\lambda^n$ 是首项，$a_n$ 是首项系数；首项系数为 1 时称为首一多项式。零多项式的所有系数都为零，不能按照“最高非零系数”定义次数。不同教材可能不定义它的次数，或约定为 $-\infty$；本文在次数有界的空间中始终显式包含零多项式。

**English:** If $a_n\ne0$, then $\deg p=n$, the leading term is $a_n\lambda^n$, and the leading coefficient is $a_n$. A polynomial with leading coefficient one is monic. The zero polynomial has no highest nonzero coefficient, so this degree definition does not apply. Texts may leave its degree undefined or set it to $-\infty$; all degree-bounded spaces here explicitly include the zero polynomial.

**中文：** 补写高次项的零系数不会改变多项式，例如 $1+\lambda=1+\lambda+0\lambda^2$。因此比较系数时，应先补齐缺项；不能把写出来的最高指数直接当作次数。

**English:** Adding zero coefficients for higher powers does not change a polynomial: $1+\lambda=1+\lambda+0\lambda^2$. Fill in missing coefficients before comparing them; the highest exponent written down is not necessarily the degree.

### 按系数相加 / Add coefficient by coefficient

$$
p(\lambda)=\sum_{i=0}^{N}a_i\lambda^i,\quad
q(\lambda)=\sum_{i=0}^{N}b_i\lambda^i
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
(p+q)(\lambda)=\sum_{i=0}^{N}(a_i+b_i)\lambda^i.
$$

**中文：** 不同次数的多项式先补零。例如 $p=1+2\lambda+\lambda^2$，$q=3-\lambda$，则 $p+q=4+\lambda+\lambda^2$。系数加法的封闭性、结合律、交换律传递到多项式加法；零多项式是单位元，逐项取负得到加法逆元。因此 $(\mathbb F[\lambda],+)$ 是交换群。

**English:** Pad lower-degree polynomials with zero coefficients. For example, if $p=1+2\lambda+\lambda^2$ and $q=3-\lambda$, then $p+q=4+\lambda+\lambda^2$. Closure, associativity, and commutativity of coefficient addition carry over to polynomial addition. The zero polynomial is the identity, and negating each coefficient gives the additive inverse. Thus $(\mathbb F[\lambda],+)$ is an Abelian group.

$$
-p(\lambda)=-a_0-a_1\lambda-\cdots-a_n\lambda^n,
\qquad p+(-p)=0.
$$

### 有限群的一个练习模型 / A practice model of a finite group

**中文：** 第一讲第 23 页提到四级移位寄存器，并让学生阅读指定教材第 3–4 页，但没有给出完整运算。以下是教学补充的“四位状态按位异或群”，用于练习群公理；它不声称复原教材中的寄存器运算。

**English:** Lecture 1, page 23, mentions a four-stage shift register and refers students to pages 3–4 of the assigned textbook, without specifying the full operation. The following bitwise-XOR group on four-bit states is a teaching supplement for practicing the group axioms; it does not reconstruct the textbook’s register operation.

$$
G=\{0,1\}^4,\qquad
(a_1,a_2,a_3,a_4)\oplus(b_1,b_2,b_3,b_4)
=(a_1\oplus b_1,a_2\oplus b_2,a_3\oplus b_3,a_4\oplus b_4).
$$

**中文：** 这里 $1\oplus1=0$，$0\oplus1=1$。运算封闭、结合且交换；单位元是 $(0,0,0,0)$，每个状态都是自己的逆元，因为 $a\oplus a=0$。群共有 $2^4=16$ 个元素。

**English:** Here $1\oplus1=0$ and $0\oplus1=1$. The operation is closed, associative, and commutative. The identity is $(0,0,0,0)$, and every state is its own inverse because $a\oplus a=0$. The group has $2^4=16$ elements.

## 1.1.4 环与域：给标量建立规则 / Rings and fields: rules for scalars

> 对应 / Source: L1, pp. 24–28；Definition 1.8, Theorem 1.2, Definition 1.10。

### 环 / Ring

**中文：** 环 $R$ 有加法和乘法。按通常的环定义，加法构成交换群，乘法满足结合律，并对加法满足左右分配律。讲义先定义环，再单独讨论乘法单位元，因此此处不把“必有乘法单位元”塞进讲义最初的环定义。

**English:** A ring $R$ has addition and multiplication. Under the usual ring convention, addition forms an Abelian group, multiplication is associative, and multiplication distributes over addition on both sides. The slides introduce the multiplicative identity separately, so we do not add its existence to their initial ring definition.

$$
a(b+c)=ab+ac,\qquad (a+b)c=ac+bc.
$$

**中文：** 若乘法还满足交换律，称为交换环。多项式的乘法通过展开并合并同类项定义；其系数满足卷积公式。$\mathbb F[\lambda]$ 是有单位元 1 的交换环。

**English:** A ring whose multiplication is commutative is a commutative ring. Polynomial multiplication is defined by expansion and collection of like powers; its coefficients satisfy a convolution formula. The set $\mathbb F[\lambda]$ is a commutative ring with identity 1.

$$
\left(\sum_i a_i\lambda^i\right)\left(\sum_j b_j\lambda^j\right)
=\sum_k\left(\sum_{i+j=k}a_i b_j\right)\lambda^k.
$$

$$
(1+\lambda)(1-\lambda)=1-\lambda^2.
$$

### 零因子、整环、单位元与逆元 / Zero divisors, integral domains, identity, and inverses

**中文：** 在交换环中，若非零元素 $a,b$ 满足 $ab=0$，它们就是零因子。没有零因子的非零交换环称为整环；现代常用定义还要求单位元 $1\ne0$。讲义也讨论非交换环中的左、右零因子；此时必须保留乘法次序。

**English:** In a commutative ring, nonzero elements $a,b$ with $ab=0$ are zero divisors. A nonzero commutative ring with no zero divisors is an integral domain; the usual modern definition also requires an identity $1\ne0$. The slides also discuss left and right zero divisors in noncommutative rings, where multiplication order must be retained.

**教学补充例子 / Supplementary example：** 非零矩阵也可能乘出零矩阵。/ Nonzero matrices can have a zero product.

$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{bmatrix},\qquad
B=\begin{bmatrix}0&0\\0&1\end{bmatrix},\qquad AB=BA=0.
$$

**中文：** 乘法单位元 $1_R$ 满足 $1_Ra=a1_R=a$。若 $ab=ba=1_R$，则 $b$ 是 $a$ 的乘法逆元。只要双侧单位元、双侧逆元存在，它们各自唯一；证明沿用 1.1.2 节的结合律论证，但环中并非每个元素都有乘法逆元。

**English:** A multiplicative identity $1_R$ satisfies $1_Ra=a1_R=a$. If $ab=ba=1_R$, then $b$ is the multiplicative inverse of $a$. Whenever a two-sided identity or inverse exists, it is unique by the associativity argument in Section 1.1.2. However, not every ring element has a multiplicative inverse.

### 域 / Field

**中文：** 域是有单位元 $1\ne0$ 的交换环，而且每个非零元素都有乘法逆元。它提供了标量加、减、乘以及除以非零数的规则。$\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ 都是域；$\mathbb Z$ 不是域，因为 $2$ 的逆元 $1/2$ 不在 $\mathbb Z$ 中。

**English:** A field is a commutative ring with identity $1\ne0$ in which every nonzero element has a multiplicative inverse. It supplies the rules for scalar addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division by nonzero scalars. The sets $\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ are fields; $\mathbb Z$ is not, because the inverse of $2$, namely $1/2$, is not an integer.

**中文：** $\mathbb F[\lambda]$ 也不是域：多项式 $\lambda$ 的逆元 $1/\lambda$ 不是多项式。但允许多项式之比，就得到有理函数域 $\mathbb F(\lambda)$。分母要求是非零多项式；这里讨论形式有理函数，不要求它在每一个实数点都能取值。

**English:** The polynomial ring $\mathbb F[\lambda]$ is not a field: the inverse of $\lambda$, namely $1/\lambda$, is not a polynomial. Allowing ratios of polynomials gives the rational-function field $\mathbb F(\lambda)$. The denominator must be a nonzero polynomial; formal rational functions need not be defined at every real evaluation point.

| 系统 / System | 需要哪些运算与规则 / Operations and rules |
|---|---|
| 群 / Group | 一个封闭、结合、有单位元和逆元的运算 / One closed, associative operation with identity and inverses |
| 环 / Ring | 加法交换群，乘法结合，左右分配 / Abelian additive group, associative multiplication, two distributive laws |
| 域 / Field | 交换环，$1\ne0$，每个非零元素有乘法逆元 / Commutative ring, nonzero identity, multiplicative inverses for nonzero elements |

**中文：** 这一节的目的，是为“在线性空间中用哪些数乘向量”建立基础。向量本身不需要能相乘或相除；标量来自域。

**English:** This section establishes which scalars may multiply vectors in a linear space. Vectors themselves need not support multiplication or division; the scalars come from a field.
