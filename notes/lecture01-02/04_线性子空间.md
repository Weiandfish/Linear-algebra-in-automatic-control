## 1.4.1 子空间：大空间中的小线性空间 / Subspaces: smaller linear spaces inside a larger one

> 对应 / Source: L2, pp. 10–11；Definition 1.16。

### 最实用的判定 / The practical subspace test

**中文：** $W\subseteq V$ 使用 $V$ 原有的加法和数乘。如果 $W$ 非空，并对加法与数乘封闭，它就是 $V$ 的子空间。其他公理由母空间继承，不必重新验证全部八条。讲义第 10 页的简写应补上非空条件，否则空集会被误判。

**English:** A subset $W\subseteq V$ uses the addition and scalar multiplication inherited from $V$. If $W$ is nonempty and closed under both operations, it is a subspace. The other axioms are inherited from the ambient space, so they need not all be checked again. Nonemptiness should be included in the abbreviated test on slide 10 to exclude the empty set.

$$
\boxed{W\ne\varnothing,\quad u,v\in W,\ a,b\in\mathbb F
\ \Longrightarrow\ au+bv\in W.}
$$

**中文：** 非空加数乘封闭自动给出 $0_V\in W$。做题时更方便先检查零向量，再检查任意线性组合。若零向量不在集合中，可以立即否定。

**English:** Nonemptiness and closure under scalar multiplication imply $0_V\in W$. In calculations, it is often convenient to check the zero vector first and then arbitrary linear combinations. If the set does not contain zero, it cannot be a subspace.

### 过原点与不过原点 / Through the origin or shifted away

**教学补充 / Teaching supplement：**

$$
W_0=\{(x,y)^T:x+y=0\},\qquad
W_1=\{(x,y)^T:x+y=1\}.
$$

**中文：** $W_0$ 是子空间：若 $u_1+u_2=0$、$v_1+v_2=0$，那么 $(au+bv)_1+(au+bv)_2=0$。$W_1$ 不是子空间，因为零向量不满足 $x+y=1$。它是一条平移后的直线。过原点是必要条件，但对任意集合而言并不充分。

**English:** The set $W_0$ is a subspace: if $u_1+u_2=0$ and $v_1+v_2=0$, then the components of $au+bv$ also sum to zero. The set $W_1$ is not a subspace because the zero vector does not satisfy $x+y=1$. It is a translated line. Containing the origin is necessary but not sufficient for an arbitrary set.

### 讲义例子：齐次方程的解空间 / Lecture example: solutions of a homogeneous system

$$
A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n},\qquad
\mathcal N(A)=\{x\in\mathbb R^n:Ax=0\}.
$$

**中文：** 零向量满足方程。若 $Ax=Ay=0$，那么 $A(ax+by)=aAx+bAy=0$，所以 $\mathcal N(A)$ 是 $\mathbb R^n$ 的子空间，叫零空间或核。讲义所说“解是 $n$ 维列向量”指解有 $n$ 个分量，并不是说零空间的维数一定为 $n$。

**English:** Zero satisfies the equation. If $Ax=Ay=0$, then $A(ax+by)=aAx+bAy=0$. Thus $\mathcal N(A)$ is a subspace of $\mathbb R^n$, called the null space or kernel. The slide’s statement that a solution is an “$n$-dimensional column vector” means that it has $n$ entries; it does not mean that the null space always has dimension $n$.

**教学补充完整例题 / Supplementary worked example：**

$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}1&2&1\\0&1&1\end{bmatrix},\qquad
\begin{cases}x_1+2x_2+x_3=0,\\x_2+x_3=0.\end{cases}
$$

**中文：** 取自由变量 $x_3=t$，由第二式得 $x_2=-t$，再由第一式得 $x_1=t$。因此解空间是一条过原点的直线，基包含一个向量。

**English:** Set the free variable $x_3=t$. The second equation gives $x_2=-t$, and the first gives $x_1=t$. The solution space is therefore a line through the origin with one basis vector.

$$
x=t\begin{bmatrix}1\\-1\\1\end{bmatrix},\qquad
\mathcal N(A)=\operatorname{span}\left\{\begin{bmatrix}1\\-1\\1\end{bmatrix}\right\},
\qquad\dim\mathcal N(A)=1.
$$

**中文：** 教学补充：若 $Ax=b$ 可解，固定一个特解 $x_p$，所有解为 $x_p+\mathcal N(A)$。当 $b\ne0$ 时，它不是线性子空间，因为不包含零向量；这解释了“齐次”为什么重要。

**English:** Teaching supplement: if $Ax=b$ is solvable and $x_p$ is a particular solution, all solutions are $x_p+\mathcal N(A)$. When $b\ne0$, this is not a linear subspace because it does not contain zero. This explains why homogeneity matters.

## 1.4.2 生成空间、基与秩 / Span, basis, and rank

> 对应 / Source: L2, pp. 12–13；Theorems 1.5–1.7。

### Span 的含义 / The meaning of span

$$
\operatorname{span}\{v_1,\ldots,v_s\}
=\left\{\sum_{i=1}^s k_iv_i:k_i\in\mathbb F\right\}.
$$

**中文：** 生成空间包含所有允许的线性组合，不只包含给定的几个向量。它是包含这些向量的最小子空间：任意包含它们的子空间，都必须因封闭性而包含它们的全部线性组合。

**English:** The span contains every allowed linear combination, not just the listed vectors. It is the smallest subspace containing them: any subspace containing the vectors must also contain all their linear combinations by closure.

**中文：** Theorem 1.5 说 span 是子空间。证明很直接：两个线性组合再作线性组合，只会重新组合系数，不会引入新的向量。Theorem 1.6 说有限维母空间中的每个子空间都可由有限个向量生成；取这个子空间的一组基即可。

**English:** Theorem 1.5 states that a span is a subspace. A linear combination of two linear combinations merely recombines their coefficients. Theorem 1.6 states that every subspace of the finite-dimensional ambient space has a finite spanning set: take a basis of that subspace.

### 向量组的秩 / Rank of a list of vectors

**中文：** 向量组的秩是其中极大线性无关组的向量数，也等于它生成空间的维数。这是 Theorem 1.7。列表中有 $s$ 个向量，不代表生成空间有 $s$ 维；有冗余时，秩更小。

**English:** The rank of a vector list is the number of vectors in a maximal independent subset, equal to the dimension of its span. This is Theorem 1.7. A list of $s$ vectors need not span an $s$-dimensional space; redundancy reduces the rank.

$$
\dim\operatorname{span}\{v_1,\ldots,v_s\}
=\operatorname{rank}[v_1\ \cdots\ v_s].
$$

### 怎样从生成向量中选基？ / How to select a basis from generators

**中文：** 教学补充：把向量写成列并行化简，找到主元列的位置，再回到原矩阵取对应列。原列与化简后的列不是同一个向量；若题目要求“从给定向量中选基”，应使用原列。

**English:** Teaching supplement: put the vectors in columns and row-reduce. Identify the pivot-column positions, then select those columns from the original matrix. Row-reduced columns are generally different vectors; selecting a basis from the given vectors requires the original columns.

$$
v_1=\begin{bmatrix}1\\0\\1\end{bmatrix},\quad
v_2=\begin{bmatrix}0\\1\\1\end{bmatrix},\quad
v_3=\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\\2\end{bmatrix},\quad
M=\begin{bmatrix}1&0&1\\0&1&1\\1&1&2\end{bmatrix}
\longrightarrow
\begin{bmatrix}1&0&1\\0&1&1\\0&0&0\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** 第 1、2 列为主元列。$v_1,v_2$ 是生成空间的一组基；$v_3=v_1+v_2$ 是冗余列。因此 $\operatorname{rank}M=2$，生成空间维数也是 2。

**English:** Columns 1 and 2 are pivot columns. The original vectors $v_1,v_2$ form a basis of the span, while $v_3=v_1+v_2$ is redundant. Hence $\operatorname{rank}M=2$, and the span has dimension two.

## 1.4.3 子空间的交、和与并 / Intersection, sum, and union of subspaces

> 对应 / Source: L2, pp. 14–17；Theorems 1.8–1.9。

### 三种运算不能混淆 / Distinguish the three operations

| 运算 / Operation | 定义 / Definition | 是否一定为子空间？/ Always a subspace? |
|---|---|---|
| 交 / Intersection | $U\cap W=\{v:v\in U\text{ and }v\in W\}$ | 是 / Yes |
| 和 / Sum | $U+W=\{u+w:u\in U,w\in W\}$ | 是 / Yes |
| 并 / Union | $U\cup W=\{v:v\in U\text{ or }v\in W\}$ | 一般不是 / Generally no |

**中文：** 交是两个空间共有的部分；和允许从两个空间各取一个向量并相加；并只收集属于任一空间的现成向量，不自动收集跨空间的和。$U+W$ 是包含 $U$ 和 $W$ 的最小子空间。

**English:** The intersection is the common part. The sum allows a vector from each space to be added. The union collects vectors already belonging to either space, without automatically including sums across the two spaces. The sum $U+W$ is the smallest subspace containing both.

**中文：** 交的封闭性来自两个空间各自封闭。和的封闭性可通过重新分组验证：$a(u_1+w_1)+b(u_2+w_2)=(au_1+bu_2)+(aw_1+bw_2)$，前一项属于 $U$，后一项属于 $W$。

**English:** Closure of the intersection follows from closure of both spaces. For the sum, regroup:
$a(u_1+w_1)+b(u_2+w_2)=(au_1+bu_2)+(aw_1+bw_2)$.
The first term belongs to $U$ and the second to $W$.

### 讲义反例：两条坐标轴的并 / Lecture counterexample: the union of two axes

$$
U=\operatorname{span}\{e_1\},\quad
W=\operatorname{span}\{e_2\}\subseteq\mathbb R^2.
$$

**中文：** $e_1,e_2$ 都在并集中，但 $e_1+e_2=(1,1)^T$ 不在任一坐标轴上，因此并不封闭。另一方面，两条轴的和是整个平面，交只有原点。

**English:** Both $e_1$ and $e_2$ belong to the union, but $e_1+e_2=(1,1)^T$ lies on neither axis, so the union is not closed. In contrast, their sum is the whole plane, and their intersection contains only zero.

$$
U\cap W=\{0\},\qquad U+W=\mathbb R^2,\qquad U\cup W\ne\mathbb R^2.
$$

**中文：** 条件补充：并“不是子空间”是一般性提醒，并非没有例外。对两个子空间，$U\cup W$ 是子空间，当且仅当 $U\subseteq W$ 或 $W\subseteq U$。若二者互不包含，取 $u\in U\setminus W$、$w\in W\setminus U$，则 $u+w$ 不属于并集。

**English:** Qualification: “the union is not a subspace” is a general warning, not a statement without exceptions. For two subspaces, $U\cup W$ is a subspace exactly when one contains the other. If neither contains the other, choose $u\in U\setminus W$ and $w\in W\setminus U$; then $u+w$ belongs to neither subspace.

### 和的生成向量 / Generators of the sum

$$
U=\operatorname{span}\{u_1,\ldots,u_s\},\quad
W=\operatorname{span}\{w_1,\ldots,w_t\}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
U+W=\operatorname{span}\{u_1,\ldots,u_s,w_1,\ldots,w_t\}.
$$

**中文：** 这是 Theorem 1.8。把两边的生成向量合并，得到的是和的生成组；还要删去冗余才能得到基。两个基直接拼在一起，通常不自动成为和的基。

**English:** This is Theorem 1.8. Combining generators gives a spanning list for the sum; redundancy must still be removed to obtain a basis. Concatenating two bases does not automatically give a basis of their sum.

### 维数公式与完整例子 / Dimension formula and worked example

$$
\boxed{\dim(U+W)=\dim U+\dim W-\dim(U\cap W).}
$$

**中文：** Theorem 1.9 的含义是：先加两边维数，再减去被数了两次的公共方向。教学补充例子：在 $\mathbb R^3$ 中，取 $U=\operatorname{span}\{e_1,e_2\}$，$W=\operatorname{span}\{e_2,e_3\}$。

**English:** Theorem 1.9 says to add the dimensions and subtract the common directions counted twice. For a supplementary example in $\mathbb R^3$, let $U=\operatorname{span}\{e_1,e_2\}$ and $W=\operatorname{span}\{e_2,e_3\}$.

$$
U\cap W=\operatorname{span}\{e_2\},\quad
U+W=\mathbb R^3,\qquad 3=2+2-1.
$$

**中文：** 证明思路：先取交空间的基 $(z_1,\ldots,z_r)$，分别把它扩成 $U$ 的基 $(z_1,\ldots,z_r,u_1,\ldots,u_p)$ 和 $W$ 的基 $(z_1,\ldots,z_r,w_1,\ldots,w_q)$。公共基只保留一份，合起来构成和的基，因此维数为 $r+p+q$。

**English:** Proof idea: take a basis $(z_1,\ldots,z_r)$ of the intersection, and extend it to bases of $U$ and $W$ by adding $u_1,\ldots,u_p$ and $w_1,\ldots,w_q$, respectively. Keeping the common basis only once produces a basis of the sum, of size $r+p+q$.

**中文：** 为确认合并后无关，若 $\sum a_i z_i+\sum b_j u_j+\sum c_k w_k=0$，则 $\sum c_k w_k$ 同时属于 $U$ 和 $W$，因此属于交空间。$W$ 中扩展基的无关性迫使所有 $c_k=0$，再用 $U$ 的基迫使其余系数为 0。

**English:** To verify independence, suppose $\sum a_i z_i+\sum b_j u_j+\sum c_k w_k=0$. Then $\sum c_k w_k$ belongs to both spaces, hence to their intersection. Independence of the extended basis of $W$ forces all $c_k=0$, after which independence of the basis of $U$ forces all remaining coefficients to vanish.

### 计算交空间的方法 / How to compute an intersection

**中文：** 教学补充：若 $B_U,B_W$ 是两空间的基矩阵，共同向量满足 $B_Ua=B_Wb$。解下面的齐次系统，再把解代入 $v=B_Ua$，即可得到交的生成向量，最后选择无关组。

**English:** Teaching supplement: if $B_U,B_W$ are basis matrices, a common vector satisfies $B_Ua=B_Wb$. Solve the following homogeneous system, substitute its solutions into $v=B_Ua$, and then select an independent set of the resulting generators.

$$
\begin{bmatrix}B_U&-B_W\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}a\\b\end{bmatrix}=0.
$$

## 1.4.4 直和与补空间 / Direct sums and complementary subspaces

> 对应 / Source: L2, pp. 18–20；Definition 1.19, Theorem 1.10, Definition 1.20, Theorem 1.11。

### 直和比普通和多了什么？ / What makes a sum direct?

**中文：** 若两个子空间只有零向量是公共元素，它们的和叫直和，本文记为 $U\oplus W$。普通和保证可以分解；直和进一步保证分解唯一。讲义使用的带点加号也表示这个意思。

**English:** If two subspaces share only the zero vector, their sum is a direct sum, written $U\oplus W$ here. An ordinary sum gives existence of a decomposition; a direct sum also gives uniqueness. The dotted-plus notation in the slides has the same meaning.

$$
\boxed{U+W=U\oplus W\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad U\cap W=\{0\}.}
$$

**中文：** 若 $v=u_1+w_1=u_2+w_2$，那么 $u_1-u_2=w_2-w_1$ 属于交空间。交只有零向量，就得到 $u_1=u_2$、$w_1=w_2$。反过来，若交中有非零 $z$，则 $z=z+0=0+z$，分解不唯一。

**English:** If $v=u_1+w_1=u_2+w_2$, then $u_1-u_2=w_2-w_1$ belongs to the intersection. A trivial intersection forces $u_1=u_2$ and $w_1=w_2$. Conversely, a nonzero $z$ in the intersection has two decompositions, $z=z+0=0+z$.

### 讲义中的等价条件 / Equivalent conditions in the slides

| 直和的等价判定 / Equivalent tests for a direct sum |
|---|
| $U\cap W=\{0\}$ |
| $\dim(U+W)=\dim U+\dim W$ |
| 两空间的基拼接后是和空间的基 / Concatenating bases gives a basis of the sum |
| 和中每个向量的分解 $v=u+w$ 唯一 / Each vector in the sum has a unique decomposition |

**中文：** 注意：$U\oplus W$ 不一定等于整个母空间 $V$。例如 $\mathbb R^3$ 中 $\operatorname{span}\{e_1\}\oplus\operatorname{span}\{e_2\}$ 只是 $xy$ 平面。要得到整个 $V$ 的直和分解，还必须检查 $U+W=V$。

**English:** A direct sum $U\oplus W$ need not equal the entire ambient space $V$. For example, $\operatorname{span}\{e_1\}\oplus\operatorname{span}\{e_2\}$ in $\mathbb R^3$ is only the $xy$-plane. A direct-sum decomposition of all of $V$ additionally requires $U+W=V$.

**中文：** 教学补充：三个及以上子空间不能只检查两两交为零。例如 $\mathbb R^2$ 中三条不同的过原点直线两两交为零，但三条线不能组成三维的直和，因为它们全在二维平面内。

**English:** Teaching supplement: for three or more subspaces, pairwise trivial intersections alone are insufficient. Three distinct lines through the origin in $\mathbb R^2$ intersect pairwise only at zero, but cannot form a three-dimensional direct sum inside a two-dimensional plane.

### 补空间存在，但通常不唯一 / Complements exist but are generally not unique

**中文：** 若 $V=U\oplus W$，则 $W$ 是 $U$ 在 $V$ 中的补空间，反之亦然。有限维情形下，先取 $U$ 的基，再把它扩充成 $V$ 的基；新增基向量生成的空间就是一个补空间。这证明了 Theorem 1.11。

**English:** If $V=U\oplus W$, then $W$ is a complement of $U$ in $V$, and vice versa. In finite dimensions, extend a basis of $U$ to a basis of $V$. The additional basis vectors span a complement, proving Theorem 1.11.

$$
\dim W=\dim V-\dim U.
$$

**讲义例子 / Lecture example：**

$$
U=\operatorname{span}\{e_1,e_2\}\subseteq\mathbb R^3,
\quad W_1=\operatorname{span}\{e_3\},
\quad W_2=\operatorname{span}\{(0,1,1)^T\}.
$$

**中文：** $W_1,W_2$ 都与 $U$ 只有零交，而且都补足第三个方向。因此 $\mathbb R^3=U\oplus W_1=U\oplus W_2$。任意 $(x,y,z)^T$ 在这两种分解中的表达分别如下。

**English:** Both $W_1$ and $W_2$ intersect $U$ only at zero and supply the missing third direction. Thus $\mathbb R^3=U\oplus W_1=U\oplus W_2$. A vector $(x,y,z)^T$ has the following decompositions.

$$
\begin{bmatrix}x\\y\\z\end{bmatrix}
=\begin{bmatrix}x\\y\\0\end{bmatrix}
+z\begin{bmatrix}0\\0\\1\end{bmatrix}
=\begin{bmatrix}x\\y-z\\0\end{bmatrix}
+z\begin{bmatrix}0\\1\\1\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** 每个选定补空间下的分解唯一，但补空间的选择本身不唯一。“补空间”也不自动表示“正交补”；正交需要额外的内积结构。

**English:** The decomposition is unique once a complement is fixed, but the choice of complement need not be unique. A complement is not automatically an orthogonal complement; orthogonality requires an inner product.
