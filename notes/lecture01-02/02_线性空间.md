## 1.2.1 线性空间：向量不一定是箭头 / Linear spaces: vectors need not be arrows

> 对应 / Source: L1, pp. 29–34；Definition 1.11, Example 1.3, Definition 1.12。

### 定义与公理 / Definition and axioms

**中文：** 域 $\mathbb F$ 上的线性空间，也称向量空间，是一个非空集合 $V$，配有向量加法 $V\times V\to V$ 和数乘 $\mathbb F\times V\to V$。向量加法构成交换群，数乘满足下面四条规则。两种运算都要封闭。

**English:** A linear space, or vector space, over a field $\mathbb F$ is a nonempty set $V$ equipped with vector addition $V\times V\to V$ and scalar multiplication $\mathbb F\times V\to V$. Vector addition forms an Abelian group, and scalar multiplication satisfies the four rules below. Both operations must be closed.

| 向量加法 / Vector addition | 数乘 / Scalar multiplication |
|---|---|
| $u+v=v+u$ | $1v=v$ |
| $(u+v)+w=u+(v+w)$ | $a(bv)=(ab)v$ |
| 存在 $0_V$，$v+0_V=v$ / An additive identity exists | $(a+b)v=av+bv$ |
| 存在 $-v$，$v+(-v)=0_V$ / Additive inverses exist | $a(u+v)=au+av$ |

**中文：** 数乘中的 $1$ 是域的单位元；$0_V$ 是向量空间的零向量。它们属于不同类型的对象。由公理可推出 $0v=0_V$、$a0_V=0_V$、$(-1)v=-v$；这些不是另外增加的公理。

**English:** The $1$ in scalar multiplication is the field identity, while $0_V$ is the zero vector. They are different kinds of objects. The axioms imply $0v=0_V$, $a0_V=0_V$, and $(-1)v=-v$; these are consequences rather than extra axioms.

**推导 / Derivation：**

$$
0v=(0+0)v=0v+0v
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 0v=0_V.
$$

**中文：** 最后一步是在向量加法群中，两边加上 $0v$ 的加法逆元。不要说“除以向量”，因为一般向量没有除法。

**English:** The last step adds the additive inverse of $0v$ to both sides in the vector additive group. Do not describe this as division by a vector; general vectors have no division operation.

### 矩阵也可以是向量 / Matrices can be vectors

**中文：** 讲义 Example 1.3 使用 $\mathbb C^{n\times m}$。在通常的矩阵加法、数乘下，它是 $\mathbb C$ 上的线性空间。这里一个“向量”就是一整张矩阵；矩阵乘法不参与这一定义。当 $m=1$ 时，得到熟悉的列向量空间 $\mathbb C^n$。

**English:** Example 1.3 uses $\mathbb C^{n\times m}$. With ordinary matrix addition and scalar multiplication, it is a vector space over $\mathbb C$. Here a “vector” is an entire matrix; matrix multiplication is not part of this vector-space definition. When $m=1$, we obtain the familiar column-vector space $\mathbb C^n$.

$$
\begin{bmatrix}1&2\\3&4\end{bmatrix}
+2\begin{bmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{bmatrix}
=\begin{bmatrix}1&4\\5&4\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** 教学补充：$\mathbb F^{n\times m}$ 的维数是 $nm$，因为每个位置都可由一个只有该位置为 1、其余为 0 的矩阵独立表示。矩阵的行数或列数单独都不是该空间的维数。

**English:** Teaching supplement: $\dim\mathbb F^{n\times m}=nm$, because each entry is independently represented by a matrix having one at that position and zeros elsewhere. Neither the row count nor the column count alone is the dimension of this space.

### 次数有界的多项式空间 / Degree-bounded polynomial spaces

$$
\mathbb F_{n-1}[\lambda]
=\{a_0+a_1\lambda+\cdots+a_{n-1}\lambda^{n-1}:a_i\in\mathbb F\}.
$$

**中文：** 这是次数不超过 $n-1$ 的多项式空间，包含零多项式。加法和数乘不会产生超过 $n-1$ 的幂，因此封闭。讲义特别提醒：“次数恰好等于 $n-1$”的集合不是线性空间，因为乘以 0 会离开该集合；两个最高次项还可能相消。

**English:** This space consists of polynomials of degree at most $n-1$, including zero. Addition and scalar multiplication cannot create powers above $n-1$, so both operations are closed. The slides emphasize that polynomials of degree exactly $n-1$ do not form a vector space: multiplication by zero leaves the set, and leading terms may cancel under addition.

**中文：** 教学补充：所有多项式 $\mathbb F[\lambda]$ 也是线性空间，但维数是无限的；有限次数空间 $\mathbb F_{n-1}[\lambda]$ 的维数是 $n$。这两个集合不能混用。

**English:** Teaching supplement: all polynomials $\mathbb F[\lambda]$ also form a vector space, but its dimension is infinite. The degree-bounded space $\mathbb F_{n-1}[\lambda]$ has dimension $n$. These two sets should not be confused.

### 标量域会影响维数 / The scalar field affects dimension

**中文：** 教学补充：同一个集合 $\mathbb C$，作为 $\mathbb C$ 上的空间，基可以取 $(1)$，维数为 1；作为 $\mathbb R$ 上的空间，基可取 $(1,i)$，维数为 2。因此“这是几维空间”必须结合标量域回答。

**English:** Teaching supplement: the same set $\mathbb C$ has basis $(1)$ and dimension one over $\mathbb C$, but basis $(1,i)$ and dimension two over $\mathbb R$. The scalar field is essential when stating a dimension.

$$
a+bi=(a+bi)\cdot1\quad\text{over }\mathbb C,
\qquad a+bi=a\cdot1+b\cdot i\quad\text{over }\mathbb R.
$$

## 1.2.2 线性组合与线性相关性 / Linear combinations and dependence

> 对应 / Source: L1, pp. 35–37；Definitions 1.13–1.14, Theorem 1.3。

### 什么叫线性组合？ / What is a linear combination?

**中文：** 用标量乘若干向量，再相加，得到它们的线性组合。若目标向量 $v$ 能这样写，就说它可由这些向量线性表示。以后遇到“是否可表示”，应把它转成一个关于系数的方程。

**English:** Multiplying vectors by scalars and adding them produces a linear combination. If a target $v$ can be written this way, it is linearly representable by those vectors. To decide representability, write an equation for the coefficients.

$$
v=k_1v_1+\cdots+k_sv_s.
$$

### 独立与相关 / Independence and dependence

$$
k_1v_1+\cdots+k_sv_s=0.
$$

**中文：** 如果这个方程只有 $k_1=\cdots=k_s=0$ 一种解，向量组线性无关；如果存在至少一个系数非零的解，向量组线性相关。“非平凡解”指系数不全为零，不是指每个系数都非零。

**English:** The vectors are linearly independent if the only solution is $k_1=\cdots=k_s=0$. They are linearly dependent if there is a solution with at least one nonzero coefficient. A nontrivial solution means the coefficients are not all zero, not that every coefficient must be nonzero.

**中文：** 直观上，线性相关表示某个向量可以用其他向量表达，因而存在冗余。含零向量的向量组一定相关。只有两个非零向量时，“相关”等价于二者互为标量倍数；三个以上向量不能只用“是否两两平行”判断。

**English:** Intuitively, dependence means that one vector can be expressed using the others, so the list contains redundancy. Any list containing the zero vector is dependent. For two nonzero vectors, dependence means one is a scalar multiple of the other. For three or more vectors, checking pairwise parallelism is insufficient.

### 完整例题：两个不平行向量，加一个冗余向量 / Worked example: two directions and one redundant vector

**教学补充 / Teaching supplement：**

$$
v_1=\begin{bmatrix}1\\0\end{bmatrix},\quad
v_2=\begin{bmatrix}0\\1\end{bmatrix},\quad
v_3=\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** $k_1v_1+k_2v_2=0$ 给出 $k_1=k_2=0$，所以前两个向量无关。但 $v_1+v_2-v_3=0$ 给出非平凡关系，所以三个向量相关。虽然它们没有任何一对平行，整体仍有冗余。

**English:** The equation $k_1v_1+k_2v_2=0$ forces $k_1=k_2=0$, so the first two vectors are independent. However, $v_1+v_2-v_3=0$ is a nontrivial relation, so the three vectors are dependent. No pair is parallel, yet the full list is redundant.

### 讲义 Theorem 1.3 的证明 / Proof of Theorem 1.3

**中文：** 若 $v_1,\ldots,v_r$ 无关，但加入 $v_{r+1}$ 后相关，则 $v_{r+1}$ 可以由前 $r$ 个向量唯一表示。先从相关关系出发：若最后一个系数为 0，就与前 $r$ 个向量无关矛盾。因此最后一个系数非零，可以在标量域中除以它。

**English:** If $v_1,\ldots,v_r$ are independent but adding $v_{r+1}$ makes the list dependent, then $v_{r+1}$ has a unique representation using the first $r$ vectors. Start with a nontrivial dependence relation. Its last coefficient cannot be zero, because that would contradict independence of the first $r$ vectors. We may therefore divide by that coefficient in the scalar field.

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{r+1}k_iv_i=0,\quad k_{r+1}\ne0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
v_{r+1}=-\sum_{i=1}^{r}\frac{k_i}{k_{r+1}}v_i.
$$

**中文：** 若它还有另一种表示，两种表示相减，得到前 $r$ 个向量的零线性组合；无关性迫使对应系数相同，因此表示唯一。

**English:** If there were another representation, subtracting the two would give a zero linear combination of the first $r$ vectors. Their independence forces the corresponding coefficients to agree, proving uniqueness.

### 做题方法 / Calculation method

**中文：** 对列向量，将它们按列组成 $M=[v_1\ \cdots\ v_s]$。判断无关性就是求解 $Mk=0$；若每一列都有主元，只有零解，向量组无关。判断 $v$ 能否表示，则解 $Mk=v$。后者可能无解、有唯一解或有多个解。

**English:** For coordinate columns, form $M=[v_1\ \cdots\ v_s]$. Independence is equivalent to $Mk=0$ having only the zero solution, which occurs when every column has a pivot. To test whether $v$ is representable, solve $Mk=v$. This system may have no solution, a unique solution, or multiple solutions.
