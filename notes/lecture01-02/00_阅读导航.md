## 符号约定 / Notation

| 符号 / Symbol | 含义 / Meaning |
|---|---|
| $\mathbb F$ | 标量域，通常为 $\mathbb R$ 或 $\mathbb C$ / Scalar field, usually real or complex |
| $V,U,W$ | 线性空间或子空间 / Linear spaces or subspaces |
| $v,\alpha,\beta$ | 抽象向量；也可能是矩阵或多项式 / Abstract vectors, possibly matrices or polynomials |
| $\lambda$ | 多项式的不定元 / Polynomial indeterminate |
| $\mathbb F_{n-1}[\lambda]$ | 次数至多 $n-1$ 的多项式及零多项式 / Polynomials of degree at most $n-1$, including zero |
| $B=(b_1,\ldots,b_n)$ | 有顺序的基 / Ordered basis |
| $[v]_B$ | 向量 $v$ 关于基 $B$ 的坐标列 / Coordinate column of $v$ in basis $B$ |
| $\operatorname{span},\dim,\operatorname{rank}$ | 生成空间、维数、秩 / Span, dimension, rank |
| $\ker T,\operatorname{im}T$ | 核与像 / Kernel and image |

**中文：** 写 $v=Bx$ 时，$Bx$ 是 $x_1b_1+\cdots+x_nb_n$ 的简写。若 $V=\mathbb F^n$ 且基向量已写成标准坐标列，$B$ 就是通常的基矩阵。若向量是多项式，这个写法表示线性组合，不意味着多项式本身就是数字列。

**English:** In $v=Bx$, the expression $Bx$ abbreviates $x_1b_1+\cdots+x_nb_n$. If $V=\mathbb F^n$ and the basis vectors are given as standard coordinate columns, $B$ is an ordinary basis matrix. For polynomial vectors, this notation denotes a linear combination; it does not identify a polynomial with a numerical column without choosing coordinates.

## 课程概览 / Course Overview

> 对应 / Source: L1, pp. 1–9。

**中文：** 线性代数研究的核心对象是线性空间，以及空间之间保持线性组合的映射。矩阵是选定基以后描述这些映射的工具。学习顺序因此是：先弄清楚对象和运算，再建立坐标，最后用矩阵计算。

**English:** Linear algebra studies linear spaces and mappings that preserve linear combinations. A matrix describes such a mapping after bases have been chosen. The learning order is therefore: understand the objects and operations, introduce coordinates, and then calculate with matrices.

$$
\text{集合与运算 / Sets and operations}
\longrightarrow \text{线性空间 / Linear spaces}
\longrightarrow \text{基与坐标 / Bases and coordinates}
\longrightarrow \text{线性映射 / Linear mappings}
\longrightarrow \text{矩阵 / Matrices}.
$$

**中文：** 讲义将课程分为两部分。前四章讲线性空间、映射、多项式与多项式矩阵、线性变换的典范形式，以及酉空间中的变换。后六章讲分解、范数、矩阵函数、矩阵值函数与微分方程、广义逆和矩阵方程。当前两个 Lecture 都属于第一章。讲义列出的课程安排为 48 学时，成绩构成为作业 30% 与考试 70%；这里只把它们作为该讲义的课程信息记录。

**English:** The slides divide the course into two parts. Chapters 1–4 cover linear spaces and mappings, polynomials and polynomial matrices, canonical forms of linear transformations, and transformations in unitary spaces. Chapters 5–10 cover decompositions, norms, matrix functions, matrix-valued functions and differential equations, generalized inverses, and matrix equations. Both current lectures belong to Chapter 1. The slides state 48 lecture hours and a grading split of 30% exercises and 70% examinations; these are recorded as information from these slides.

**中文：** 讲义的学习建议是理解定义和定理背后的思想，并先用二维或三维例子理解抽象概念。它还列出 MIT 线性代数公开课、《Matrix Analysis》和黄琳《系统与控制理论中的线性代数》作为延伸资料。本笔记的直接依据仍是这两个 Lecture。

**English:** The slides recommend understanding the ideas behind definitions and theorems, beginning with two- or three-dimensional examples. They also list the MIT linear algebra course, *Matrix Analysis*, and Huang Lin’s *Linear Algebra in Systems and Control Theory* as further reading. The direct sources for these notes remain the two lectures.
