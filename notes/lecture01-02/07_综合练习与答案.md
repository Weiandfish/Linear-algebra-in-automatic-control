## 解答 / Solutions

### A1 群与域 / Groups and fields

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** 整数加法封闭、结合，单位元为 $0$；每个整数 $a$ 的加法逆元 $-a$ 仍为整数，所以是群，且加法交换。

**Step 1.** Integer addition is closed and associative, with identity zero and inverse $-a$ for every integer. It is an abelian group.

$$
\begin{gathered}
a+0=a,\\
a+(-a)=0.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 2 步。** 整数乘法虽封闭、结合且有单位元 $1$，但不是每个元素都有乘法逆元。只要一个元素失败，整个结构就不是群。

**Step 2.** Integer multiplication has closure, associativity and identity one, but not an inverse for every element. A single failure rules out a group.

$$
\begin{gathered}
0b=0\ne1\quad (\forall b\in\mathbb Z),\\
2b=1\Longrightarrow b=1/2\notin\mathbb Z.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 3 步。** 非零有理数乘法封闭、结合，单位元为 $1$，每个 $a=p/q\ne0$ 的逆元为 $q/p$，仍属非零有理数，故是交换群。域还要求加法成交换群、乘法分配；整数与有理数都满足这些，其区别在所有非零数的乘法逆元。

**Step 3.** Nonzero rationals are closed under multiplication and have inverse $q/p$ for $p/q\ne0$, so they form an abelian group. Both integer and rational arithmetic have abelian addition and distributivity; the missing nonzero multiplicative inverses prevent the integers from being a field.

$$
\boxed{(\mathbb Z,+),\ (\mathbb Q\setminus\{0\},\times)\text{ are groups};\quad\mathbb Z\text{ is not a field},\quad\mathbb Q\text{ is a field}.}
$$

### A2 多项式运算 / Polynomial operations

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** 相同次数的项分别相加；未出现的系数按零处理。

**Step 1.** Add coefficients of like powers, treating omitted coefficients as zero.

$$
\begin{gathered}
p+q=(2-2)+(-1+1)\lambda+3\lambda^2+\lambda^3=3\lambda^2+\lambda^3,\\
\deg(p+q)=3.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 2 步。** 求负多项式时每项变号；乘法按分配律展开。

**Step 2.** Negate every coefficient and expand the product using distributivity.

$$
\begin{gathered}
-p=-2+\lambda-3\lambda^2,\\
(1+\lambda)(1-\lambda)=1-\lambda+\lambda-\lambda^2=1-\lambda^2.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 3 步。** 本题常数、一次项相消，但三次项保留，故次数仍为 $3$。若最高次项相消，次数才可能低于两者次数的最大值；若完全相消则得到零多项式，其次数不按普通非零多项式定义。

**Step 3.** The cubic term survives here. Degree can fall below the larger input degree when leading terms cancel. Complete cancellation gives the zero polynomial, whose degree requires a separate convention.

$$
\begin{gathered}
\lambda^2+(-\lambda^2+1)=1,\\
\deg1=0.
\end{gathered}
$$

### A3 线性空间与子空间 / Vector spaces and subspaces

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** 子空间须含零，并对任意线性组合封闭。若 $u,v\in U_0$，其三个分量之和都为零，那么 $au+bv$ 的分量之和也为零，且零向量属于 $U_0$。

**Step 1.** A subspace contains zero and is closed under arbitrary linear combinations. The coordinate sum of $au+bv$ is zero whenever both input sums are zero.

$$
\sum_i(au_i+bv_i)=a\sum_i u_i+b\sum_i v_i=0\quad \Longrightarrow U_0\text{ is a subspace}.
$$

**第 2 步。** $U_1$ 不含零向量，因此不是子空间。对称矩阵集含零矩阵，任意两个对称矩阵的实线性组合仍对称，因此是子空间。

**Step 2.** The affine set $U_1$ excludes zero and fails the test. Symmetric matrices contain zero and remain symmetric under real linear combinations.

$$
\begin{gathered}
0+0+0\ne1,\\
(aM+bN)^T=aM^T+bN^T=aM+bN.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 3 步。** 次数恰好为二的集合不含零多项式；例如 $\lambda^2\in P$，但 $0\cdot\lambda^2=0\notin P$，所以不是子空间。

**Step 3.** Exactly quadratic polynomials exclude zero: multiplying $\lambda^2$ by zero leaves the set. Thus the set is not a subspace.

$$
\boxed{U_0,S\text{ are subspaces};\quad U_1,P\text{ are not}.}
$$

### A4 相关性与表示 / Dependence and representation

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** 先找零线性组合。第三个向量等于前两个之和，所以三个向量相关；它没有增添新方向。

**Step 1.** The third vector is the sum of the first two, giving a nontrivial zero relation and no new spanning direction.

$$
\begin{gathered}
v_3=v_1+v_2,\\
v_1+v_2-v_3=0.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 2 步。** 检验前两个是否独立：零组合的第一、第三分量分别直接给出两个系数为零，因此它们构成生成空间的基。

**Step 2.** The first and third coordinates of a zero combination force both coefficients to vanish. The first two vectors therefore form a basis of the span.

$$
\begin{gathered}
av_1+bv_2=(a,a+b,b)^T=0\iff a=b=0,\\
\dim\operatorname{span}\{v_1,v_2,v_3\}=2.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 3 步。** 令 $av_1+bv_2=v$ 并比较分量，得到唯一基坐标。若使用三个生成向量，则可以沿相关关系调整系数，故不唯一。

**Step 3.** Compare coordinates to solve for the unique basis coefficients. With all three generators, the dependence relation provides one free parameter.

$$
\begin{gathered}
(a,a+b,b)=(2,3,1)\Longrightarrow(a,b)=(2,1),\\
[v]_{(v_1,v_2)}=\begin{pmatrix}2\\1\end{pmatrix}.\\
v=(2-t)v_1+(1-t)v_2+t v_3\quad (t\in\mathbb R).
\end{gathered}
$$

### A5 多项式基 / Polynomial basis

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** 基须同时线性无关并生成整个空间。先对三个候选基函数作一般线性组合，并按幂整理。

**Step 1.** A basis must be independent and spanning. Expand an arbitrary combination of the proposed basis polynomials.

$$
a\cdot1+b(1+\lambda)+c(\lambda+\lambda^2)=(a+b)+(b+c)\lambda+c\lambda^2.
$$

**第 2 步。** 若等于零多项式，每个幂的系数都为零，依次求得 $c=b=a=0$，故独立。对于任意目标 $r+s\lambda+t\lambda^2$，下面的系数方程均有解，所以张成全空间。

**Step 2.** For a zero polynomial, coefficient comparison forces all three coefficients to zero. For any target polynomial, the displayed equations have a solution, proving spanning.

$$
\begin{gathered}
c=t,\\
b+c=s\Longrightarrow b=s-t,\\
a+b=r\Longrightarrow a=r-s+t.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 3 步。** 本题 $r=2,s=3,t=1$，代入得 $a=0,b=2,c=1$。坐标按基的既定顺序排列，并代回检验。

**Step 3.** Substitute the target coefficients, order the answer according to the basis and reconstruct the polynomial to verify it.

$$
\begin{gathered}
\boxed{[p]_B=(0,2,1)^T},\\
0\cdot1+2(1+\lambda)+(\lambda+\lambda^2)=2+3\lambda+\lambda^2.
\end{gathered}
$$

### A6 过渡矩阵 / Transition matrix

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** $C=BP$ 表示 $P$ 的第 $j$ 列是 $c_j$ 在旧基 $B$ 下的坐标。本题旧基为标准基，可直接按列放入。

**Step 1.** The columns of $P$ are the old coordinates of the new basis vectors. Since the old basis is standard, these are the displayed columns.

$$
P=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix},\quad \det P=1,\quad P^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\-1&2\end{pmatrix}.
$$

**第 2 步。** 同一个向量满足 $[v]_B=P[v]_C$；已知旧坐标，应左乘逆矩阵得到新坐标。

**Step 2.** Old coordinates equal $P$ times new coordinates, so multiply the known old coordinate column by the inverse.

$$
[v]_C=P^{-1}[v]_B=\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\-1&2\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}5\\3\end{pmatrix}=\boxed{\begin{pmatrix}2\\1\end{pmatrix}}.
$$

**第 3 步。** 新坐标表示用两个新基向量作线性组合，确实还原原向量。

**Step 3.** The new coefficients reconstruct exactly the original vector.

$$
2c_1+c_2=2(2,1)^T+(1,1)^T=(5,3)^T=v.
$$

### A7 核、像与维数 / Kernel, image, and dimensions

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** 核由所有满足 $Ax=0$ 的输入组成。写一般输入 $x=(x_1,x_2,x_3)^T$，令输出的两个分量同时为零，并取自由参数 $x_3=t$。

**Step 1.** The kernel consists of inputs mapped to zero. Solve both output equations, using the third input coordinate as a free parameter.

$$
Ax=(x_1+x_3,x_2-x_3)^T=0\iff x_1=-t,\ x_2=t,\ x_3=t.\quad \boxed{\ker T=\{t(-1,1,1)^T:t\in\mathbb R\}}.
$$

**第 2 步。** 像是全部可能输出。任意 $(u,v)^T$ 均可取输入 $(u,v,0)^T$ 得到，故像就是整个输出空间；也可由原矩阵前两列得出。

**Step 2.** Every output column is attained by setting the third input coordinate to zero. The first two original matrix columns also form an image basis.

$$
\begin{gathered}
T\big((u,v,0)^T\big)=(u,v)^T,\\
\boxed{\operatorname{im}T=\mathbb R^2=\operatorname{span}\{(1,0)^T,(0,1)^T\}}.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 3 步。** 核的基有一个向量，像的基有两个向量。非零核意味着不同输入可能有相同输出；像覆盖整个陪域意味着满射。

**Step 3.** The kernel has one basis vector and the image has two. A nontrivial kernel rules out injectivity, whereas the full image gives surjectivity.

$$
\begin{gathered}
\operatorname{nullity}T=1,\quad \operatorname{rank}T=2,\quad 1+2=\dim\mathbb R^3;\\
T\text{ is surjective, not injective}.
\end{gathered}
$$

### A8 交、和、并与直和 / Intersection, sum, union, and direct sum

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** 先写两空间的一般元素。在两者交集中，第三分量必须既为零，又等于 $W$ 中的自由参数，故该参数为零。

**Step 1.** Write generic vectors in both spaces. Membership in the intersection forces the third coordinate of a vector in $W$ to vanish.

$$
\begin{gathered}
U=\{(x,y,0)^T\},\quad W=\{(a,a,b)^T\},\\
U\cap W=\{a(1,1,0)^T\}=\operatorname{span}\{e_1+e_2\},\quad \dim(U\cap W)=1.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 2 步。** 和包含 $U$ 的 $e_1,e_2$，也包含 $W$ 的 $e_3$，因此生成全部三维空间。交非零，因此和不是直和。

**Step 2.** The sum contains all three standard basis vectors and hence is the whole ambient space. Its nonzero intersection prevents a direct sum.

$$
U+W=\mathbb R^3,\quad \text{basis }(e_1,e_2,e_3),\quad \dim(U+W)=3=2+2-1.
$$

**第 3 步。** 并要求向量属于至少一边，却不保证两边的向量相加后还属于其中一边。以下反例证明其不对加法封闭。

**Step 3.** For a union, vectors may come from different sides, and their sum need not lie in either. This counterexample disproves closure.

$$
e_1\in U,\ e_3\in W,\quad e_1+e_3=(1,0,1)^T\notin U\quad \text{and}\quad e_1+e_3\notin W.
$$

### A9 补空间与分解 / Complement and decomposition

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** 直和要求交为零且和为全空间。先让两种一般元素相等，比较分量求交；再对任意向量给出分解。

**Step 1.** A direct sum requires zero intersection and full spanning. Compare generic elements for the intersection, then explicitly decompose an arbitrary vector.

$$
\begin{gathered}
a(1,1)^T=b(1,0)^T\Longrightarrow(a,a)=(b,0)\Longrightarrow a=b=0.\\
(x,y)^T=y(1,1)^T+(x-y)(1,0)^T.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 2 步。** 上述通式也给出系数唯一性：第二分量先确定 $a=y$，第一分量再确定 $b=x-y$。代入 $v$ 得到分解。

**Step 2.** The second coordinate fixes the coefficient in $U$, and the first then fixes the coefficient in $W$, proving uniqueness. Substitute the given vector.

$$
\boxed{v=5(1,1)^T-2(1,0)^T}.
$$

**第 3 步。** 另一补空间可取纵轴。它与 $U$ 交为零，且任意向量仍能唯一分解如下。

**Step 3.** The vertical axis is another complement: its intersection with $U$ is zero and the following formula spans the plane.

$$
\begin{gathered}
W'=\operatorname{span}\{(0,1)^T\},\\
(x,y)^T=x(1,1)^T+(y-x)(0,1)^T.
\end{gathered}
$$

### A10 线性判定 / Linearity tests

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** $T_1$ 可写成固定矩阵乘坐标列，利用矩阵乘法的分配律与数乘相容性直接验证。

**Step 1.** Write the first map as matrix multiplication and use distributivity and compatibility with scalar multiplication.

$$
M=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix},\quad T_1(au+bv)=M(au+bv)=aMu+bMv=aT_1(u)+bT_1(v).
$$

**第 2 步。** 任何线性映射必须把零映到零，因为 $T(0)=T(0+0)=2T(0)$。$T_2$ 不满足该必要条件。

**Step 2.** Every linear map sends zero to zero, as follows by applying additivity to zero plus zero. The second map violates this condition.

$$
T_2(0,0)=(1,0)^T\ne(0,0)^T.
$$

**第 3 步。** $T_3$ 虽把零映到零，但不保持数乘。一个必要条件成立并不能代替完整的线性验证。

**Step 3.** The third map sends zero to zero but fails homogeneity. Passing one necessary test does not establish linearity.

$$
\begin{gathered}
T_3(2(1,0))=(4,0)^T\ne(2,0)^T=2T_3(1,0).\\
\boxed{T_1\text{ linear};\quad T_2,T_3\text{ nonlinear}.}
\end{gathered}
$$

### A11 非方阵的矩阵表示 / Rectangular representation

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** 矩阵有三行、两列，满足 $[T(u)]_E=[T]_{E\leftarrow B}[u]_B$。先求两个输入基向量的像，再将输出坐标逐列排入。

**Step 1.** The matrix has three rows and two columns and acts on input basis coordinates. Compute the images of the two basis vectors and use their standard output columns.

$$
\begin{gathered}
T(b_1)=(1,2,1)^T,\quad T(b_2)=(1,0,-1)^T,\\
\boxed{[T]_{E\leftarrow B}=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\2&0\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}}.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 2 步。** 输入坐标 $(2,1)^T$ 的意思是 $u=2b_1+b_2$。先还原，再直接用映射和矩阵两种方法检验输出。

**Step 2.** The given coordinates mean $u=2b_1+b_2$. Reconstruct the input and check the output both directly and by the matrix.

$$
u=2(1,1)^T+(1,-1)^T=(3,1)^T,\quad T(u)=(3,4,1)^T,\quad \begin{pmatrix}1&1\\2&0\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}2\\1\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}3\\4\\1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

**第 3 步。** 一般输出的中间分量等于第一、第三分量之和。反之每个满足这个关系的向量都由输入 $(r,t)^T$ 得到，所以这恰好描述整个像，而不仅是包含关系。

**Step 3.** Every output has its middle coordinate equal to the sum of the outer two. Conversely, every vector satisfying that relation comes from input $(r,t)^T$.

$$
\operatorname{im}T=\{(r,s,t)^T:s=r+t\}=\operatorname{span}\{(1,1,0)^T,(0,1,1)^T\},\quad \dim\operatorname{im}T=2.
$$

### A12 相似变换 / Similarity transformation

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** 新输入坐标先乘 $P$ 成为旧坐标，旧矩阵计算后再乘 $P^{-1}$ 转为新输出坐标。

**Step 1.** Convert the new input coordinates to old coordinates with $P$, apply the old matrix, then convert back with its inverse.

$$
\begin{gathered}
[T(v)]_C=P^{-1}[T(v)]_B=P^{-1}A[v]_B=P^{-1}AP[v]_C,\\
A_C=P^{-1}AP.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 2 步。** 由 $P^2=2I$ 得到逆矩阵，再按顺序相乘。

**Step 2.** Since $P^2=2I$, its inverse is $P/2$. Multiply in the derived order.

$$
\begin{gathered}
P^{-1}=\tfrac12\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix},\quad AP=\begin{pmatrix}2&2\\3&-3\end{pmatrix},\\
\boxed{A_C=\begin{pmatrix}5/2&-1/2\\-1/2&5/2\end{pmatrix}}.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 3 步。** 把新矩阵的结果转成旧坐标，应与直接用旧矩阵相同；这给出不需要再求逆的检验。

**Step 3.** Converting the new-matrix output to old coordinates agrees with applying the old matrix after conversion. This verifies the result without another inversion.

$$
PA_C=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}5/2&-1/2\\-1/2&5/2\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}2&2\\3&-3\end{pmatrix}=AP.
$$
