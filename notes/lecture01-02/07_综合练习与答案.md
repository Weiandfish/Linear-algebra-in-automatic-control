# 综合练习与答案 / Exercises and answers

**中文：** 以下 12 题均为教学补充，按两个 Lecture 的进度排列。先做题，再到后面的答案区核对；不仅核对数字，也核对自己是否写出了必要条件和理由。

**English:** The following 12 exercises are teaching supplements ordered by the lectures’ progression. Solve them before consulting the answer section. Check not only numerical results but also conditions and reasoning.

## 题目 / Questions

<a id="e1"></a>

### E1 群与域 / Groups and fields

**中文：** 判断 $(\mathbb Z,+)$、$(\mathbb Z,\times)$、$(\mathbb Q\setminus\{0\},\times)$ 是否为群。再说明为什么 $\mathbb Z$ 不是域而 $\mathbb Q$ 是域。

**English:** Decide whether $(\mathbb Z,+)$, $(\mathbb Z,\times)$, and $(\mathbb Q\setminus\{0\},\times)$ are groups. Explain why $\mathbb Z$ is not a field whereas $\mathbb Q$ is.

<a id="e2"></a>

### E2 多项式运算 / Polynomial operations

$$
p=2-\lambda+3\lambda^2,\qquad q=-2+\lambda+\lambda^3.
$$

**中文：** 求 $p+q$、$-p$、$\deg(p+q)$，并解释为什么加法后次数可能下降。再计算 $(1+\lambda)(1-\lambda)$。

**English:** Find $p+q$, $-p$, and $\deg(p+q)$. Explain why addition can lower polynomial degree. Also compute $(1+\lambda)(1-\lambda)$.

<a id="e3"></a>

### E3 线性空间与子空间 / Vector spaces and subspaces

**中文：** 使用通常运算，判断以下集合是否为相应母空间的子空间，并给出理由：$\mathbb R^3$ 中 $x+y+z=0$ 的解集；$x+y+z=1$ 的解集；所有实对称 $2\times2$ 矩阵；次数恰好为 2 的实多项式。

**English:** With the usual operations, classify these subsets of their ambient spaces: solutions of $x+y+z=0$ in $\mathbb R^3$; solutions of $x+y+z=1$; all symmetric real $2\times2$ matrices; real polynomials of degree exactly two. Give reasons.

<a id="e4"></a>

### E4 相关性与表示 / Dependence and representation

$$
v_1=\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\\0\end{bmatrix},\quad
v_2=\begin{bmatrix}0\\1\\1\end{bmatrix},\quad
v_3=\begin{bmatrix}1\\2\\1\end{bmatrix},\quad
v=\begin{bmatrix}2\\3\\1\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** 判断三个向量是否无关，求其生成空间的基与维数，再用所选基表示 $v$。这种基下的表示是否唯一？如果使用三个生成向量，系数是否唯一？

**English:** Test independence, find a basis and dimension of the span, and express $v$ using that basis. Is the basis representation unique? Are coefficients unique if all three generators are used?

<a id="e5"></a>

### E5 多项式基 / A polynomial basis

$$
B=(1,1+\lambda,\lambda+\lambda^2),\qquad p=2+3\lambda+\lambda^2.
$$

**中文：** 验证 $B$ 是 $\mathbb R_2[\lambda]$ 的基，并求 $[p]_B$。

**English:** Verify that $B$ is a basis of $\mathbb R_2[\lambda]$ and find $[p]_B$.

<a id="e6"></a>

### E6 过渡矩阵 / A transition matrix

$$
B=(e_1,e_2),\qquad
C=\left(\begin{bmatrix}2\\1\end{bmatrix},\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix}\right),\qquad
v=\begin{bmatrix}5\\3\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** 按 $C=BP$ 的约定求 $P$、$P^{-1}$ 和 $[v]_C$，并把新坐标还原检验。

**English:** Using the convention $C=BP$, find $P$, $P^{-1}$, and $[v]_C$. Reconstruct the vector to check your result.

<a id="e7"></a>

### E7 核、像与维数 / Kernel, image, and dimensions

$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}1&0&1\\0&1&-1\end{bmatrix},\qquad
T:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R^2,\quad T(x)=Ax.
$$

**中文：** 求核的一组基、像的一组基、秩和零度。$T$ 是否单射？是否满射？

**English:** Find bases of the kernel and image, rank, and nullity. Is $T$ injective? Is it surjective?

<a id="e8"></a>

### E8 交、和、并与直和 / Intersection, sum, union, and direct sum

$$
U=\operatorname{span}\{e_1,e_2\},\qquad
W=\operatorname{span}\{e_1+e_2,e_3\}\subseteq\mathbb R^3.
$$

**中文：** 求 $U\cap W$ 和 $U+W$ 的基与维数。它们的和是直和吗？$U\cup W$ 是子空间吗？给出理由。

**English:** Find bases and dimensions of $U\cap W$ and $U+W$. Is the sum direct? Is $U\cup W$ a subspace? Justify your answers.

<a id="e9"></a>

### E9 补空间与唯一分解 / A complement and unique decomposition

$$
U=\operatorname{span}\{(1,1)^T\},\quad
W=\operatorname{span}\{(1,0)^T\},\quad v=(3,5)^T.
$$

**中文：** 证明 $\mathbb R^2=U\oplus W$，并求 $v=u+w$ 的唯一分解。再给 $U$ 找一个不同的补空间。

**English:** Prove $\mathbb R^2=U\oplus W$ and find the unique decomposition $v=u+w$. Find another complement of $U$.

<a id="e10"></a>

### E10 线性判定 / Linearity tests

**中文：** 在实数域上，判断 $T_1(x,y)=(x+y,x-y)$、$T_2(x,y)=(x+1,y)$、$T_3(x,y)=(x^2,y)$ 是否线性。对线性映射给出一般验证，对非线性映射给出反例。

**English:** Over $\mathbb R$, test the linearity of $T_1(x,y)=(x+y,x-y)$, $T_2(x,y)=(x+1,y)$, and $T_3(x,y)=(x^2,y)$. Give a general verification for the linear map and counterexamples for the others.

<a id="e11"></a>

### E11 非方阵的矩阵表示 / A rectangular matrix representation

$$
T:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R^3,\quad T\big((x,y)^T\big)=(x,x+y,y)^T,
\qquad B=((1,1)^T,(1,-1)^T).
$$

**中文：** 输出使用 $\mathbb R^3$ 的标准基 $E$。求 $[T]_{E\leftarrow B}$；若输入的 $B$ 坐标为 $(2,1)^T$，求实际输入向量及其像。再描述像空间。

**English:** Use the standard output basis $E$ of $\mathbb R^3$. Find $[T]_{E\leftarrow B}$. If the input has $B$-coordinates $(2,1)^T$, find the actual input and its image. Describe the image space.

<a id="e12"></a>

### E12 同一映射在两组基下的矩阵 / One mapping in two bases

$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}2&0\\0&3\end{bmatrix},\qquad
P=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** 若新基等于旧基乘 $P$，求新基下的矩阵 $A'$，并通过 $AP=PA'$ 检验。

**English:** If the new basis equals the old basis multiplied by $P$, find the new matrix $A'$ and check it using $AP=PA'$.

## 解答 / Solutions

<a id="a1"></a>

### A1 群与域 / Groups and fields

**中文：** 第一项是加法交换群，单位元为 0，逆元为 $-a$。第二项不是群，例如 0 无乘法逆元，2 也没有整数逆元。第三项是乘法交换群：非零有理数的乘积仍非零，单位元为 1，逆元 $1/a$ 仍是非零有理数。整数缺少非零元素的普遍乘法逆元，因此不是域；有理数满足这一要求，因此是域。

**English:** The first is an additive Abelian group with identity 0 and inverses $-a$. The second is not a group: zero has no multiplicative inverse, and 2 has no integer inverse. The third is a multiplicative Abelian group: nonzero rational numbers are closed under multiplication, the identity is 1, and $1/a$ is again nonzero rational. Integers lack multiplicative inverses for all nonzero elements, so they are not a field; rationals satisfy this requirement.

<a id="a2"></a>

### A2 多项式运算 / Polynomial operations

$$
p+q=3\lambda^2+\lambda^3,\qquad
-p=-2+\lambda-3\lambda^2,\qquad \deg(p+q)=3,
\qquad (1+\lambda)(1-\lambda)=1-\lambda^2.
$$

**中文：** 这里常数项和一次项相消，但最高三次项保留，次数为 3。一般情况下若最高次项也相消，次数会下降；例如 $\lambda^2+(-\lambda^2+1)=1$。

**English:** The constant and linear terms cancel here, but the cubic term remains, so the degree is three. If leading terms cancel, the degree can decrease; for example, $\lambda^2+(-\lambda^2+1)=1$.

<a id="a3"></a>

### A3 线性空间与子空间 / Vector spaces and subspaces

**中文：** 第一项是齐次线性方程的核，所以是子空间。第二项不包含零向量，所以不是。第三项是子空间，因为 $S^T=S$、$R^T=R$ 推出 $(aS+bR)^T=aS+bR$，并且零矩阵对称。第四项不是，因为乘以 0 后得到零多项式，次数不再恰好为 2。

**English:** The first is the kernel of a homogeneous linear equation, hence a subspace. The second excludes zero, hence is not. The third is a subspace because $S^T=S$ and $R^T=R$ imply $(aS+bR)^T=aS+bR$, and the zero matrix is symmetric. The fourth fails closure under multiplication by zero.

<a id="a4"></a>

### A4 相关性与表示 / Dependence and representation

$$
v_3=v_1+v_2,\qquad v=2v_1+v_2.
$$

**中文：** 三个向量相关。前两个无关：比较第一和第三分量即可迫使零组合的两个系数都为零。因此 $(v_1,v_2)$ 是生成空间的基，维数为 2，$v$ 的基坐标为 $(2,1)^T$ 且唯一。使用全部三个生成向量时，系数不唯一，可以把同一个关系的任意倍数加进去。

**English:** The three vectors are dependent. The first two are independent, since the first and third components force both coefficients of a zero combination to vanish. Thus $(v_1,v_2)$ is a basis of the span, of dimension two, and $v$ has unique basis coordinates $(2,1)^T$. Using all three generators gives nonunique coefficients because arbitrary multiples of their dependence relation can be added.

$$
v=(2-t)v_1+(1-t)v_2+t v_3,\qquad t\in\mathbb R.
$$

<a id="a5"></a>

### A5 多项式基 / Polynomial basis

$$
a\cdot1+b(1+\lambda)+c(\lambda+\lambda^2)
=(a+b)+(b+c)\lambda+c\lambda^2.
$$

**中文：** 若结果为零，则 $c=0$，再得 $b=0$、$a=0$，故无关。任意标准系数 $(r,s,t)$ 可取 $c=t$、$b=s-t$、$a=r-s+t$，故生成全部二次以下多项式。对本题 $p$，得到 $c=1,b=2,a=0$。

**English:** If the expression is zero, coefficient comparison gives $c=0$, then $b=0$ and $a=0$, proving independence. Any monomial coefficients $(r,s,t)$ are obtained with $c=t$, $b=s-t$, and $a=r-s+t$, proving spanning. For this $p$, we get $c=1,b=2,a=0$.

$$
[p]_B=\begin{bmatrix}0\\2\\1\end{bmatrix}.
$$

<a id="a6"></a>

### A6 过渡矩阵 / Transition matrix

$$
P=\begin{bmatrix}2&1\\1&1\end{bmatrix},\quad
P^{-1}=\begin{bmatrix}1&-1\\-1&2\end{bmatrix},\quad
[v]_C=P^{-1}\begin{bmatrix}5\\3\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}2\\1\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** 还原：$2(2,1)^T+(1,1)^T=(5,3)^T$。直接用 $P[v]_B$ 会把方向用反。

**English:** Reconstruction gives $2(2,1)^T+(1,1)^T=(5,3)^T$. Using $P[v]_B$ would apply the conversion in the wrong direction.

<a id="a7"></a>

### A7 核、像与维数 / Kernel, image, and dimensions

$$
Ax=0\ \Longrightarrow\ x_1=-x_3,\quad x_2=x_3
\ \Longrightarrow\quad
\ker T=\operatorname{span}\{(-1,1,1)^T\}.
$$

**中文：** 前两列为 $\mathbb R^2$ 的标准基，所以像空间是整个 $\mathbb R^2$，可取基 $(e_1,e_2)$。秩为 2，零度为 1，$3=2+1$。非零核说明不单射；像等于陪域说明满射。

**English:** The first two columns are the standard basis of $\mathbb R^2$, so the image is all of $\mathbb R^2$ with basis $(e_1,e_2)$. Rank is two and nullity one, giving $3=2+1$. The nontrivial kernel prevents injectivity; equality of image and codomain gives surjectivity.

<a id="a8"></a>

### A8 交、和、并与直和 / Intersection, sum, union, and direct sum

**中文：** $W$ 中向量形如 $(a,a,b)^T$；要同时属于 $U$，必须 $b=0$。因此交的基为 $(e_1+e_2)$，维数为 1。和包含 $e_1,e_2,e_3$，所以是整个 $\mathbb R^3$，维数为 3，符合 $3=2+2-1$。交非零，和不是直和。并也不是子空间：$e_1,e_3$ 都在并中，但 $e_1+e_3=(1,0,1)^T$ 不在任何一边。

**English:** Vectors in $W$ have the form $(a,a,b)^T$; membership in $U$ requires $b=0$. The intersection therefore has basis $(e_1+e_2)$ and dimension one. The sum contains $e_1,e_2,e_3$, so it is all of $\mathbb R^3$, of dimension three, consistent with $3=2+2-1$. The nonzero intersection prevents a direct sum. The union is not a subspace: it contains $e_1,e_3$ but not their sum $(1,0,1)^T$.

<a id="a9"></a>

### A9 补空间与分解 / Complement and decomposition

**中文：** 若 $a(1,1)^T=b(1,0)^T$，比较第二分量得 $a=0$，再得 $b=0$，所以交为零。两空间维数各为 1，直和维数为 2，因此等于整个平面。分解系数由 $a=5$、$a+b=3$ 得到。

**English:** If $a(1,1)^T=b(1,0)^T$, the second component gives $a=0$, then $b=0$, so the intersection is trivial. Both spaces have dimension one, making the direct sum two-dimensional and equal to the whole plane. The decomposition coefficients satisfy $a=5$ and $a+b=3$.

$$
v=5\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix}-2\begin{bmatrix}1\\0\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** 另一个补空间可取 $\operatorname{span}\{(0,1)^T\}$。它与 $U$ 同样交为零，且合起来生成整个平面。

**English:** Another complement is $\operatorname{span}\{(0,1)^T\}$; it also has a trivial intersection with $U$ and together they span the plane.

<a id="a10"></a>

### A10 线性判定 / Linearity tests

$$
T_1(v)=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{bmatrix}v
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad T_1(au+bv)=aT_1(u)+bT_1(v).
$$

**中文：** $T_1$ 线性。$T_2$ 不线性，因为 $T_2(0,0)=(1,0)$。$T_3$ 不线性：$T_3(2,0)=(4,0)$，但 $2T_3(1,0)=(2,0)$。最后一个例子再次说明零映到零不足以证明线性。

**English:** The map $T_1$ is linear. The map $T_2$ is not, since $T_2(0,0)=(1,0)$. The map $T_3$ is not, since $T_3(2,0)=(4,0)$ but $2T_3(1,0)=(2,0)$. The last example again shows that mapping zero to zero is insufficient.

<a id="a11"></a>

### A11 非方阵的矩阵表示 / Rectangular representation

$$
T(b_1)=\begin{bmatrix}1\\2\\1\end{bmatrix},\quad
T(b_2)=\begin{bmatrix}1\\0\\-1\end{bmatrix},\quad
[T]_{E\leftarrow B}=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\2&0\\1&-1\end{bmatrix}.
$$

$$
u=2b_1+b_2=\begin{bmatrix}3\\1\end{bmatrix},\qquad
[T(u)]_E=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\2&0\\1&-1\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}2\\1\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}3\\4\\1\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** 像空间是平面 $\{(r,s,t)^T:s=r+t\}$，维数为 2。注意矩阵所乘的 $(2,1)^T$ 是输入的 $B$ 坐标，实际输入是 $(3,1)^T$。

**English:** The image is the plane $\{(r,s,t)^T:s=r+t\}$, of dimension two. The matrix multiplies the input’s $B$-coordinates $(2,1)^T$, whereas the actual input is $(3,1)^T$.

<a id="a12"></a>

### A12 相似变换 / Similarity transformation

$$
A'=P^{-1}AP
=\begin{bmatrix}5/2&-1/2\\-1/2&5/2\end{bmatrix},\qquad
AP=\begin{bmatrix}2&2\\3&-3\end{bmatrix}=PA'.
$$

**中文：** 等式 $AP=PA'$ 表示“先从新坐标转为旧坐标，再按旧矩阵映射”与“先按新矩阵映射，再转回旧坐标”得到相同结果。它也是检查相似变换乘法次序的办法。

**English:** The equality $AP=PA'$ says that converting new coordinates to old and applying the old matrix agrees with applying the new matrix and then converting back. It checks the multiplication order in a similarity transformation.
