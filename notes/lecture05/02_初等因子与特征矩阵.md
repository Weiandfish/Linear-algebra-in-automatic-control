## 一、把不变多项式分解为素因子幂 / Factor invariant polynomials into prime powers

**中文：** 在复数域，设全部不同根为 $\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_s$，不变多项式可写为

**English:** Over the complex field, let the distinct roots be $\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_s$. Write the invariant polynomials as

$$
d_i(\lambda)=\prod_{j=1}^s(\lambda-\alpha_j)^{e_{ij}},\qquad
0\le e_{1j}\le e_{2j}\le\cdots\le e_{rj}.
$$

**中文：** 所有指数为正的完整因子幂 $(\lambda-\alpha_j)^{e_{ij}}$，连同出现重数，组成初等因子的多重集合。指数为零的 $1$ 不计，零对角元也不计。同一根来自不同不变多项式的幂要分别保留，不能合并成一个总幂。

**English:** The full powers $(\lambda-\alpha_j)^{e_{ij}}$ with positive exponent, counted with their occurrences, form the multiset of elementary divisors. Omit unit factors and zero diagonal entries. Powers belonging to different invariant polynomials remain separate even when they have the same root.

**中文：** 一般域上完全同理，只需将一次因子换成首一不可约多项式。实数域可能出现不可约二次因子的幂，不能默认所有初等因子都来自实根。

**English:** Over a general field, replace linear factors by monic irreducible polynomials. Over the reals, powers of irreducible quadratics can occur; elementary divisors need not all arise from real roots.

**教学补充例题 / Teaching supplement：**

$$
S=\operatorname{diag}\bigl(1,(\lambda-1)^2,(\lambda-1)^3(\lambda+2)\bigr).
$$

**中文：** 初等因子为 $(\lambda-1)^2$、$(\lambda-1)^3$、$\lambda+2$。不能把前两个合并成 $(\lambda-1)^5$；总重数虽相同，分配信息却会丢失。

**English:** The elementary divisors are $(\lambda-1)^2$, $(\lambda-1)^3$ and $\lambda+2$. Replacing the first two by $(\lambda-1)^5$ preserves total multiplicity but loses their distribution among invariant polynomials.

## 二、如何恢复不变多项式 / Reconstructing invariant polynomials

**中文：** 必须知道秩 $r$。对每个根，单独把初等因子的指数从小到大排列；不足 $r$ 项时在**左侧**补零。按列对应的相同位置相乘，得到 $d_1,\ldots,d_r$，这样自然满足整除链。

**English:** The rank $r$ is required. For each root, sort its elementary-divisor exponents in ascending order and pad with zeros on the left to length $r$. Multiply the corresponding prime powers position by position to obtain $d_1,\ldots,d_r$ in divisibility order.

**教学补充例题 / Teaching supplement：** 已知秩为 $3$，初等因子为 $(\lambda-1)$、$(\lambda-1)^3$、$(\lambda+2)^2$。 / Suppose the rank is three and these are the elementary divisors.

| 因子 / Factor | 第 1 项 / Position 1 | 第 2 项 / Position 2 | 第 3 项 / Position 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\lambda-1$ 的指数 / Exponents | 0 | 1 | 3 |
| $\lambda+2$ 的指数 / Exponents | 0 | 0 | 2 |

$$
(d_1,d_2,d_3)=\bigl(1,\lambda-1,(\lambda-1)^3(\lambda+2)^2\bigr).
$$

**中文：** “补在左侧”使每个素因子的指数沿整除链不下降。若把指数任意配对，可能得到不满足整除关系的对角元。

**English:** Left padding makes every prime exponent nondecreasing along the divisibility chain. Arbitrary pairing can produce diagonal entries that fail the required divisibility.

## 三、为什么只看初等因子还不够 / Why elementary divisors alone can be insufficient

**中文：** 第 18 页的提醒涉及被省略的单位因子与零块。仅给初等因子，并没有给出它们属于多少个非零不变多项式，也没有给出整个矩阵的大小。

**English:** The warning on slide 18 concerns omitted unit factors and zero blocks. Elementary divisors alone do not specify the number of nonzero invariant polynomials or the full matrix size.

**教学补充反例 / Teaching counterexample：**

$$
A=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda,0),\qquad
B=\operatorname{diag}(1,\lambda).
$$

**中文：** 两者同为二阶矩阵，初等因子都只有 $\lambda$，但秩分别为 $1,2$，所以不等价。即使矩阵大小相同，也不能丢掉秩。

**English:** Both are two-by-two and have the single elementary divisor $\lambda$, but their ranks are one and two. Hence they are not equivalent. Equal dimensions do not remove the need to retain rank.

**中文：** 反过来，**同型、同秩、初等因子多重集合相同**足以推出等价：按上一节恢复相同的不变多项式，再补入同样的零行列。因此满秩同型矩阵可以直接用初等因子判断等价。

**English:** Conversely, equal size, equal rank and the same multiset of elementary divisors imply equivalence: reconstruct identical invariant polynomials and then add identical zero padding. For full-rank matrices of the same size, elementary divisors therefore suffice.

## 四、分块对角矩阵：初等因子取并集 / Block diagonal matrices: take a multiset union

**中文：** 定理 2.29-2.30 说明，分块对角矩阵的初等因子，是各对角块初等因子的多重集合并集。重复出现的因子仍重复保留；各块的秩相加。

**English:** Theorems 2.29-2.30 state that the elementary divisors of a block diagonal matrix form the multiset union of those of its diagonal blocks. Repeated factors stay repeated, and block ranks add.

$$
A=\operatorname{diag}(B_1,\ldots,B_t),\qquad
\operatorname{rank}A=\sum_{i=1}^t\operatorname{rank}B_i.
$$

**中文：** 对角矩阵是每块为一阶矩阵的特例（定理 2.31）：将每个非零对角多项式分解，收集它的完整素因子幂。不能先把全部对角元相乘再分解，否则会合并本应分开的初等因子。

**English:** A diagonal matrix is the one-by-one-block case of Theorem 2.31. Factor each nonzero diagonal polynomial and collect its full prime powers. Factoring the product of all diagonal entries instead would incorrectly merge separate elementary divisors.

**教学补充例题一 / Teaching supplement 1：**

$$
A=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda^2,\lambda).
$$

**中文：** 初等因子为 $\lambda^2$ 和 $\lambda$；交换对角位置后得到 Smith 形 $\operatorname{diag}(\lambda,\lambda^2)$。不能写成唯一初等因子 $\lambda^3$。

**English:** The elementary divisors are $\lambda^2$ and $\lambda$. Swapping diagonal positions gives Smith form $\operatorname{diag}(\lambda,\lambda^2)$. They must not be replaced by the single elementary divisor $\lambda^3$.

**教学补充例题二 / Teaching supplement 2：**

$$
B=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda,\lambda-1).
$$

**中文：** 初等因子为 $\lambda$、$\lambda-1$。秩为 $2$，两种因子的指数均排成 $(0,1)$，因此不变多项式为 $1$ 与 $\lambda(\lambda-1)$。这说明分块矩阵的不变多项式不能直接照抄各块的列表。

**English:** The elementary divisors are $\lambda$ and $\lambda-1$. Rank is two, and both exponent lists are $(0,1)$, so the invariant polynomials are one and $\lambda(\lambda-1)$. Thus block invariant lists cannot simply be concatenated without rebuilding the divisibility chain.

## 五、特征矩阵：从常数矩阵进入多项式矩阵 / Characteristic matrices

**讲义例 2.25 的定义 / Definition in lecture Example 2.25：** 对常数矩阵 $A\in\mathbb F^{n\times n}$，其特征矩阵为 $X_A(\lambda)=\lambda I_n-A$。 / For a constant matrix, its characteristic matrix, called the eigenmatrix in the homework, is $X_A(\lambda)=\lambda I_n-A$.

**中文：** 最高次系数为 $I_n$，故行列式是首一的 $n$ 次多项式，矩阵在有理函数域上满秩。其不变多项式满足

**English:** The leading coefficient is $I_n$, so the determinant is monic of degree $n$ and the polynomial matrix has full rank over the rational-function field. Its invariant polynomials satisfy

$$
\det(\lambda I_n-A)=d_1(\lambda)\cdots d_n(\lambda).
$$

**中文：** 这个乘积是特征多项式，但乘积本身不包含全部不变多项式的分配信息。下面的数值矩阵来自作业，不是课件第 21 页给出的原例数据。

**English:** This product is the characteristic polynomial, but the product alone does not record how factors are distributed among invariant polynomials. The numerical matrix below comes from the homework, rather than from numerical data on slide 21.

**作业例题：第四次作业第 4 题 / Homework example: Problem 4.** 以下计算取实数或复数域。 / Work over the real or complex field.

$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}-7&-12&-6\\3&5&3\\3&6&2\end{bmatrix},\qquad
X_A=\begin{bmatrix}\lambda+7&12&6\\-3&\lambda-5&-3\\-3&-6&\lambda-2\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** 存在非零常数元素，故 $D_1=1$。在 $\lambda=-1$ 处三行互为倍数、秩为 $1$，全部二阶子式都在该点为零，所以均含 $\lambda+1$。其中一个子式恰为非零常数倍：

**English:** A nonzero constant entry gives $D_1=1$. At $\lambda=-1$ the rows are proportional and rank is one. Every second-order minor therefore vanishes there and is divisible by $\lambda+1$. One such minor is exactly a nonzero constant multiple:

$$
\begin{vmatrix}\lambda+7&6\\-3&-3\end{vmatrix}=-3(\lambda+1).
$$

**中文：** 由此 $D_2=\lambda+1$；计算行列式得 $D_3=(\lambda+1)^2(\lambda-2)$。最终

**English:** Hence $D_2=\lambda+1$, while the determinant gives $D_3=(\lambda+1)^2(\lambda-2)$. Consequently,

$$
S_{X_A}=\operatorname{diag}\bigl(1,\lambda+1,(\lambda+1)(\lambda-2)\bigr).
$$

**中文：** 复数域上的初等因子为 $\lambda+1$、$\lambda+1$、$\lambda-2$。前两项必须分别保留，不能合并成 $(\lambda+1)^2$。若把题目中的域取为特征 $3$ 的域，则 $A=-I$，标准形改为 $(\lambda+1)I_3$，因此系数域条件确实会影响答案。

**English:** Over the complex field the elementary divisors are $\lambda+1$, $\lambda+1$ and $\lambda-2$. The first two remain separate. In characteristic three, however, $A=-I$ and the Smith form is $(\lambda+1)I_3$, illustrating why the coefficient field matters.
