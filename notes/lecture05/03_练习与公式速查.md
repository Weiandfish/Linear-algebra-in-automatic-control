# 练习与公式速查 / Exercises and Formula Reference

[阅读导航 / Guide](00_阅读导航.md) · [Smith 标准形 / Smith form](01_Smith标准形与不变多项式.md) · [初等因子 / Elementary divisors](02_初等因子与特征矩阵.md)

**中文：** 以下 8 题均为教学补充，具体计算取实数或复数域。先明确矩阵大小、秩、整除链和重数，再核对答案。

**English:** These eight exercises are teaching supplements over the real or complex field. Specify matrix size, rank, divisibility and multiplicities before checking the answers.

## 练习 / Exercises

### 1. 合法操作与幺模 / Admissible operations and unimodularity

**题目：** 把一行乘 $\lambda$、把一行乘 $-2$、把另一行的 $\lambda^2$ 倍加到本行，哪些是合法初等变换？$\operatorname{diag}(1,0)$ 的非零不变多项式只有 $1$，它是否幺模？

**Question:** Which are admissible: scaling a row by $\lambda$, scaling it by $-2$, or adding $\lambda^2$ times a different row? Is $\operatorname{diag}(1,0)$ unimodular despite having the single nonzero invariant polynomial one?

**解：** 后两种合法；$\lambda$ 不是多项式环的单位。该对角矩阵不满秩，行列式为零，不幺模。判断幺模须同时满足满秩和全部不变多项式为 $1$。

**Answer:** The latter two operations are admissible; $\lambda$ is not a unit. The diagonal matrix is rank deficient with zero determinant, so it is not unimodular. Full rank and unit invariant polynomials are both required.

### 2. 行列式因子与初等因子 / Determinantal and elementary divisors

**题目：** 对 $A=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda,\lambda^2)$，求 $D_1,D_2$、不变多项式与初等因子。

**Question:** Find $D_1,D_2$, the invariant polynomials and the elementary divisors of $A=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda,\lambda^2)$.

$$
D_1=\lambda,\quad D_2=\lambda^3,\quad
(d_1,d_2)=(\lambda,\lambda^2).
$$

**解：** 初等因子为 $\lambda$、$\lambda^2$，不是单独的 $\lambda^3$。

**Answer:** The elementary divisors are $\lambda$ and $\lambda^2$, not a single $\lambda^3$.

### 3. 相同行列式是否足够 / Is an equal determinant enough?

**题目：** $A=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda,\lambda)$ 与 $B=\operatorname{diag}(1,\lambda^2)$ 是否等价？

**Question:** Are $A=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda,\lambda)$ and $B=\operatorname{diag}(1,\lambda^2)$ equivalent?

**解：** 不等价。二者行列式均为 $\lambda^2$，但 $D_1(A)=\lambda$、$D_1(B)=1$。它们本身已是不同的 Smith 形。

**Answer:** No. Both determinants equal $\lambda^2$, but $D_1(A)=\lambda$ and $D_1(B)=1$. They are already distinct Smith forms.

### 4. 从行列式因子恢复 / Recovering invariants from determinantal divisors

**题目：** 一个满秩三阶矩阵满足 $D_1=1$、$D_2=\lambda-1$、$D_3=(\lambda-1)^3(\lambda+2)$，求不变多项式与初等因子。

**Question:** A full-rank three-by-three matrix has $D_1=1$, $D_2=\lambda-1$ and $D_3=(\lambda-1)^3(\lambda+2)$. Find its invariant polynomials and elementary divisors.

$$
(d_1,d_2,d_3)=\bigl(1,\lambda-1,(\lambda-1)^2(\lambda+2)\bigr).
$$

**解：** 依次计算 $D_k/D_{k-1}$。初等因子为 $\lambda-1$、$(\lambda-1)^2$、$\lambda+2$；整除链成立。

**Answer:** Take consecutive quotients $D_k/D_{k-1}$. The elementary divisors are $\lambda-1$, $(\lambda-1)^2$ and $\lambda+2$, and the divisibility chain holds.

### 5. 从初等因子恢复 / Reconstructing from elementary divisors

**题目：** 秩为 $3$ 的矩阵，其初等因子为 $\lambda-1$、$(\lambda-1)^3$、$(\lambda+2)^2$。求全部非零不变多项式。

**Question:** A rank-three matrix has elementary divisors $\lambda-1$, $(\lambda-1)^3$ and $(\lambda+2)^2$. Find all nonzero invariant polynomials.

**解：** 两个根的指数分别排成 $(0,1,3)$、$(0,0,2)$，同位置相乘，得到

**Answer:** Sort and pad the exponent lists to $(0,1,3)$ and $(0,0,2)$, then multiply by position:

$$
d_1=1,\qquad d_2=\lambda-1,\qquad d_3=(\lambda-1)^3(\lambda+2)^2.
$$

### 6. 初等因子遗漏了什么 / What elementary divisors omit

**题目：** 比较 $A=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda,0,0)$ 与 $B=\operatorname{diag}(1,\lambda,0)$ 的初等因子、秩与等价性。

**Question:** Compare the elementary divisors, ranks and equivalence of $A=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda,0,0)$ and $B=\operatorname{diag}(1,\lambda,0)$.

**解：** 二者的初等因子都只有 $\lambda$，但秩为 $1$ 与 $2$，所以不等价。初等因子不计单位因子，也不记录零块大小。

**Answer:** Both have the single elementary divisor $\lambda$, but ranks one and two, so they are not equivalent. Elementary divisors omit unit factors and do not record zero-block sizes.

### 7. 上双对角结构 / Upper bidiagonal structure

**题目：** 四阶上双对角矩阵的对角元都是 $\lambda-2$，上次对角元依次为 $2,-1,3$，求 Smith 形。

**Question:** A four-by-four upper bidiagonal matrix has diagonal entries $\lambda-2$ and superdiagonal entries $2,-1,3$. Find its Smith form.

**解：** 对 $k=1,2,3$，前 $k$ 行与第 $2$ 至 $k+1$ 列的子式分别为 $2,-2,-6$，均为非零常数。故 $D_1=D_2=D_3=1$，行列式为 $(\lambda-2)^4$，得到

**Answer:** The first $k$ rows and columns $2$ through $k+1$ give minors $2,-2,-6$ for $k=1,2,3$. Thus the first three determinantal divisors are one and the determinant is $(\lambda-2)^4$:

$$
S=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,1,(\lambda-2)^4).
$$

### 8. 伴随结构 / Companion structure

**题目：** 求 $C=\begin{bmatrix}\lambda&0&2\\-1&\lambda&-1\\0&-1&\lambda\end{bmatrix}$ 的 Smith 形。

**Question:** Find the Smith form of $C=\begin{bmatrix}\lambda&0&2\\-1&\lambda&-1\\0&-1&\lambda\end{bmatrix}$.

**解：** 元素中有 $-1$，故 $D_1=1$；第 $2,3$ 行、第 $1,2$ 列构成的子式为 $1$，故 $D_2=1$。行列式为 $\lambda^3-\lambda+2$，所以

**Answer:** A constant entry gives $D_1=1$. The minor on rows two and three and columns one and two is one, giving $D_2=1$. The determinant is $\lambda^3-\lambda+2$, hence

$$
S_C=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,\lambda^3-\lambda+2).
$$

## 核心公式 / Core formulas

| 概念 / Concept | 公式与条件 / Formula and conditions |
| --- | --- |
| 幺模 / Unimodular | $\det U$ 为非零常数 / A nonzero constant determinant |
| 等价 / Equivalence | 同型矩阵 $B=PAQ$，$P,Q$ 幺模 / Same size, unimodular factors |
| Smith 形 / Smith form | 非零对角元首一，$d_i\mid d_{i+1}$，其余为零 / Monic divisibility chain and zero padding |
| 行列式因子 / Determinantal divisor | $D_0=1$，$D_k=d_1\cdots d_k$，$k\le r$ |
| 不变多项式 / Invariant polynomial | $d_k=D_k/D_{k-1}$，$1\le k\le r$ |
| 超过秩的子式 / Beyond rank | $k>r\Rightarrow D_k=0$，不作 $0/0$ / Do not form $0/0$ |
| 初等因子 / Elementary divisors | 各 $d_i$ 的正指数不可约因子幂，保留重数 / Positive prime powers, counted with occurrences |
| 恢复条件 / Reconstruction | 已知秩，指数升序且左侧补零 / Known rank, sorted exponents, left zero padding |
| 分块对角 / Block diagonal | 初等因子多重集合取并集 / Multiset union of elementary divisors |
| 特征矩阵 / Characteristic matrix | $X_A=\lambda I-A$，$\det X_A=\prod_{i=1}^n d_i$ |

**中文：** 自查：先分清 $D_k$、$d_k$ 与初等因子；结果须首一、满足整除链；单位因子与零块分别计数；判等价还要确认大小与秩。满秩方阵最后检查不变多项式的乘积是否等于行列式的首一化结果。

**English:** Distinguish $D_k$, $d_k$ and elementary divisors. Normalize to monic form, check the divisibility chain, and retain unit factors and zero padding separately. Equivalence also requires dimensions and rank. For a full-rank square matrix, check that the invariant product is the monic normalization of its determinant.
