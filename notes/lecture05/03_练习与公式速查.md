### 1. 合法操作与幺模 / Admissible operations and unimodularity

**题目：** 在 $\mathbb R[\lambda]$ 上，判断以下行变换是否为可逆的多项式初等变换：一行乘 $\lambda$；一行乘 $-2$；一行加另一行的 $\lambda^2$ 倍。再判断 $H=\operatorname{diag}(1,0)$ 是否幺模，并解释其唯一非零不变多项式为 $1$ 是否足够。

**Question:** Over the real polynomial ring, test these row operations for polynomial invertibility: multiply a row by $\lambda$; multiply it by $-2$; add $\lambda^2$ times a different row. Is $H=\operatorname{diag}(1,0)$ unimodular when its sole nonzero invariant is one?

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** 合法变换须能用多项式系数的操作还原。乘 $\lambda$ 的逆操作要除以 $\lambda$，不属于多项式行变换，因此不合法；乘非零常数则可以逆。

**Step 1.** Admissible operations must have polynomial inverses. Scaling by $\lambda$ would require division by $\lambda$, whereas a nonzero constant has a constant inverse.

$$
\begin{gathered}
r_i\mapsto\lambda r_i\text{ requires }1/\lambda\notin\mathbb R[\lambda];\\
r_i\mapsto-2r_i\text{ has inverse }r_i\mapsto-\tfrac12r_i.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 2 步。** 加上另一行的任意多项式倍数，可通过减去同样的倍数还原，故合法。

**Step 2.** Adding a polynomial multiple of a different row is invertible by subtraction of that same multiple.

$$
\begin{gathered}
r_i\mapsto r_i+\lambda^2r_j\quad (i\ne j),\\
r_i\mapsto r_i-\lambda^2r_j.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 3 步。** $H$ 不满秩，行列式为零，因此不幺模。非零不变多项式全为单位之外，还须矩阵是方阵且满秩，才能保证幺模。

**Step 3.** The matrix is singular with zero determinant, so it is not unimodular. Unit nonzero invariants are insufficient without square full rank.

$$
\begin{gathered}
\operatorname{rank} H=1<2,\quad \det H=0,\quad H^2=H;\\
H\text{ is not unimodular}.
\end{gathered}
$$

### 2. 行列式因子与初等因子 / Determinantal and elementary divisors

**题目：** 对 $A=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda,\lambda^2)\in\mathbb R[\lambda]^{2\times2}$，求首一行列式因子 $D_1,D_2$、不变多项式 $d_1,d_2$ 和初等因子。$D_k$ 是所有 $k$ 阶子式的最大公因式，$D_0=1$。

**Question:** Find the monic determinantal divisors, invariant polynomials and elementary divisors of the displayed polynomial matrix. The $k$th determinantal divisor is the gcd of all $k$th-order minors, with $D_0=1$.

$$
A=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda,\lambda^2).
$$

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** 一阶子式就是各元素；二阶子式只有整个行列式。含零的最大公因式按非零项计算。

**Step 1.** First-order minors are the entries; the only second-order minor is the determinant. Zero entries do not change the gcd of the nonzero entries.

$$
\begin{gathered}
D_1=\gcd(\lambda,0,0,\lambda^2)=\lambda,\\
D_2=\det A=\lambda^3.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 2 步。** 依次相除得到不变多项式，再把每个不变多项式分别分解为正指数不可约因子幂。不同位置的因子分别保留，不能把它们合成行列式。

**Step 2.** Take consecutive quotients, then factor each invariant separately into positive irreducible powers. Keep distinct occurrences rather than combining them into the determinant.

$$
\begin{gathered}
d_1=D_1/D_0=\lambda,\quad d_2=D_2/D_1=\lambda^2,\\
\boxed{\text{elementary divisors: }\lambda,\lambda^2}.
\end{gathered}
$$

### 3. 相同行列式是否足够 / Is an equal determinant enough?

**题目：** 在 $\mathbb R[\lambda]$ 上，判断 $A=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda,\lambda)$ 与 $B=\operatorname{diag}(1,\lambda^2)$ 是否经幺模左右变换等价。计算各自的行列式因子，解释只看行列式是否足够。

**Question:** Determine polynomial-matrix equivalence under unimodular factors for these diagonal matrices. Compute their determinantal divisors and explain whether determinant equality suffices.

$$
\begin{gathered}
A=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda,\lambda),\\
B=\operatorname{diag}(1,\lambda^2).
\end{gathered}
$$

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** 两者的行列式相同，故二阶行列式因子相同；但一阶因子取所有元素的最大公因式，需要单独计算。

**Step 1.** Equal determinants give equal second-order divisors, but first-order gcds must also agree for equivalence.

$$
\begin{gathered}
D_2(A)=D_2(B)=\lambda^2,\\
D_1(A)=\gcd(\lambda,\lambda)=\lambda,\quad D_1(B)=\gcd(1,\lambda^2)=1.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 2 步。** 不变多项式分别是下列两组，均已满足整除链，所以已是不同的 Smith 标准形。该标准形在首一约定下唯一，故不等价。

**Step 2.** The resulting divisibility chains are already different monic Smith forms. Their uniqueness rules out equivalence despite equal determinants.

$$
\begin{gathered}
(d_1(A),d_2(A))=(\lambda,\lambda),\\
(d_1(B),d_2(B))=(1,\lambda^2).
\end{gathered}
$$

### 4. 从行列式因子恢复 / Recovering invariants from determinantal divisors

**题目：** 某 $3\times3$ 满秩实多项式矩阵的首一行列式因子如下。求全部非零不变多项式和初等因子，检查整除链及乘积。这里给的是多项式矩阵，并未假设它是某个三阶常数矩阵的特征矩阵。

**Question:** A full-rank three-by-three real polynomial matrix has the displayed determinantal divisors. Find its invariant and elementary divisors and check the chain and product. It is not assumed to be a characteristic matrix of a constant matrix.

$$
D_0=1,\quad D_1=1,\quad D_2=\lambda-1,\quad D_3=(\lambda-1)^3(\lambda+2).
$$

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** 不变多项式用相邻行列式因子作商。不要把 $D_3$ 直接当成第三个不变多项式。

**Step 1.** Compute successive quotients. The determinant divisor itself is the product of the invariants, not the last invariant.

$$
d_1=1,\quad d_2=\frac{\lambda-1}{1}=\lambda-1,\quad d_3=\frac{(\lambda-1)^3(\lambda+2)}{\lambda-1}=(\lambda-1)^2(\lambda+2).
$$

**第 2 步。** 单位因子 $1$ 不产生初等因子；对其余两项分别取各一次因子的完整幂，重复出现时分别记下。乘积回到 $D_3$，整除链也成立。

**Step 2.** The unit factor contributes no elementary divisor. Factor each nonunit separately, retaining repeated occurrences. Verify the product and divisibility chain.

$$
\begin{gathered}
\boxed{\lambda-1,\ (\lambda-1)^2,\ \lambda+2},\\
1\mid(\lambda-1)\mid(\lambda-1)^2(\lambda+2),\quad d_1d_2d_3=D_3.
\end{gathered}
$$

### 5. 从初等因子恢复 / Reconstructing from elementary divisors

**题目：** 某实多项式矩阵秩为 $3$，其全部初等因子为 $\lambda-1$、$(\lambda-1)^3$、$(\lambda+2)^2$，每个列出项各出现一次。求三个非零不变多项式，说明指数如何排齐。

**Question:** A rank-three real polynomial matrix has exactly the three listed elementary-divisor occurrences. Reconstruct all three nonzero invariant polynomials, explaining the exponent alignment.

$$
\begin{gathered}
\operatorname{rank}A=3,\\
\mathcal E(A)=\{\lambda-1,(\lambda-1)^3,(\lambda+2)^2\}.
\end{gathered}
$$

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** 固定每个不可约因子，收集其指数并升序排列。非零不变多项式共有三个，所以每组不足三项时从左侧补零；零指数表示该位置没有该因子。

**Step 1.** For each irreducible factor, sort its exponents and pad on the left to the known rank. A zero exponent means that factor is absent at that position.

$$
\begin{gathered}
\lambda-1:\ (1,3)\longmapsto(0,1,3),\\
\lambda+2:\ (2)\longmapsto(0,0,2).
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 2 步。** 同一位置的各因子相乘，得到整除链。再分解这些结果，应恰好恢复题目给出的初等因子，没有新增或丢失。

**Step 2.** Multiply factors at matching positions. The increasing exponent lists guarantee divisibility, and factoring the result recovers the original multiset.

$$
\begin{gathered}
\boxed{d_1=1,\quad d_2=\lambda-1,\quad d_3=(\lambda-1)^3(\lambda+2)^2},\\
d_1\mid d_2\mid d_3.
\end{gathered}
$$

### 6. 初等因子遗漏了什么 / What elementary divisors omit

**题目：** 在 $\mathbb R[\lambda]$ 上比较以下两个 $3\times3$ 多项式矩阵的秩、全部非零不变多项式、初等因子与等价性，并解释初等因子丢失哪些信息。

**Question:** Compare the ranks, nonzero invariant polynomials, elementary divisors and equivalence of these polynomial matrices. Explain what information elementary divisors omit.

$$
\begin{gathered}
A=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda,0,0),\\
B=\operatorname{diag}(1,\lambda,0).
\end{gathered}
$$

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** 对角非零多项式的个数就是这里的多项式矩阵秩。两矩阵已按非零整除链和零项顺序排列，可直接读取 Smith 形。

**Step 1.** For these diagonal matrices, polynomial rank counts nonzero diagonal entries. They are already in Smith order, with zeros after the nonzero divisibility chain.

$$
\begin{gathered}
\operatorname{rank} A=1,\quad (d_i(A))=(\lambda);\\
\operatorname{rank} B=2,\quad (d_i(B))=(1,\lambda).
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 2 步。** 分解非零不变多项式并略去单位项时，两者都只留下一个 $\lambda$。但幺模左右乘法保持秩，所以不能把秩一变成秩二。

**Step 2.** Discarding unit invariants leaves the same single elementary divisor in each case. Unimodular factors preserve rank, so the different ranks rule out equivalence.

$$
\begin{gathered}
\mathcal E(A)=\mathcal E(B)=\{\lambda\},\\
A\not\sim_{\rm equiv}B.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 3 步。** 一般多项式矩阵的初等因子不记录单位不变多项式与零块；须额外保留矩阵大小、秩，才可恢复完整 Smith 形。

**Step 3.** Elementary divisors omit unit invariant factors and zero padding. Matrix dimensions and rank are needed to reconstruct the complete Smith form.

### 7. 上双对角结构 / Upper bidiagonal structure

**题目：** 在 $\mathbb R[\lambda]$ 上求下列四阶上双对角矩阵的 Smith 形。请展示足以确定 $D_1,D_2,D_3$ 的子式，再求 $D_4$ 和各不变多项式。

**Question:** Find the Smith form of the displayed upper bidiagonal polynomial matrix. Exhibit minors determining the first three determinantal divisors, then compute the determinant and invariant polynomials.

$$
H=\begin{pmatrix}\lambda-2&2&0&0\\0&\lambda-2&-1&0\\0&0&\lambda-2&3\\0&0&0&\lambda-2\end{pmatrix}.
$$

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** 只要某个 $k$ 阶子式是非零常数，所有 $k$ 阶子式的首一最大公因式就只能是 $1$。分别取前 $k$ 行和第 $2$ 至 $k+1$ 列，可得到三个容易算的三角子式。

**Step 1.** A nonzero constant minor forces the monic gcd of all minors of that order to be one. Select the first $k$ rows and columns two through $k+1$.

$$
\begin{gathered}
k=1:\ 2;\\
k=2:\ \det\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\\lambda-2&-1\end{pmatrix}=-2;\\
k=3:\ \det\begin{pmatrix}2&0&0\\\lambda-2&-1&0\\0&\lambda-2&3\end{pmatrix}=-6.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 2 步。** 原矩阵上三角，行列式为对角元乘积。依次取商后，只有最后一个不变多项式非常数。

**Step 2.** The original matrix is triangular, so its determinant is the product of its diagonal entries. Consecutive quotients leave only the final invariant nonconstant.

$$
\begin{gathered}
D_1=D_2=D_3=1,\quad D_4=(\lambda-2)^4,\\
\boxed{S_H=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,1,(\lambda-2)^4)}.
\end{gathered}
$$

### 8. 伴随结构 / Companion structure

**题目：** 求下列 $3\times3$ 实多项式矩阵的行列式因子与 Smith 形。展示常数子式为何足够确定前两个因子，并展开整个行列式。

**Question:** Find the determinantal divisors and Smith form of this real polynomial matrix. Use constant minors for the first two divisors and expand the full determinant.

$$
C=\begin{pmatrix}\lambda&0&2\\-1&\lambda&-1\\0&-1&\lambda\end{pmatrix}.
$$

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** 一阶子式中已有常数 $-1$，故 $D_1=1$。取第 $2,3$ 行及第 $1,2$ 列，二阶子式为 $1$，故 $D_2=1$。

**Step 1.** A constant entry forces the first gcd to one. The displayed constant second-order minor forces the second gcd to one as well.

$$
\begin{gathered}
\det\begin{pmatrix}-1&\lambda\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}=1,\\
D_1=D_2=1.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 2 步。** 沿第一行展开行列式，注意第三列的代数余子式符号为正。再由相邻商求不变多项式并写出对角矩阵。

**Step 2.** Expand along the first row, with a positive cofactor sign in column three. Then compute consecutive quotients and form the Smith matrix.

$$
\begin{gathered}
D_3=\det C=\lambda(\lambda^2-1)+2\cdot1=\lambda^3-\lambda+2,\\
\boxed{S_C=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,\lambda^3-\lambda+2)}.
\end{gathered}
$$

## 核心公式 / Core formulas

| 概念 / Concept | 公式与条件 / Formula and conditions |
| --- | --- |
| 幺模 / Unimodular | $\det U$ 为非零常数 / A nonzero constant determinant |
| 等价 / Equivalence | 同型矩阵 $B=PAQ$，$P,Q$ 幺模 / Same size, unimodular factors |
| Smith 形 / Smith form | 非零对角元首一，$d_i\mid d_{i+1}$，其余为零 / Monic divisibility chain and zero padding |
| 行列式因子 / Determinantal divisor | $D_0=1$，$D_k=d_1\cdots d_k$，$k\le r$ |
| 不变多项式 / Invariant polynomial | $d_k=D_k/D_{k-1}$，$1\le k\le r$ |
| 超过秩的子式 / Beyond rank | $k>r\Rightarrow D_k=0$，不作 $0/0$ / Do not form $0/0$ |
| 初等因子 / Elementary divisors | 各 $d_i$ 的正指数不可约因子幂，保留重数 / Positive prime powers, counted with occurrences |
| 恢复条件 / Reconstruction | 已知秩，指数升序且左侧补零 / Known rank, sorted exponents, left zero padding |
| 分块对角 / Block diagonal | 初等因子多重集合取并集 / Multiset union of elementary divisors |
| 特征矩阵 / Characteristic matrix | $X_A=\lambda I-A$，$\det X_A=\prod_{i=1}^n d_i$ |

**中文：** 自查：先分清 $D_k$、$d_k$ 与初等因子；结果须首一、满足整除链；单位因子与零块分别计数；判等价还要确认大小与秩。满秩方阵最后检查不变多项式的乘积是否等于行列式的首一化结果。

**English:** Distinguish $D_k$, $d_k$ and elementary divisors. Normalize to monic form, check the divisibility chain, and retain unit factors and zero padding separately. Equivalence also requires dimensions and rank. For a full-rank square matrix, check that the invariant product is the monic normalization of its determinant.
