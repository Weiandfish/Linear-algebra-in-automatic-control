## 统一符号 / Notation

- **中文：** 一般 Smith 理论在域 $\mathbb F$ 上的一元多项式环中成立；数值例题默认取实数或复数域。用一次因子描述所有初等因子时取复数域；一般域上改用首一不可约因子。

  **English:** Smith theory works over a univariate polynomial ring over a field $\mathbb F$. Numerical examples use the real or complex field unless stated otherwise. Linear factors describe all elementary divisors over the complex field; over a general field use monic irreducibles.

- **中文：** $D_k$ 是全部 $k$ 阶子式的首一最大公因式，称行列式因子；$D_0=1$。若秩为 $r$，规定 $k>r$ 的 $D_k=0$。不变多项式为 $d_k=D_k/D_{k-1}$，只对 $1\le k\le r$ 使用此商。

  **English:** $D_k$ is the monic gcd of the $k\times k$ minors, called a determinantal divisor; set $D_0=1$. For rank $r$, define $D_k=0$ when $k>r$. Invariant polynomials satisfy $d_k=D_k/D_{k-1}$ for $1\le k\le r$ only.

- **中文：** 多项式矩阵的秩按“子式是否恒零”判断；等价变换为 $B=PAQ$，其中 $P,Q$ 幺模。首一单位不变多项式 $1$ 要保留，初等因子则不计常数与零。

  **English:** Polynomial rank depends on minors being identically zero. Equivalence has the form $B=PAQ$ with unimodular $P,Q$. Keep unit invariant polynomials equal to one; elementary divisors omit constants and zeros.
