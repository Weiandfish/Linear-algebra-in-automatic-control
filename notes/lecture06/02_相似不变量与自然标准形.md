## 一、特征值不变，坐标随基改变 / Eigenvalues persist; coordinates change

**中文：** 若 $B=P^{-1}AP$，则 $\mu I-B=P^{-1}(\mu I-A)P$，所以特征多项式相同。若 $Ax=\rho x$，则

**English:** If $B=P^{-1}AP$, then $\mu I-B=P^{-1}(\mu I-A)P$, giving identical characteristic polynomials. If $Ax=\rho x$, then

$$
B(P^{-1}x)=P^{-1}Ax=\rho(P^{-1}x).
$$

**中文：** 因此相似矩阵的特征值及代数重数相同，特征空间通过 $P^{-1}$ 一一对应，几何重数也相同。它们的特征向量坐标一般不同；若矩阵来自同一抽象算子的两组基，则对应的抽象向量是同一个。

**English:** Similar matrices have the same eigenvalues and algebraic multiplicities. Their eigenspaces correspond bijectively through $P^{-1}$, so geometric multiplicities agree. Coordinate eigenvectors generally differ; for two bases of the same abstract operator, the corresponding abstract vector is unchanged.

## 二、用特征矩阵把相似问题接到 Smith 理论 / Connecting similarity to Smith theory

**中文：** 对同阶常数矩阵，课件定理 3.8 给出

**English:** For constant matrices of the same size, Theorem 3.8 states

$$
\boxed{A\sim B\iff\mu I-A\text{ 与 / and }\mu I-B\text{ 幺模等价 / unimodularly equivalent}}.
$$

**中文：** 正向由上一节公式直接得到：常数可逆矩阵也是幺模矩阵。反向是本定理的实质。结合第五讲，判断常数矩阵相似，等价于比较各自**特征矩阵**的完整不变多项式。

**English:** The forward direction follows immediately because constant invertible matrices are unimodular. The converse is the substantive part of the theorem. Together with Lecture 5, similarity can be tested by comparing the complete invariant lists of the characteristic matrices.

**中文：** 对 $d$ 阶 $A$，$\det(\mu I-A)$ 首一且次数为 $d$，所以特征矩阵满秩，Smith 形有 $d$ 个非零不变多项式。去掉等于 $1$ 的项，记余下各项为 $\psi_1,\ldots,\psi_\ell$，则

**English:** For $d$-by-$d$ $A$, the determinant of $\mu I-A$ is monic of degree $d$. Its Smith form thus has $d$ nonzero invariant polynomials. Discard the entries equal to one and denote the remainder by $\psi_1,\ldots,\psi_\ell$. Then

$$
\begin{gathered}
\psi_i\text{ 首一且非常数 / monic and nonconstant},\qquad \psi_i\mid\psi_{i+1},\\
\chi_A(\mu)=\prod_{i=1}^{\ell}\psi_i(\mu),\qquad
\sum_{i=1}^{\ell}\deg\psi_i=d.
\end{gathered}
$$

**中文：** 这里不是对常数矩阵 $A$ 本身计算 Smith 形。比如一个可逆常数矩阵的多项式 Smith 形总为单位矩阵，这显然无法区分它的特征值。

**English:** These invariants belong to $\mu I-A$, not to the constant matrix $A$ treated as a polynomial matrix. An invertible constant matrix itself always has identity Smith form, which would not distinguish its eigenvalues.

## 三、列伴随矩阵：一个多项式对应一个块 / Column companion matrices

**中文：** 对首一多项式 $\psi(\mu)=\mu^m+c_{m-1}\mu^{m-1}+\cdots+c_1\mu+c_0$，定义列伴随矩阵

**English:** For the monic polynomial $\psi(\mu)=\mu^m+c_{m-1}\mu^{m-1}+\cdots+c_1\mu+c_0$, define the column companion matrix

$$
C(\psi)=\begin{bmatrix}
0&0&\cdots&0&-c_0\\
1&0&\ddots&\vdots&-c_1\\
0&1&\ddots&0&\vdots\\
\vdots&\ddots&\ddots&0&-c_{m-2}\\
0&\cdots&0&1&-c_{m-1}
\end{bmatrix}\in\mathbb F^{m\times m}.
$$

**中文：** 若 $m=1$，就是一阶矩阵 $[-c_0]$。次对角线是 $1$，最后一列是系数取负；系数按常数项到最高次前一项排列，不要倒序。

**English:** For $m=1$, this is the one-by-one matrix $[-c_0]$. Ones lie on the subdiagonal and the final column contains negated coefficients, ordered from the constant term upward.

**中文：** 其特征矩阵正是第四次作业第 3 题的结构：有常数子式 $(-1)^k$，行列式为 $\psi$，因此

**English:** Its characteristic matrix has the structure of Homework 4, Problem 3: constant minors $(-1)^k$ and determinant $\psi$. Consequently,

$$
\det(\mu I-C(\psi))=\psi(\mu),\qquad
S_{\mu I-C(\psi)}=\operatorname{diag}(I_{m-1},\psi).
$$

**教学补充例题 / Teaching supplement：**

$$
\psi(\mu)=\mu^3+2\mu^2-\mu+4
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
C(\psi)=\begin{bmatrix}0&0&-4\\1&0&1\\0&1&-2\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** 最后一列来自 $-(4,-1,2)^T$；该三阶块只对应一个非常数不变多项式 $\psi$。

**English:** The last column is $-(4,-1,2)^T$. This three-by-three block has exactly one nonconstant invariant polynomial, namely $\psi$.

## 四、自然标准形的构造 / Constructing natural normal form

**中文：** 自然标准形也称有理标准形，不要求特征多项式在标量域中分解成一次因子。课件定理 3.9 给出

**English:** The natural normal form, also called rational canonical form, does not require the characteristic polynomial to split into linear factors. Theorem 3.9 gives

$$
\boxed{A\sim N=\operatorname{diag}\bigl(C(\psi_1),\ldots,C(\psi_\ell)\bigr)}.
$$

1. **中文：** 先对 $\mu I-A$ 求 Smith 形，去掉单位项 $1$；令 $m_i=\deg\psi_i$，检查 $\sum_i m_i=d$。

   **English:** Find the Smith form of $\mu I-A$ and remove unit entries. Set $m_i=\deg\psi_i$ and check that their sum is $d$.

2. **中文：** 给每个 $\psi_i$ 建立大小为 $m_i$ 的列伴随块，再按整除链的顺序放到块对角线上。

   **English:** Build a column companion block of size $m_i$ for each $\psi_i$ and place the blocks in divisibility order.

3. **中文：** 第 22-24 页的证明把单位项重新分组，得到 $\mu I-A$ 等价于各 $\operatorname{diag}(I_{m_i-1},\psi_i)$ 的分块直和；每块又等价于 $\mu I-C(\psi_i)$。所以 $\mu I-A$ 与 $\mu I-N$ 等价，再由定理 3.8 得 $A\sim N$。

   **English:** Slides 22-24 regroup the unit entries so that $\mu I-A$ is equivalent to the block sum of $\operatorname{diag}(I_{m_i-1},\psi_i)$. Each block is equivalent to $\mu I-C(\psi_i)$. Thus $\mu I-A$ and $\mu I-N$ are equivalent, and Theorem 3.8 yields similarity.

**中文：** Smith 形是多项式矩阵的等价标准形；自然标准形是常数矩阵的相似标准形。若还要求换基矩阵，要寻找常数可逆 $P$ 并检查 $AP=PN$。Smith 运算得到的左右幺模矩阵可能依赖 $\mu$，不能直接当作这个 $P$。

**English:** Smith form classifies polynomial matrices under equivalence; natural normal form classifies constant matrices under similarity. To find a basis change as well, seek a constant invertible $P$ and verify $AP=PN$. The polynomial factors from a Smith reduction can depend on $\mu$ and cannot simply be used as that $P$.

## 五、完整例题：标准形与换基矩阵 / Worked example: form and basis change

**教学补充例题 / Teaching supplement：**

$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{bmatrix},\qquad
\mu I-A=\begin{bmatrix}\mu-1&-1\\0&\mu-1\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** 存在常数元素 $-1$，故 $D_1=1$；行列式为 $(\mu-1)^2$。只有一个非常数不变多项式 $\psi=(\mu-1)^2=\mu^2-2\mu+1$，所以

**English:** The constant entry $-1$ gives $D_1=1$ and the determinant is $(\mu-1)^2$. The single nonconstant invariant polynomial is $\psi=(\mu-1)^2=\mu^2-2\mu+1$, hence

$$
N=C(\psi)=\begin{bmatrix}0&-1\\1&2\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** 选 $v=(0,1)^T$，则 $Av=(1,1)^T$，二者独立。取它们为新基，得到

**English:** Choose $v=(0,1)^T$. Then $Av=(1,1)^T$, and these vectors are independent. Taking them as the new basis gives

$$
P=(v,Av)=\begin{bmatrix}0&1\\1&1\end{bmatrix},\qquad
AP=PN=\begin{bmatrix}1&2\\1&1\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** 因此 $P^{-1}AP=N$。原矩阵的特征向量 $e_1$ 在新基下的坐标是 $P^{-1}e_1=(-1,1)^T$，确有 $N(-1,1)^T=(-1,1)^T$。坐标改变，抽象向量仍是 $e_1$。

**English:** Thus $P^{-1}AP=N$. The old eigenvector $e_1$ has new coordinates $P^{-1}e_1=(-1,1)^T$, and indeed $N(-1,1)^T=(-1,1)^T$. The coordinates change while the abstract vector remains $e_1$.

## 六、作业中的微分：一个循环块 / Differentiation: one cyclic block

**作业例题：第五次作业第 4 题 / Homework example: Problem 4.** 在 $\mathbb C_n[s]$ 上令 $D(f)=f'$。 / Let $D(f)=f'$ on $\mathbb C_n[s]$.

**中文：** 基 $(1,s,\ldots,s^n)$ 下的矩阵 $N_D$ 只有 $N_{j-1,j}=j$ 非零，指标从 $0$ 开始。$\mu I-N_D$ 为上双对角矩阵。前 $k$ 行和第 $2$ 至 $k+1$ 列（这里行列按通常的 $1$ 起始数数）组成的子式为 $(-1)^k k!\ne0$，因此

**English:** In the basis $(1,s,\ldots,s^n)$, the only nonzero entries are $N_{j-1,j}=j$, with indices starting at zero. The matrix $\mu I-N_D$ is upper bidiagonal. Counting rows and columns from one, its first $k$ rows and columns two through $k+1$ give the nonzero constant minor $(-1)^k k!$. Thus

$$
D_1=\cdots=D_n=1,\qquad D_{n+1}=\mu^{n+1},\qquad
S_{\mu I-N_D}=\operatorname{diag}(I_n,\mu^{n+1}).
$$

**中文：** 唯一初等因子是 $\mu^{n+1}$；它不是 $n+1$ 个分开的 $\mu$。唯一特征值为 $0$，几何重数为 $1$，全部特征向量为非零常数多项式。

**English:** The sole elementary divisor is $\mu^{n+1}$, rather than $n+1$ separate copies of $\mu$. Zero is the only eigenvalue, its geometric multiplicity is one, and all eigenvectors are nonzero constant polynomials.

**教学补充：直接找到自然标准形的基 / Teaching supplement: a direct companion basis.**

$$
\mathcal B=\left(\frac{s^n}{n!},\frac{s^{n-1}}{(n-1)!},\ldots,s,1\right).
$$

**中文：** 求导将每个基向量送到下一个，最后一个送到零。因此在这个逆序归一化基下，矩阵次对角线全为 $1$，其余为零，正是 $C(\mu^{n+1})$。这也直接显示 $D^{n+1}=0$，且 $D^n(s^n)=n!\ne0$。

**English:** Differentiation sends each basis vector to the next and the final one to zero. Its matrix in this reversed normalized basis has ones on the subdiagonal and zeros elsewhere, exactly $C(\mu^{n+1})$. It also directly shows $D^{n+1}=0$ but $D^n(s^n)=n!\ne0$.

## 七、衔接第五讲：分块初等因子 / Connecting back to block elementary divisors

**作业例题：第五次作业第 1 题 / Homework example: Problem 1.**

$$
B(\lambda)=\begin{bmatrix}\lambda^3+\lambda&\lambda^2\\\lambda^2-3\lambda&2\lambda\end{bmatrix}.
$$

$$
C(\lambda)=\begin{bmatrix}
1-\lambda&\lambda^2&\lambda\\
2\lambda-1&\lambda(1-\lambda)&-2\lambda\\
1+\lambda^2&\lambda^2&-\lambda^2
\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** 元素的最大公因式为 $\lambda$，行列式为 $\lambda^2(\lambda+1)(\lambda+2)$，故 $S_B=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda,\lambda(\lambda+1)(\lambda+2))$。作业中的 $C(\lambda)$ 是前次作业已经算过的三阶矩阵，其 Smith 形为 $\operatorname{diag}(1,\lambda,\lambda(\lambda+1))$。

**English:** The entry gcd is $\lambda$ and the determinant is $\lambda^2(\lambda+1)(\lambda+2)$, so $S_B=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda,\lambda(\lambda+1)(\lambda+2))$. The homework's three-by-three $C(\lambda)$ is the previous homework matrix with Smith form $\operatorname{diag}(1,\lambda,\lambda(\lambda+1))$.

**中文：** $\operatorname{diag}(B,C)$ 的初等因子按重数取并集：$\lambda$ 四个，$\lambda+1$ 两个，$\lambda+2$ 一个。不要把相同因子先合并成一个高次幂。

**English:** Take a multiset union for $\operatorname{diag}(B,C)$: four copies of $\lambda$, two of $\lambda+1$ and one of $\lambda+2$. Do not merge equal entries into a single higher power.

**中文：** 本题对象已经是多项式矩阵，直接用其 Smith 形。第 4 题对象是线性算子，先取特征矩阵。两种初等因子的计算对象不同。

**English:** Problem 1 already gives a polynomial matrix, so use its Smith form directly. Problem 4 gives a linear operator, so first form its characteristic matrix. These are different starting objects for computing elementary divisors.
