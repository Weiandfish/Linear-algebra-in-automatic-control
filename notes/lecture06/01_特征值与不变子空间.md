## 一、同一个算子，不同的坐标矩阵 / One operator, different coordinate matrices

**中文：** 设 $T:V\to V$，基 $B=(b_1,\ldots,b_d)$。矩阵的第 $j$ 列是 $T(b_j)$ 的坐标，因此

**English:** For $T:V\to V$ and basis $B=(b_1,\ldots,b_d)$, column $j$ records the coordinates of $T(b_j)$. Thus

$$
T(B)=BA,\qquad [T(v)]_B=A[v]_B.
$$

**中文：** 若新基 $C=BP$，其中 $P$ 为常数可逆矩阵，则

**English:** If the new basis is $C=BP$ for a constant invertible matrix $P$, then

$$
[v]_B=P[v]_C,\qquad A_C=P^{-1}A_BP.
$$

**中文：** 这是相似变换。输入、输出是同一空间并同时换基，所以左右矩阵不能独立选择。

**English:** This is similarity. The same basis change is used for both input and output, so the left and right factors are linked.

## 二、不变子空间：像仍留在里面 / Invariant subspaces

**中文：** 子空间 $W\subseteq V$ 满足 $T(W)\subseteq W$ 时，称其为 $T$-不变。只要求包含，不要求 $T(W)=W$。若 $W$ 的基为 $\beta_1,\ldots,\beta_m$，只需检查每个 $T(\beta_j)$ 都属于 $W$。

**English:** A subspace $W$ is $T$-invariant when $T(W)\subseteq W$. Equality is not required. For a basis $\beta_1,\ldots,\beta_m$ of $W$, it suffices to check that each $T(\beta_j)$ belongs to $W$.

$$
T(\beta_1,\ldots,\beta_m)
=(\beta_1,\ldots,\beta_m)A_W.
$$

**中文：** 将该基扩充为整个 $V$ 的基，矩阵具有块上三角结构，左下角为零；若补空间也不变，右上角也为零，才得到块对角结构。

**English:** Extend this basis to one of $V$. The matrix is block upper triangular, with zero lower-left block. If the chosen complement is also invariant, the upper-right block vanishes as well and the matrix becomes block diagonal.

$$
[T]=\begin{bmatrix}A_W&*\\0&A_{22}\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**教学补充例题 / Teaching supplement：** 在 $V=\mathbb C_2[t]$ 上令 $D(f)=f'$，取 $W=\operatorname{span}\{1,t\}$。 / Let $D(f)=f'$ on $V=\mathbb C_2[t]$ and take $W=\operatorname{span}\{1,t\}$.

$$
D(1)=0,\quad D(t)=1,\quad D(W)=\operatorname{span}\{1\}\subsetneq W.
$$

**中文：** 因而 $W$ 不变；但 $D$ 的零特征空间只有常数多项式，$W$ 本身不是特征空间。这是第 7 页措辞需要保留的区别。

**English:** Hence $W$ is invariant, but the zero eigenspace consists only of constants, so $W$ is not an eigenspace. This distinguishes the notions discussed on slide 7.

## 三、特征值、特征向量与特征空间 / Eigenvalues, eigenvectors and eigenspaces

**中文：** 若 $T(v)=\rho v$ 且 $v\ne0$，称 $\rho$ 为特征值、$v$ 为相应特征向量。特征值可以为零。特征空间是核，包含零向量；特征向量则必须排除零向量。

**English:** If $T(v)=\rho v$ with $v\ne0$, then $\rho$ is an eigenvalue and $v$ an associated eigenvector. Zero is a valid eigenvalue. The eigenspace is a kernel and includes zero, while eigenvectors must be nonzero.

$$
E_\rho(T)=\ker(T-\rho I),\qquad
v\text{ 为特征向量 / eigenvector}\iff v\in E_\rho(T)\setminus\{0\}.
$$

**中文：** 对有限维矩阵 $A=[T]_B$，先解特征方程，再求齐次方程的非零解，最后把坐标还原成空间中的向量：

**English:** For finite-dimensional $A=[T]_B$, solve the characteristic equation, find nonzero solutions of the homogeneous system, then convert coordinates back into vectors:

$$
\chi_A(\mu)=\det(\mu I-A),\qquad
\chi_A(\rho)=0,\qquad (A-\rho I)x=0,\qquad v=Bx.
$$

**中文：** 每个特征空间都不变，因为其中每个向量的像都是它的 $\rho$ 倍。单个特征向量还生成一维不变子空间。一般域上特征方程可能没有根，例如实矩阵旋转 $90$ 度时没有实特征值；在复数域则有 $i,-i$。

**English:** Every eigenspace is invariant because $T$ acts there as multiplication by $\rho$. A single eigenvector spans a one-dimensional invariant subspace. Over a general field the characteristic equation need not have a root: a real ninety-degree rotation has no real eigenvalues but has complex eigenvalues $i,-i$.

## 四、代数重数与几何重数 / Algebraic and geometric multiplicities

**中文：** 代数重数 $m_\rho$ 是 $\rho$ 作为特征多项式根的重数；几何重数 $g_\rho$ 是特征空间的维数。

**English:** The algebraic multiplicity $m_\rho$ is the root multiplicity in the characteristic polynomial. The geometric multiplicity $g_\rho$ is the dimension of the eigenspace.

$$
g_\rho=\dim E_\rho(T)=d-\operatorname{rank}(A-\rho I),\qquad
1\le g_\rho\le m_\rho.
$$

**中文：** 后一不等式的教学补充证明：把特征空间的一组基扩充为 $V$ 的基，矩阵左上块为 $\rho I_{g_\rho}$、左下块为零，所以特征多项式至少含 $(\mu-\rho)^{g_\rho}$。

**English:** A supplementary proof of the inequality extends an eigenspace basis to a basis of $V$. The upper-left block is $\rho I_{g_\rho}$ and the lower-left block is zero, so the characteristic polynomial contains at least $(\mu-\rho)^{g_\rho}$.

**教学补充例题 / Teaching supplement：**

$$
D\big|_{\mathbb C_2[t]}\ \longleftrightarrow\
N=\begin{bmatrix}0&1&0\\0&0&2\\0&0&0\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** $\chi_N(\mu)=\mu^3$，唯一特征值为 $0$，代数重数为 $3$。$\ker N=\operatorname{span}\{(1,0,0)^T\}$，几何重数为 $1$，对应抽象向量是常数多项式。三重根并不提供三个无关特征向量。

**English:** Its characteristic polynomial is $\mu^3$, so zero has algebraic multiplicity three. The kernel is spanned by $(1,0,0)^T$, giving geometric multiplicity one and corresponding to constant polynomials. A triple root need not supply three independent eigenvectors.

## 五、不同特征空间的和是直和 / Distinct eigenspaces form a direct sum

**中文：** 对不同特征值 $\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_s$，各取特征空间的一组基，合起来仍线性无关，因此

**English:** For distinct eigenvalues $\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_s$, concatenate bases of their eigenspaces. The combined vectors remain independent, so

$$
E_{\rho_1}(T)+\cdots+E_{\rho_s}(T)
=E_{\rho_1}(T)\oplus\cdots\oplus E_{\rho_s}(T).
$$

**中文：** 教学补充证明：若 $\sum_j v_j=0$、$v_j\in E_{\rho_j}(T)$，对等式作用 $\prod_{j\ne k}(T-\rho_jI)$，只剩 $\prod_{j\ne k}(\rho_k-\rho_j)v_k=0$。系数非零，故每个 $v_k=0$。

**English:** For a supplementary proof, apply $\prod_{j\ne k}(T-\rho_jI)$ to $\sum_jv_j=0$, where $v_j\in E_{\rho_j}(T)$. Only $\prod_{j\ne k}(\rho_k-\rho_j)v_k=0$ remains. Its scalar coefficient is nonzero, so every $v_k$ vanishes.

**中文：** 直和未必等于整个空间。能选到特征向量基，当且仅当 $\sum_\rho g_\rho=d$。在复数域上，这也等价于每个特征值都满足 $g_\rho=m_\rho$，即算子可对角化。

**English:** The direct sum need not fill the whole space. An eigenvector basis exists exactly when $\sum_\rho g_\rho=d$. Over the complex field this is equivalent to $g_\rho=m_\rho$ at every eigenvalue, that is, diagonalizability.

## 六、作业中的微分与平移 / Differentiation and translation in the homework

**作业例题一：全体多项式上的微分 / Homework example 1: differentiation on all polynomials.**

**中文：** $D:\mathbb C[t]\to\mathbb C[t]$ 由求导定义。$D(uf+vg)=uD(f)+vD(g)$，故线性；$D(f)=0$ 当且仅当正次项系数全为零，故 $\ker D=\operatorname{span}\{1\}$。这个空间是无限维的，本题不需要有限阶特征矩阵。

**English:** Differentiation on $\mathbb C[t]$ is linear since $D(uf+vg)=uD(f)+vD(g)$. Its kernel is the constant polynomials. This space is infinite-dimensional; this problem does not require a finite characteristic matrix.

**作业例题二：有限维空间上的平移 / Homework example 2: finite-dimensional translation.** 令 $T_a(f)(t)=f(t+a)$，$a\ne0$。 / Let $T_a(f)(t)=f(t+a)$, with $a\ne0$.

$$
(t+a)^j=\sum_{i=0}^j\binom ji a^{j-i}t^i,\qquad
[T_a]_{ij}=\begin{cases}\binom ji a^{j-i},&0\le i\le j\le n,\\0,&i>j.\end{cases}
$$

**中文：** 行列从 $0$ 编号，因为基从 $1=t^0$ 开始。例如 $n=2$ 时

**English:** Indices start at zero because the basis begins with $1=t^0$. For $n=2$,

$$
[T_a]_{(1,t,t^2)}=\begin{bmatrix}1&a&a^2\\0&1&2a\\0&0&1\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** 对角元全为 $1$，所以唯一特征值为 $1$。若 $\deg f=m\ge1$、首项系数为 $c_m$，则 $f(t+a)-f(t)$ 的首项为 $mc_mat^{m-1}\ne0$，故平移不动的多项式只能为常数。因此 $E_1(T_a)=\operatorname{span}\{1\}$；代数重数为 $n+1$，几何重数为 $1$。

**English:** All diagonal entries are one, so one is the only eigenvalue. For a degree-$m\ge1$ polynomial with leading coefficient $c_m$, the leading term of $f(t+a)-f(t)$ is $mc_mat^{m-1}\ne0$. Thus only constants are fixed, giving $E_1(T_a)=\operatorname{span}\{1\}$, algebraic multiplicity $n+1$ and geometric multiplicity one.

## 七、讲义中的复合与幂 / Composition and powers in the lecture

**中文：** 第 19 页的计算说明，固定同一个基后，$T^k$ 的矩阵就是 $A^k$。若 $T(v)=\rho v$，归纳得到 $T^k(v)=\rho^k v$（$k\ge1$）。因此相同特征向量可用于计算反复作用。

**English:** Slide 19 shows that, in a fixed basis, the matrix of $T^k$ is $A^k$. If $T(v)=\rho v$, induction gives $T^k(v)=\rho^k v$ for $k\ge1$. The same eigenvector therefore simplifies repeated application.

$$
v=\sum_jc_jv_j,\quad T(v_j)=\rho_jv_j
\ \Longrightarrow\ T^k(v)=\sum_jc_j\rho_j^k v_j.
$$

**中文：** 这条展开只适用于已经写成特征向量组合的向量；没有特征向量基时，不能默认每个向量都能这样分解。

**English:** This expansion applies to vectors already expressed as eigenvector combinations. Without an eigenvector basis, not every vector admits such a decomposition.
