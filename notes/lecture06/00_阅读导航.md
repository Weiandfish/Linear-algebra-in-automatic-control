## 符号与范围 / Notation and scope

- **中文：** $T$ 表示线性算子，$A$ 表示它在某个基下的常数矩阵。有限维空间的维数写作 $d$；作业中的 $\mathbb C_n[t]$ 是次数不超过 $n$ 的空间，维数为 $n+1$。

  **English:** $T$ is a linear operator and $A$ its constant matrix in a chosen basis. Use $d$ for a finite dimension; the homework's $\mathbb C_n[t]$ consists of polynomials of degree at most $n$ and has dimension $n+1$.

- **中文：** $\rho$ 是特征值，$\mu$ 是特征矩阵 $\mu I-A$ 中的不定元，$t$ 或 $s$ 是被作用的多项式的变量。不要把作用对象的变量与谱参数混在一起。

  **English:** $\rho$ denotes an eigenvalue, $\mu$ the indeterminate in $\mu I-A$, and $t$ or $s$ the variable of the polynomial on which the operator acts. Distinguish a polynomial's input variable from the spectral parameter.

- **中文：** 第五次作业第 2 题的 $\mathbb C[\lambda]$ 是全体复系数多项式空间，没有统一的次数上界；第 3、4 题则是有限维空间。具体算例取实数或复数域，一般相似与自然标准形理论在域 $\mathbb F$ 上成立。

  **English:** Homework 5, Problem 2 uses all of $\mathbb C[\lambda]$, with no common degree bound. Problems 3 and 4 use finite-dimensional spaces. Concrete examples use real or complex scalars; similarity and natural normal form work over a general field $\mathbb F$.

## 需要讲清的条件 / Conditions and clarifications

**中文：** 第 7 页把不变子空间与特征空间联系起来，但不能理解成二者相同：每个特征空间都不变，反过来不成立。第 10 页展开的齐次方程末行，对角项应为 $+(\rho-a_{dd})x_d$。第 17—18 页说“特征向量与基无关”，指抽象向量；坐标列随换基变成 $P^{-1}x$。

**English:** Slide 7 relates invariant subspaces to eigenspaces, but they are not the same notion: every eigenspace is invariant, while the converse fails. The last diagonal term in the expanded system on slide 10 should be $+(\rho-a_{dd})x_d$. The basis-independence statement on slides 17-18 concerns abstract eigenvectors; their coordinate columns change to $P^{-1}x$.

**中文：** 第 21 页自然标准形中的不变多项式，来自 $\mu I-A$，不是对常数矩阵 $A$ 本身作多项式 Smith 化。伴随块的大小应为该不变多项式的次数 $n_i$；第 21、23 页出现的未定义大小符号统一按 $n_i\times n_i$ 理解。课件对教材第 88、96 页的引用只作为阅读线索；这里新增的证明、数值例题标为教学补充。

**English:** In slide 21, the invariant polynomials for natural normal form belong to $\mu I-A$, not to a polynomial Smith reduction of the constant matrix $A$ itself. Each companion block has size equal to the degree $n_i$ of its polynomial; use $n_i\times n_i$ for the undefined size notation on slides 21 and 23. References to textbook pages 88 and 96 remain reading pointers. Added proofs and numerical examples are marked as teaching supplements.
