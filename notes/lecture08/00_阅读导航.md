## 统一符号与必要条件 / Notation and conditions

- **中文：** 用 $T$ 表示算子，$A$ 表示其常数坐标矩阵，$z$ 表示零化多项式中的不定元。作业里被平移的多项式变量暂写作 $s$，与原题的 $\lambda$ 只是重命名。

  **English:** Use $T$ for an operator, $A$ for its constant coordinate matrix and $z$ for the annihilating-polynomial indeterminate. Write $s$ for the variable being translated; this only renames the homework's $\lambda$.

- **中文：** $m_v$、$m_W$、$m_T$ 分别表示向量处、子空间处和全空间上的最小多项式。均取非零首一形式；规定 $m_0=1$、$m_{\{0\}}=1$。零多项式可以零化，但不能作为最小多项式。

  **English:** The symbols $m_v,m_W,m_T$ distinguish a vector, a subspace and the whole space. Normalize each to be nonzero and monic; set $m_0=m_{\{0\}}=1$. The zero polynomial annihilates but is not a minimal polynomial.

- **中文：** 子空间处的零化条件不要求子空间不变；讨论限制算子、第一分解与第二分解时，需要相应子空间不变。有限维时最小多项式必存在；第 7 次作业第 1 题只需使用题目已给定的最小多项式。

  **English:** Annihilation on a subspace does not itself require invariance. Restrictions and the decomposition theorems do. Existence is automatic in finite dimension; Homework 7, Problem 1 already assumes the relevant minimal polynomial exists.

- **中文：** $\mathbb C_n[s]$ 的维数为 $n+1$。平移步长 $a\ne0$，并取复数域，保证阶乘和 $a$ 可逆。实或复矩阵的具体计算按题设进行。

  **English:** The dimension of $\mathbb C_n[s]$ is $n+1$. Translation uses nonzero $a$ over the complex field, so factorials and $a$ are invertible. Concrete matrices use the real or complex setting of the homework.

## 讲义勘误与阅读线索 / Clarifications and reading pointers

**中文：** 第 5 页的低次多项式推论要排除零多项式。最小多项式唯一性需要首一化。第 15、21 页直和式中重复的第二个 $W_1$ 应为 $W_2$；第 21 页坐标基中重复的第二个 $\alpha_1$ 应为 $\alpha_2$，标题“3.52”按第 3.5.2 节理解。

**English:** The low-degree corollary on slide 5 must exclude the zero polynomial. Uniqueness requires monic normalization. In the direct sums on slides 15 and 21, the second repeated $W_1$ should be $W_2$. On slide 21, the repeated second basis vector should be $\alpha_2$, and “3.52” means Section 3.5.2.

**中文：** 第 16 页最小多项式公式中的分母，本笔记明确写为特征矩阵的 $D_{d-1}$，其中 $d$ 是矩阵阶数、$D_k$ 是首一行列式因子，$D_0=1$。第 22—23 页只给出广义 Vandermonde 矩阵的关系，未给完整通式；笔记另给可直接验证的二阶例子，不冒充教材原例。

**English:** The denominator in slide 16 is expressed here unambiguously as the characteristic matrix's determinantal divisor $D_{d-1}$, with $d$ the matrix order and $D_0=1$. Slides 22-23 state a relation involving a generalized Vandermonde matrix without giving its full formula. The notes supply a separately labeled, verifiable two-dimensional example.

**中文：** 课件引用教材例 2.8 及第 117—129 页的证明或例子，没有完整复现数值题设。本套保留这些线索，新增计算标为教学补充或作业例题。

**English:** The slides refer to textbook Example 2.8 and proofs or examples on pages 117-129 without reproducing their numerical statements. These remain reading pointers; added calculations are labeled as teaching supplements or homework examples.
