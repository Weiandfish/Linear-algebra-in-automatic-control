### 1. 向量处与全空间 / A vector and the whole space

**题目：** 在 $\mathbb C^2$ 标准基 $e_1,e_2$ 中，$T=\operatorname{diag}(1,2)$。向量处最小多项式 $m_v(z)$ 是使 $m_v(T)v=0$ 的最低次数首一多项式；整体 $m_T$ 要对所有向量成立。分别求 $m_{e_1},m_{e_1+e_2},m_T$。

**Question:** For $T=\operatorname{diag}(1,2)$ on complex two-space, find the monic minimal annihilators at $e_1$ and $e_1+e_2$ and on the whole space. A local annihilator only needs to kill the stated vector.

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** 对角矩阵代入多项式后，每个对角元分别代入该多项式。先作用到 $e_1$，只有第一个分量需要为零。

**Step 1.** Polynomial evaluation of a diagonal matrix evaluates each diagonal entry. At $e_1$, only the first coordinate must vanish.

$$
\begin{gathered}
p(T)=\operatorname{diag}(p(1),p(2)),\quad p(T)e_1=p(1)e_1,\\
\boxed{m_{e_1}(z)=z-1}.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 2 步。** 对 $e_1+e_2$，两个独立方向的系数都必须为零，因此多项式必须同时含两个不同根。一次多项式不可能同时满足，二次乘积即可。

**Step 2.** At the sum of the two basis vectors, both independent coefficients must vanish. The two distinct roots require their product, and no linear monic polynomial suffices.

$$
\begin{gathered}
p(T)(e_1+e_2)=p(1)e_1+p(2)e_2=0\iff p(1)=p(2)=0,\\
\boxed{m_{e_1+e_2}=(z-1)(z-2)}.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 3 步。** 整体零化要同时零化两个基向量，条件仍是两个代入值都为零，所以整体最小多项式与上一个向量处的结果相同。

**Step 3.** An operator annihilator must kill both basis vectors, giving the same two-root condition as for their sum.

$$
\boxed{m_T(z)=(z-1)(z-2)}.
$$

### 2. 分块矩阵不能总取乘积 / A block lcm is not always a product

**题目：** 复矩阵 $A$ 由一个二阶 Jordan 块和两个一阶块组成，如下。求 $m_A(z)$ 与 $\chi_A(z)=\det(zI-A)$，说明重复出现的根为何不能一律把指数相加。

**Question:** Find the minimal and characteristic polynomials of this complex block matrix. Explain why repeated-root exponents are combined differently in the two polynomials.

$$
\begin{gathered}
A=\operatorname{diag}(J_2(1),[1],[-2]),\\
J_2(1)=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.
\end{gathered}
$$

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** 二阶块减去 $I_2$ 后非零但平方为零，因此其最小多项式是 $(z-1)^2$。两个一阶块分别需要 $z-1$、$z+2$。

**Step 1.** Subtracting the identity from the size-two block gives a nonzero square-zero matrix. The scalar blocks need their corresponding linear factors.

$$
J_2(1)-I_2=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}\ne0,\quad (J_2(1)-I_2)^2=0.
$$

**第 2 步。** 零化整个分块矩阵必须同时零化每块，所以取最小公倍式：同一根取最大指数。特征多项式来自行列式乘积，同一根的指数要相加。

**Step 2.** A block annihilator must kill every block, hence uses their lcm and the maximum exponent per root. Determinants multiply, adding characteristic exponents.

$$
\begin{gathered}
\boxed{m_A=\operatorname{lcm}((z-1)^2,z-1,z+2)=(z-1)^2(z+2)},\\
\boxed{\chi_A=(z-1)^2(z-1)(z+2)=(z-1)^3(z+2)}.
\end{gathered}
$$

### 3. 指数与广义特征空间维数 / Exponents and primary dimensions

**题目：** 某复矩阵的全部 Jordan 块如下：根 $-3$ 的块长为 $1,1,2$，根 $-2+5i$ 和 $-2-5i$ 处各为 $1,2$。求整体维数、最小多项式与三个广义特征空间维数，并解释 $G_\rho=\ker(A-\rho I)^2$ 中的指数。

**Question:** A complex matrix has exactly the listed Jordan blocks at three roots. Find the total dimension, minimal polynomial and three generalized-eigenspace dimensions, explaining why a square power suffices for each kernel.

$$
\begin{gathered}
-3:(1,1,2),\\
-2+5i:(1,2),\\
-2-5i:(1,2).
\end{gathered}
$$

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** 整体维数把所有块占据的维数相加；某个根的广义特征空间收集该根的全部块，也要相加。

**Step 1.** Sum all block sizes for total dimension, and sum only the blocks at one root for its generalized-eigenspace dimension.

$$
\dim G_{-3}=1+1+2=4,\quad \dim G_{-2+5i}=1+2=3,\quad \dim G_{-2-5i}=1+2=3,\quad n=4+3+3=10.
$$

**第 2 步。** 最小多项式在每个根处只取最大块长，这里均为二。将共轭因子乘在一起可写成实系数形式。

**Step 2.** The minimal polynomial uses only the largest size at each root, here two. Pair the conjugate factors to obtain a real-coefficient expression.

$$
\boxed{m_A(z)=(z+3)^2(z+2-5i)^2(z+2+5i)^2=(z+3)^2[(z+2)^2+25]^2}.
$$

**第 3 步。** 在根 $\rho$ 的块上，$(A-\rho I)^2$ 已全部变零；其余根的块减去 $\rho I$ 后，对角元都是非零的“其他根减去 $\rho$”，所以三角行列式非零、矩阵可逆，其核不会包含那些方向。指数二表示最多需要作用两次，并非空间只有二维。

**Step 3.** The square kills all blocks at the chosen root. On every other-root block the shifted triangular matrix has nonzero diagonal, hence nonzero determinant and no kernel. The exponent counts applications rather than dimension.

$$
G_\rho=\ker(A-\rho I)^2\quad \text{for each listed root}.
$$

### 4. 直接检验最小性 / Checking minimality directly

**题目：** 对 $A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\0&2\end{pmatrix}\in\mathbb C^{2\times2}$，直接证明最低次数首一零化多项式为 $(z-2)^2$。既要验证能零化，也要排除次数更低的可能。

**Question:** Directly prove that the displayed matrix has minimal polynomial $(z-2)^2$, checking annihilation and excluding lower-degree monic annihilators.

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\0&2\end{pmatrix}.
$$

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** 先减去对应特征值的单位矩阵并计算平方，得到一个二次首一零化多项式。

**Step 1.** Subtract twice the identity and square the result to obtain a monic quadratic annihilator.

$$
\begin{gathered}
N=A-2I_2=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}\ne0,\\
N^2=0.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 2 步。** 零次首一多项式只有 $1$，代入后是 $I_2\ne0$。一次首一多项式形如 $z-c$；若它零化，则 $A=cI_2$，但 $A$ 的超对角元为 $1$，无法成立。

**Step 2.** The only degree-zero monic polynomial is one and evaluates to a nonzero identity. A linear monic annihilator would make the matrix a scalar identity, impossible because of the off-diagonal one.

$$
\begin{gathered}
1(A)=I_2\ne0,\\
(z-c)(A)=A-cI_2=\begin{pmatrix}2-c&1\\0&2-c\end{pmatrix}\ne0\quad (\forall c\in\mathbb C).
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 3 步。** 已找到二次零化多项式，且所有更低次数都失败，因此它就是最小多项式。

**Step 3.** The quadratic works and every smaller degree fails, proving minimality.

$$
\boxed{m_A(z)=(z-2)^2}.
$$

### 5. 判断循环向量 / Testing a cyclic vector

**题目：** 在 $\mathbb C^3$ 中，向量 $w$ 的循环子空间为 $\mathcal S_A[w]=\operatorname{span}\{w,Aw,A^2w,\ldots\}$。对下列 $A,v$ 判断 $v$ 与标准基向量 $e_1$ 能否分别生成整个空间，并求各自的最小多项式。

**Question:** For the displayed matrix and vector, test whether each of $v,e_1$ is cyclic on complex three-space and find their local minimal polynomials. The cyclic subspace is the span of successive matrix iterates.

$$
\begin{gathered}
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1&0\\0&1&0\\0&0&2\end{pmatrix},\\
v=(0,1,1)^T.
\end{gathered}
$$

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** 三维空间中若前三个迭代向量已独立，就张成全空间。先实际相乘算出它们，再组成列矩阵并检查行列式。

**Step 1.** In dimension three, independence of the first three iterates proves full spanning. Compute the iterates and the determinant of their column matrix.

$$
\begin{gathered}
Av=(1,1,2)^T,\quad A^2v=(2,1,4)^T,\quad P=(v,Av,A^2v)=\begin{pmatrix}0&1&2\\1&1&1\\1&2&4\end{pmatrix},\\
\det P=-3+2=-1\ne0.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 2 步。** 因此 $v$ 循环，且不存在二次以下的非零多项式关系。矩阵的两个块要求 $(z-1)^2$ 和 $z-2$，其乘积可以零化整个矩阵，故得到次数三的局部最小多项式。

**Step 2.** Thus no polynomial relation of degree below three kills $v$. The block annihilator of degree three kills the whole matrix, fixing the local minimal polynomial.

$$
\begin{gathered}
\mathcal S_A[v]=\mathbb C^3,\\
\boxed{m_v=m_A=(z-1)^2(z-2)}.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 3 步。** 而 $e_1$ 每次乘 $A$ 都不变，所有迭代仍在同一条线上，只能生成一维。

**Step 3.** The first standard basis vector is fixed by every iterate, so its cyclic subspace stays one-dimensional.

$$
\begin{gathered}
Ae_1=e_1,\quad \mathcal S_A[e_1]=\operatorname{span}\{e_1\}\ne\mathbb C^3,\\
\boxed{m_{e_1}=z-1}.
\end{gathered}
$$

### 6. 实际构造平移的 Jordan 基 / Constructing a translation Jordan basis

**题目：** 在 $V=\mathbb C_2[s]$ 上定义 $Tf(s)=f(s+2)$。旧基为 $E=(1,s,s^2)$，$A=[T]_E$。构造新基 $F=(f_0,f_1,f_2)$ 及其旧坐标矩阵 $X$，使 $X^{-1}AX=J_3(1)$；验证链关系和可逆性。

**Question:** For translation by two on quadratic complex polynomials, construct a Jordan basis and its coordinate matrix relative to $(1,s,s^2)$. Verify the chain relations, intertwining equation and invertibility.

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** 先对旧基平移，逐列得到旧矩阵。新基要满足 $(T-I)f_0=0$、$(T-I)f_1=f_0$、$(T-I)f_2=f_1$，使新矩阵的超对角元为 $1$。

**Step 1.** Translate the old basis to form its matrix. The new basis must satisfy the displayed finite-difference chain to obtain superdiagonal ones.

$$
T1=1,\quad Ts=s+2,\quad T(s^2)=s^2+4s+4,\quad A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2&4\\0&1&4\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

**第 2 步。** 从 $f_0=1$ 开始，取一次式使平移差为 $1$，再取归一化二次式使其差为 $s/2$。下面逐项代入验证，而不是只写候选基。

**Step 2.** Start with one, choose a linear polynomial with difference one and then a normalized quadratic with the required linear difference. Verify each substitution.

$$
\begin{gathered}
f_0=1,\quad f_1=s/2,\quad f_2=s(s-2)/8,\\
(T-I)f_1=(s+2)/2-s/2=1,\\
(T-I)f_2=[s(s+2)-s(s-2)]/8=s/2.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 3 步。** 三个新基函数分别按常数、一次、二次系数写列坐标。矩阵上三角且对角元非零，故为一组基；链关系等价于下面的矩阵乘法检验。

**Step 3.** Form the columns from the monomial coefficients. Nonzero diagonal entries make this triangular coordinate matrix invertible; the chain equations give the intertwining identity.

$$
\boxed{X=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&0\\0&1/2&-1/4\\0&0&1/8\end{pmatrix}},\quad \det X=1/16,\quad AX=X\begin{pmatrix}1&1&0\\0&1&1\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix},\quad \boxed{X^{-1}AX=J_3(1)}.
$$

### 7. 广义特征向量的精确阶数 / Exact generalized-eigenvector order

**题目：** 在 $\mathbb C_2[s]$ 中，$Tf(s)=f(s+2)$、$\Delta=T-I$。对 $f(s)=3s^2-s+4$，求使 $\Delta^kf=0$ 的最小正整数 $k$，即其在特征值 $1$ 处的广义特征阶数，并求向量处最小多项式 $m_f(z)$。

**Question:** For translation by two and finite difference $\Delta=T-I$, find the exact generalized order at eigenvalue one and local minimal polynomial of $f(s)=3s^2-s+4$.

$$
V=\mathbb C_2[s],\quad (Tg)(s)=g(s+2),\quad \Delta=T-I,\quad f(s)=3s^2-s+4.
$$

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** $\Delta$ 表示平移后的函数减去原函数。先展开 $f(s+2)$，不要把它直接当成求导。

**Step 1.** Finite difference means translated polynomial minus the original, so first expand the translation explicitly.

$$
\begin{gathered}
f(s+2)=3(s+2)^2-(s+2)+4=3s^2+11s+14,\\
\Delta f=12s+10.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 2 步。** 对第一次结果再做相同的平移差。常数的平移差为零；首次为零在第三次，第二次仍不为零，才可判定确切阶数。

**Step 2.** Apply the same difference again. A constant has zero finite difference, so the third iterate is the first zero while the second remains nonzero.

$$
\begin{gathered}
\Delta^2f=[12(s+2)+10]-(12s+10)=24\ne0,\\
\Delta^3f=24-24=0,\\
\boxed{k=3}.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 3 步。** 整体平移只有根 $1$，并且 $T-I$ 在此空间三次幂为零。因此局部最小多项式是 $z-1$ 的幂；前两次不为零排除了更小幂。

**Step 3.** Translation has only eigenvalue one and its difference is nilpotent here. The nonzero second iterate excludes every lower power for the local annihilator.

$$
\boxed{m_f(z)=(z-1)^3}.
$$

### 8. 第一分解与第二分解 / First versus second decomposition

**题目：** 某有限维复算子的全部非平凡不变多项式为下列三项。第一分解按不同根收集广义特征空间，第二分解按非平凡不变多项式给出循环分量。求两种分解的各分量维数，验证总和，并判断根 $-1$ 的广义特征空间是否循环。

**Question:** A finite-dimensional complex operator has exactly the three displayed nonconstant invariants. Find component dimensions for the root-based primary decomposition and the invariant-based cyclic decomposition, check their totals and test cyclicity of the primary space at minus one.

$$
\begin{gathered}
x=z+1,\quad q=(z+1)^2+1,\\
\psi_1=x,\quad \psi_2=x^2q,\quad \psi_3=x^2q^2.
\end{gathered}
$$

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** 先把实二次因子在复数域拆开，分别从三个不变多项式读取每个根的块长。每次出现一个正指数幂，就对应一个块。

**Step 1.** Split the quadratic into complex linear factors and collect the block exponents at each root from all invariants, retaining every occurrence.

$$
\begin{gathered}
q=(z+1-i)(z+1+i);\\
-1:(1,2,2),\quad -1+i:(1,2),\quad -1-i:(1,2).
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 2 步。** 第一分解把同一根的所有块聚在一起，维数为该组块长之和。第二分解的各循环分量维数等于各自不变多项式的次数。

**Step 2.** Primary dimensions sum the block lengths at each root. Cyclic-component dimensions equal the degrees of the invariant polynomials.

$$
\begin{gathered}
\boxed{(\dim G_{-1},\dim G_{-1+i},\dim G_{-1-i})=(5,3,3)},\\
\boxed{(\deg\psi_1,\deg\psi_2,\deg\psi_3)=(1,4,6)},\quad 5+3+3=1+4+6=11.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 3 步。** 根 $-1$ 的广义特征空间有三个块，最大块长仅二，所以其限制算子的最小多项式次数为二，小于空间维数五，不能由单个向量循环生成。

**Step 3.** The primary space at minus one has three blocks but largest size two. Its restriction has minimal-polynomial degree two, smaller than its dimension five, preventing cyclicity.

$$
\begin{gathered}
m_{T|_{G_{-1}}}=(z+1)^2,\\
\deg m_{T|_{G_{-1}}}=2<5.
\end{gathered}
$$

## 二、公式与条件速查 / Formulas and conditions

| 用途 / Purpose | 公式与条件 / Formula and conditions |
| --- | --- |
| 向量处零化 / Local annihilation | $p(T)v=0\iff m_v\mid p$；$m_0=1$ |
| 包含关系 / Inclusion | $U\subseteq W\Rightarrow m_U\mid m_W$ |
| 子空间处 / At a subspace | $m_W=\operatorname{lcm}_i m_{w_i}$，$(w_i)$ 是基 / a basis |
| 相似不变 / Similarity | $m_{X^{-1}AX}=m_A$，$X$ 可逆 / invertible |
| 最大不变多项式 / Largest invariant | $m_A=d_d=\chi_A/D_{d-1}$，$d\ge1,D_0=1$ |
| 分块 / Blocks | $m_{\operatorname{diag}(A_i)}=\operatorname{lcm}_i m_{A_i}$ |
| Jordan 判据 / Jordan test | $m_A=\prod_\rho(z-\rho)^{b_\rho}$，在复数域且 $b_\rho$ 为最大块长 / over $\mathbb C$, with largest block sizes |
| 第一分解 / First decomposition | $W=\bigoplus_i(W\cap\ker\varphi_i(T))$，$W$ 不变且 $m_W=\prod_i\varphi_i$ 两两互素 / invariant $W$, pairwise coprime factors |
| 循环维数 / Cyclic dimension | $\dim\mathcal S_T[v]=\deg m_v$ |
| 整体循环 / Whole-space cyclicity | $T$ 循环 $\iff\deg m_T=\dim V$ / $T$ is cyclic iff the degrees agree |
| 第二分解 / Second decomposition | $V=\bigoplus_i\mathcal S_T[\beta_i]$，$m_{\beta_i}=\psi_i$，$\psi_i$ 为非平凡不变多项式 / nonconstant invariant polynomials |
| 平移算子 / Translation | $m_T=(z-1)^{n+1}$，在 $\mathbb C_n[s]$ 上且 $a\ne0$ / on $\mathbb C_n[s]$, $a\ne0$ |

**中文：** 做题时先确认空间维数和系数域，再区分求的是向量处还是整体最小多项式。求出 $X$ 后直接验证 $AX=XJ$，通常比先求逆更方便。 / **English:** First check the dimension and field, then distinguish local from global minimal polynomials. Verify a proposed change of basis through $AX=XJ$, usually without computing an inverse.
