## 一、一个向量反复作用能生成多大的空间 / The orbit of one vector

**中文：** 设向量 $v$ 的最小多项式 $m_v$ 次数为 $r$，定义

**English:** If the minimal polynomial at $v$ has degree $r$, define

$$
\mathcal S_T[v]=\operatorname{span}\{v,Tv,\ldots,T^{r-1}v\}
=\{p(T)v:p\in\mathbb F[z]\}.
$$

**中文：** 前 $r$ 个向量独立，否则会出现更低次零化多项式；$T^rv$ 可由前者表示，所有更高次作用也一样。因此

**English:** The first $r$ vectors are independent, or a lower-degree annihilator would exist. The vector $T^rv$ is a combination of them, as are all higher iterates. Hence

**中文：** 当 $v=0$ 时，循环子空间为 $\{0\}$，基为空，$r=0$。 / **English:** For $v=0$, the cyclic subspace is $\{0\}$, its basis is empty and $r=0$.

$$
\dim\mathcal S_T[v]=r=\deg m_v,\qquad T(\mathcal S_T[v])\subseteq\mathcal S_T[v].
$$

**中文：** 在循环基 $(v,Tv,\ldots,T^{r-1}v)$ 下，$T$ 的矩阵为 $C(m_v)$。最后一列来自 $m_v(T)v=0$，所以系数要取负。循环子空间上的最小多项式仍为 $m_v$，因为 $p(T)$ 与所有 $T^k$ 交换。

**English:** In the cyclic basis $(v,Tv,\ldots,T^{r-1}v)$, the matrix is $C(m_v)$. Its last column comes from $m_v(T)v=0$, explaining the negative coefficients. The restriction has the same minimal polynomial $m_v$, since polynomial expressions in $T$ commute with its powers.

**中文：** 整个 $d$ 维空间是单个循环空间，当且仅当 $\deg m_T=d$。充分性使用上一章的“最小多项式可由一个向量取得”，再比较维数。

**English:** A $d$-dimensional space is cyclic exactly when $\deg m_T=d$. Sufficiency follows by choosing a vector attaining $m_T$ and comparing dimensions.

## 二、完整例题：循环基与伴随矩阵 / A complete cyclic-basis example

**教学补充例题 / Teaching supplement：**

$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}1&1&0\\0&1&0\\0&0&2\end{bmatrix},\qquad
v=\begin{bmatrix}0\\1\\1\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** 两个块的最小多项式为 $(z-1)^2$ 与 $z-2$，互素，故 $m_A=(z-1)^2(z-2)=z^3-4z^2+5z-2$。取

**English:** The block minimal polynomials are coprime, so $m_A=(z-1)^2(z-2)=z^3-4z^2+5z-2$. Take

$$
P=(v,Av,A^2v)=\begin{bmatrix}0&1&2\\1&1&1\\1&2&4\end{bmatrix},\qquad
C(m_A)=\begin{bmatrix}0&0&2\\1&0&-5\\0&1&4\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** $\det P=-1$，且 $AP=PC(m_A)$。因此 $v$ 是整个三维空间的循环生成元。按根分解会得到维数 $2+1$ 的两个主分量，但按循环分解只需一个三维块。

**English:** Here $\det P=-1$ and $AP=PC(m_A)$. Thus $v$ generates the whole three-dimensional space cyclically. Primary decomposition groups it as dimensions two plus one, while a cyclic decomposition uses one block of dimension three.

## 三、第二分解：按不变多项式分循环空间 / Second decomposition into cyclic spaces

**中文：** 设特征矩阵的非常数不变多项式为 $\psi_1\mid\cdots\mid\psi_\ell$。可以选择生成元 $\beta_i$，使

**English:** Given the nonconstant invariant polynomials $\psi_1\mid\cdots\mid\psi_\ell$ of the characteristic matrix, choose generators $\beta_i$ so that

$$
V=\bigoplus_{i=1}^{\ell}\mathcal S_T[\beta_i],\qquad
m_{\beta_i}=\psi_i,\qquad
\dim\mathcal S_T[\beta_i]=\deg\psi_i.
$$

**中文：** 这就是第二分解，对应自然标准形 $\operatorname{diag}(C(\psi_1),\ldots,C(\psi_\ell))$。它按不变多项式分组，第一分解按互素主因子分组，两个分组方向不同。

**English:** This second decomposition corresponds to the natural normal form. It groups by invariant polynomials, whereas the first decomposition groups by coprime primary factors.

**中文：** 从 Jordan 链理解：单条链的链尾可生成整条链空间，因为 $(T-\rho I)^k$ 是 $T$ 的多项式。对同一个不变多项式中的不同根，各取对应链尾再求和，局部最小多项式互素，其和便生成这一整个循环分量。这说明课件第 21—22 页中的 $W_{ij}$ 和 $W_i$ 都可循环。

**English:** A Jordan chain is generated by its terminal vector because powers of $T-\rho I$ are polynomials in $T$. For the different roots inside one invariant polynomial, sum their chain terminals. Their local minimal polynomials are coprime, and the sum generates that whole cyclic component. This explains why both the chain spaces $W_{ij}$ and the grouped spaces $W_i$ in the slides are cyclic.

**作业联系 / Homework connection：**

| 例子 / Example | 第一分解的分量维数 / Primary dimensions | 第二分解的循环维数 / Cyclic dimensions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HW7.3 | $4,3,3$ | $1,3,6$ |
| HW7.4 | $5,3,3$ | $1,4,6$ |

**中文：** 每一行的两侧维数和相同，但分量不能按位置逐项对应。两者都来自同一组 Jordan 链的不同组合。

**English:** Each row has the same total dimension on both sides, but the components do not correspond position by position. Both arise by regrouping the same Jordan chains.

## 四、平移算子：用有限差分找 Jordan 基 / Translation and a finite-difference Jordan basis

**作业例题：HW7.2、HW8.5。 / Homework example: HW7.2 and HW8.5.** 取 $T_af(s)=f(s+a)$，$a\ne0$，定义在 $\mathbb C_n[s]$。 / Let $T_af(s)=f(s+a)$, $a\ne0$, on $\mathbb C_n[s]$.

**中文：** 记 $\Delta=T_a-I$。若 $f$ 的次数为 $d\ge1$、首项系数为 $c_d$，则 $\Delta f$ 的首项为 $dc_da s^{d-1}$；常数的差分为零。对任意非零 $f$ 反复作用得

**English:** Put $\Delta=T_a-I$. For degree $d\ge1$ and leading coefficient $c_d$, the leading term of $\Delta f$ is $dc_da s^{d-1}$; constants have zero difference. For every nonzero $f$, iteration gives

$$
\Delta^df=c_dd!a^d\ne0,\qquad\Delta^{d+1}f=0.
$$

**中文：** 所以每个非零多项式都是特征值 $1$ 的广义特征向量，确切阶数为 $d+1$；其局部最小多项式为 $(z-1)^{d+1}$。全空间最小多项式为 $(z-1)^{n+1}$，只有一个 Jordan 块。

**English:** Every nonzero polynomial is generalized at eigenvalue one, of exact order $d+1$, with local minimal polynomial $(z-1)^{d+1}$. The whole-space minimal polynomial is $(z-1)^{n+1}$, giving one Jordan block.

**中文：** 适合有限差分的链是 Newton 多项式，而非直接照搬微分算子的阶乘幂基：

**English:** Newton polynomials provide a finite-difference chain:

$$
f_0=1,\qquad f_k(s)=\frac{s(s-a)\cdots(s-(k-1)a)}{k!a^k},\qquad
\Delta f_k=f_{k-1}\quad(k\ge1).
$$

**中文：** 按 $(f_0,f_1,\ldots,f_n)$ 排列基向量，设原单项式基下的矩阵为 $A$。令 $X$ 的第 $k$ 列为 $f_k$ 的系数列，则

**English:** Order the basis as $(f_0,f_1,\ldots,f_n)$ and let $A$ be the monomial-basis matrix. Take the coefficient columns of $f_k$ as $X$. Then

$$
X_{ik}=[s^i]f_k(s),\qquad X_{kk}=\frac1{k!a^k},\qquad
AX=XJ_{n+1}(1).
$$

**中文：** 行列从 $0$ 编号。$X$ 上三角、对角元非零，因此可逆。相应特征矩阵的 Smith 形为 $\operatorname{diag}(I_n,(z-1)^{n+1})$。

**English:** Indices start at zero. The nonzero diagonal makes $X$ invertible. The characteristic matrix has Smith form $\operatorname{diag}(I_n,(z-1)^{n+1})$.

**教学补充例题 / Teaching supplement：** 对 $n=2,a=2$， / For $n=2,a=2$,

$$
f_0=1,\quad f_1=\frac s2,\quad f_2=\frac{s(s-2)}8,\qquad
X=\begin{bmatrix}1&0&0\\0&1/2&-1/4\\0&0&1/8\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** 原矩阵 $A=\begin{bmatrix}1&2&4\\0&1&4\\0&0&1\end{bmatrix}$，可直接检查 $AX=XJ_3(1)$。这里第二列和第三列的低次项使差分关系恰好归一化为系数 $1$。

**English:** The original matrix is $A=\begin{bmatrix}1&2&4\\0&1&4\\0&0&1\end{bmatrix}$, and direct multiplication verifies $AX=XJ_3(1)$. The lower-degree terms normalize the difference relation to coefficient one.

## 五、平移算子的循环生成元 / A cyclic generator for translation

**中文：** 取 $\beta=s^n$，则 $\beta,\Delta\beta,\ldots,\Delta^n\beta$ 的次数从 $n$ 降到 $0$，均有非零首项，故是一组基。又 $\Delta=T_a-I$，这些向量都属于 $\mathcal S_{T_a}[\beta]$，所以

**English:** With $\beta=s^n$, the vectors $\beta,\Delta\beta,\ldots,\Delta^n\beta$ have distinct degrees from $n$ to zero and form a basis. Since $\Delta=T_a-I$, they all belong to its cyclic space. Thus

$$
\boxed{\mathbb C_n[s]=\mathcal S_{T_a}[s^n],\qquad\ell=1}.
$$

**中文：** $1$ 虽是普通特征向量，但当 $n\ge1$ 时只能生成一维常数空间，不能生成整个多项式空间。

**English:** The constant one is an ordinary eigenvector, but for $n\ge1$ it generates only the one-dimensional constant space, not all polynomials.

## 六、教学补充：验证自然形与 Jordan 形之间的关系 / Teaching supplement: verifying the form-to-form relation

**中文：** 课件写 $VN=JV$，但没有展示通式。对 $p(z)=(z-\rho)^2$，可直接构造

**English:** The slides state $VN=JV$ without displaying the general formula. For $p(z)=(z-\rho)^2$, take

$$
N=C(p)=\begin{bmatrix}0&-\rho^2\\1&2\rho\end{bmatrix},\qquad
V=\begin{bmatrix}0&1\\1&\rho\end{bmatrix},\qquad
VN=J_2(\rho)V.
$$

**中文：** $\det V=-1$，故 $VNV^{-1}=J_2(\rho)$。若改用习惯的 $X^{-1}NX=J$，应取 $X=V^{-1}$。这能防止把两种换基方向混写。

**English:** Since $\det V=-1$, we have $VNV^{-1}=J_2(\rho)$. To use the convention $X^{-1}NX=J$, take $X=V^{-1}$. The distinction fixes the direction of the basis change.
