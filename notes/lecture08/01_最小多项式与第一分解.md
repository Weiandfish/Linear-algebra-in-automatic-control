## 一、零化一个向量与零化整个空间 / Annihilating a vector versus a space

**中文：** 对 $T\in L(V)$，多项式 $p(z)=\sum c_kz^k$ 定义算子 $p(T)=\sum c_kT^k$，其中 $T^0=I$。若 $p(T)v=0$，就说 $p$ 在向量 $v$ 处零化。所有这样的多项式组成理想，其唯一首一生成元是 $m_v$。

**English:** For $T\in L(V)$, define $p(T)=\sum c_kT^k$, with $T^0=I$. The polynomial annihilates at $v$ when $p(T)v=0$. Such polynomials form an ideal whose unique monic generator is $m_v$.

$$
I_v=\{p:p(T)v=0\}=\langle m_v\rangle,\qquad
p(T)v=0\iff m_v\mid p.
$$

**中文：** 在 $d$ 维空间中，$v,Tv,\ldots,T^dv$ 必线性相关，故存在非零零化多项式。取次数最低者并首一化即可。若更低次非零多项式也零化，便与最小性矛盾。零向量单独约定 $m_0=1$。

**English:** In dimension $d$, the vectors $v,Tv,\ldots,T^dv$ are dependent, giving a nonzero annihilating polynomial. Normalize one of least degree to be monic. A nonzero polynomial of lower degree cannot annihilate the vector. Set $m_0=1$.

**中文：** 子空间 $W$ 处要求对每个 $w\in W$ 都成立。若 $(w_1,\ldots,w_r)$ 是其基，只需逐个检查基向量；全空间的最小多项式记为 $m_T$。

**English:** Annihilation on $W$ requires vanishing at every $w\in W$. It suffices to check a basis. Write $m_T$ for the whole-space minimal polynomial.

$$
m_W=\operatorname{lcm}(m_{w_1},\ldots,m_{w_r}),\qquad
U\subseteq W\subseteq V\Longrightarrow m_U\mid m_W\mid m_T.
$$

**中文：** 这里不必先假设 $W$ 不变；但要把它解释为限制算子 $T|_W$ 的最小多项式，就必须有 $T(W)\subseteq W$。

**English:** This annihilation definition does not require invariance. Interpreting it as the minimal polynomial of a restriction $T|_W$ does require $T(W)\subseteq W$.

**教学补充例题 / Teaching supplement：** 设 $T=\operatorname{diag}(1,2)$。 / Let $T=\operatorname{diag}(1,2)$.

$$
m_{e_1}=z-1,\quad m_{e_2}=z-2,\quad
m_{e_1+e_2}=m_T=(z-1)(z-2).
$$

**中文：** 向量处的最小多项式可能小于全空间的最小多项式；求向量问题不能直接照抄全空间结果。

**English:** A vector's minimal polynomial can be smaller than the whole-space polynomial, so the latter cannot simply be copied into a local problem.

## 二、矩阵表示、Jordan 块与 Cayley-Hamilton / Matrices, Jordan blocks and Cayley-Hamilton

**中文：** 若 $A=[T]_B$，则 $[p(T)v]_B=p(A)[v]_B$，所以算子与其矩阵具有相同的零化及最小多项式。相似换基不改变这些多项式。

**English:** With $A=[T]_B$, the identity $[p(T)v]_B=p(A)[v]_B$ makes annihilation and minimal polynomials agree between operator and coordinates. Similarity preserves them.

**中文：** 对单个 Jordan 块，$(J_m(\rho)-\rho I)^m=0$，但低一阶幂不为零。在复数域上，每个根只取其最大块长，得到

**English:** A Jordan block satisfies $(J_m(\rho)-\rho I)^m=0$ but not the preceding power. Over the complex field, take the largest block size at each root:

$$
m_A(z)=\prod_\rho(z-\rho)^{b_\rho},\qquad
b_\rho=\max\{\text{该根的块长 / block sizes at }\rho\}.
$$

**中文：** 对特征矩阵，记首一行列式因子为 $D_k$，$D_0=1$。若 $A$ 为 $d$ 阶，则

**English:** Let $D_k$ be the monic determinantal divisors of the characteristic matrix, with $D_0=1$. For $d$-by-$d$ $A$,

$$
\boxed{m_A=d_d=\frac{D_d}{D_{d-1}}=\frac{\chi_A}{D_{d-1}}},\qquad
\chi_A(z)=\det(zI-A).
$$

**中文：** 即最小多项式是最大不变多项式，不是所有不变多项式的乘积。后者才是特征多项式。Cayley-Hamilton 定理给出 $\chi_A(A)=0$，因而 $m_A\mid\chi_A$、$\deg m_A\le d$。

**English:** The minimal polynomial is the largest invariant polynomial, while their product is the characteristic polynomial. Cayley-Hamilton gives $\chi_A(A)=0$, hence $m_A\mid\chi_A$ and $\deg m_A\le d$.

**中文：** 课件定理 3.26 还把 $p(A)=0$ 写成矩阵多项式的右整除：存在 $Q(z)$ 使 $p(z)I=Q(z)(zI-A)$。右除法的常数余式是 $p(A)$，因此这与零化等价。

**English:** Theorem 3.26 also expresses annihilation as right divisibility: $p(z)I=Q(z)(zI-A)$ for a polynomial matrix $Q$. Right division leaves constant remainder $p(A)$, making the two conditions equivalent.

## 三、分块矩阵取最小公倍式 / Block matrices use an lcm

$$
A=\operatorname{diag}(A_1,\ldots,A_r)
\Longrightarrow m_A=\operatorname{lcm}(m_{A_1},\ldots,m_{A_r}).
$$

**中文：** 因为 $p(A)$ 的每个对角块就是 $p(A_i)$，必须同时零化所有块。只有各块最小多项式两两互素时，最小公倍式才等于乘积。例如 $\operatorname{diag}(1,1)$ 的最小多项式为 $z-1$，而非 $(z-1)^2$。

**English:** The blocks of $p(A)$ are $p(A_i)$, so they must all vanish. The lcm equals a product only for pairwise coprime minimal polynomials. For example, $\operatorname{diag}(1,1)$ has minimal polynomial $z-1$, not its square.

**作业例题：第 8 次第 1—2 题 / Homework examples: HW8.1-2.**

$$
A_1=\begin{bmatrix}-7&16&8\\-4&9&4\\-4&7&5\end{bmatrix},\qquad
A_2=\operatorname{diag}\left(\begin{bmatrix}0&1\\-1&2\end{bmatrix},\begin{bmatrix}0&1\\-4&-4\end{bmatrix}\right).
$$

**中文：** $A_1$ 的特征多项式为 $(z-1)(z-3)^2$，且 $\operatorname{rank}(A_1-3I)=2$，所以根 $3$ 对应二阶块。$A_2$ 的两个二阶块分别满足 $(B-I)^2=0$、$(C+2I)^2=0$，且一次幂不为零。故

**English:** For $A_1$, the characteristic polynomial is $(z-1)(z-3)^2$ and rank at three is two, forcing a size-two block there. The two blocks of $A_2$ satisfy $(B-I)^2=0$ and $(C+2I)^2=0$ but not the first powers. Hence

$$
m_{A_1}=(z-1)(z-3)^2,\qquad m_{A_2}=(z-1)^2(z+2)^2.
$$

## 四、第一分解：按互素因子分空间 / First decomposition by coprime factors

**中文：** 设 $W$ 不变，$m_W=\varphi_1\cdots\varphi_r$，各因子两两互素。则

**English:** Suppose $W$ is invariant and $m_W=\varphi_1\cdots\varphi_r$ with pairwise coprime factors. Then

$$
W=\bigoplus_{i=1}^r W_i,\qquad
W_i=W\cap\ker\varphi_i(T),\qquad m_{W_i}=\varphi_i.
$$

**中文：** 教学补充证明：置 $q_i=m_W/\varphi_i$，由贝祖等式选 $b_i$，使 $e_i=b_iq_i$ 满足对 $\varphi_i$ 余 $1$、对其余因子余 $0$。于是 $w_i=e_i(T)w$ 属于 $W_i$，且 $w=\sum_iw_i$。这些投影也能从和中单独取出每个分量，证明和为直和。

**English:** A supplementary proof sets $q_i=m_W/\varphi_i$ and uses Bezout to choose $e_i=b_iq_i$, congruent to one modulo $\varphi_i$ and zero modulo the other factors. The vectors $w_i=e_i(T)w$ lie in $W_i$ and sum to $w$. The same projections isolate each component, proving directness.

**中文：** 在复数域中取 $\varphi_i=(z-\rho_i)^{b_i}$，就得到广义特征空间分解；这里 $b_i$ 是最大块长，$\dim W_i$ 则是所有该根块长之和，二者不要混淆。

**English:** Over the complex field, taking $\varphi_i=(z-\rho_i)^{b_i}$ gives the generalized-eigenspace decomposition. The exponent $b_i$ is the largest block size, whereas $\dim W_i$ sums all block sizes at that root.

**作业例题：两组初等因子 / Homework example: two different divisor lists.**

| 来源 / Source | 整体维数 / Dimension | 各根块长 / Block sizes | 广义特征空间维数 / Primary dimensions | 最小多项式 / Minimal polynomial |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HW7.3 / HW8.3 | 10 | $-3:(1,1,2)$；$-2\pm5i:(1,2)$ | $4,3,3$ | $(z+3)^2((z+2)^2+25)^2$ |
| HW7.4 / HW8.4 | 11 | $-1:(1,2,2)$；$-1\pm i:(1,2)$ | $5,3,3$ | $(z+1)^2((z+1)^2+1)^2$ |

**中文：** 两组的最大块长都为 $2$，所以相应广义特征空间均等于 $\ker(A-\rho_iI)^2$，但各分量维数不同。

**English:** Both lists have maximum block size two at each root, so each primary space is $\ker(A-\rho_iI)^2$, but their component dimensions differ.

## 五、一个向量能取得整个子空间的最小多项式 / Attainment at one vector

**作业证明：第 7 次第 1 题 / Homework proof: HW7.1.**

**中文：** 写 $m_W=\prod p_i^{a_i}$，其中 $p_i$ 互异首一不可约，$q_i=m_W/p_i^{a_i}$。最小性保证可以选 $v_i\in W$ 使 $(m_W/p_i)(T)v_i\ne0$。取 $y_i=q_i(T)v_i$，则

**English:** Factor $m_W=\prod p_i^{a_i}$ into distinct monic irreducibles and put $q_i=m_W/p_i^{a_i}$. Minimality provides $v_i\in W$ with $(m_W/p_i)(T)v_i\ne0$. Set $y_i=q_i(T)v_i$. Then

$$
p_i(T)^{a_i}y_i=0,\qquad p_i(T)^{a_i-1}y_i\ne0,
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad m_{y_i}=p_i^{a_i}.
$$

**中文：** $W$ 不变保证 $y_i\in W$。互素分量求和的最小多项式是它们的乘积，因此 $x=\sum_i y_i$ 满足 $m_x=m_W$。若 $W=\{0\}$，取 $x=0$ 即可。此证明不需要假设标量域无限。

**English:** Invariance keeps each $y_i$ in $W$. A sum of vectors with pairwise coprime minimal polynomials has their product as its minimal polynomial, so $x=\sum_i y_i$ attains $m_W$. For the zero subspace take $x=0$. No infinite-field assumption is needed.
