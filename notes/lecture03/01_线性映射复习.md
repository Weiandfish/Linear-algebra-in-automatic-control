# 线性映射复习 / Reviewing Linear Mappings

[阅读导航 / Guide](00_阅读导航.md) · [术语与公式 / Reference](08_术语与公式速查.md)

> 讲义内容 / Lecture content: slides 3-7; Definition 1.21.

## 1. 保持线性组合 / Preserving linear combinations

**中文：** 设 $U,V$ 是同一标量域 $\mathbb F$ 上的线性空间。映射 $T:U\to V$ 线性，是指它同时保持加法和数乘。两条定义可以合并成一条；证明一个映射线性时，使用合并后的等式通常更直接。

**English:** Let $U,V$ be linear spaces over the same scalar field $\mathbb F$. A map $T:U\to V$ is linear when it preserves addition and scalar multiplication. The two conditions can be combined into one, which is often the most direct form for a proof.

$$
T(u+v)=T(u)+T(v),\qquad T(au)=aT(u),
$$

$$
\boxed{T(au+bv)=aT(u)+bT(v)}
\quad(u,v\in U;\ a,b\in\mathbb F).
$$

**中文：** “保持”意味着先在输入空间做线性组合，再映射，与先分别映射、再在输出空间做同样的组合，结果一致。输入向量可以是数字列、函数、多项式或矩阵；判断线性性看运算，不看对象的外观。

**English:** Preservation means that forming a combination before mapping gives the same result as mapping first and forming the corresponding output combination. Input vectors may be numerical columns, functions, polynomials, or matrices. Linearity concerns their operations, not their appearance.

### 矩阵乘法例子 / Matrix multiplication example

**中文：** 课件第 6 页取 $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$，定义 $T(x)=Ax$。由矩阵乘法的分配律和数乘规则，立即得到线性性。其输入空间为 $\mathbb R^n$，输出空间为 $\mathbb R^m$。

**English:** Slide 6 takes $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$ and defines $T(x)=Ax$. Distributivity and the rules for scalar multiplication immediately prove linearity. Its domain is $\mathbb R^n$ and its codomain is $\mathbb R^m$.

$$
A(ax+by)=aAx+bAy.
$$

**教学补充 / Teaching supplement：** 对矩阵空间上的 $T(X)=AX+XB$，两项都是线性映射，直接展开可得下式。这正是 Homework 2 第 2(2) 题的证明方法。

**English:** For the matrix-space map $T(X)=AX+XB$, both terms are linear. Expansion gives the identity below, which is the method for Homework 2, Problem 2(2).

$$
T(aX+bY)=a(AX+XB)+b(AY+YB)=aT(X)+bT(Y).
$$

## 2. 恒等映射和零映射 / Identity and zero maps

**中文：** 恒等映射 $I_V:V\to V$ 保留每个向量；零映射 $0_{U,V}:U\to V$ 把每个输入都送到输出空间的零向量。两者都线性。恒等映射的输入、输出是同一个空间；零映射允许输入、输出空间不同。

**English:** The identity $I_V:V\to V$ leaves every vector unchanged. The zero map $0_{U,V}:U\to V$ sends every input to the zero vector of the codomain. Both are linear. Identity uses the same space on both sides; a zero map may have different domain and codomain spaces.

$$
I_V(v)=v,\qquad 0_{U,V}(u)=0_V.
$$

**中文：** 教学补充：只有输入、输出使用同一组基时，恒等映射的矩阵才是单位矩阵。若使用不同的输入基与输出基，它的矩阵是坐标转换矩阵。这区分了“映射本身”和“选定基后的矩阵”。

**English:** Teaching supplement: the identity map has the identity matrix when input and output use the same basis. With different bases, its matrix converts coordinates between those bases. This distinguishes the map itself from its matrix in chosen coordinates.

## 3. 线性性的三个结果 / Three consequences of linearity

**中文：** 第一，线性映射一定把零送到零。由 $T(0)=T(0+0)=T(0)+T(0)$，两边减去 $T(0)$ 即可。第二，线性组合可以逐项通过映射。第三，线性相关的输入，其像仍线性相关。

**English:** First, a linear map sends zero to zero: subtract $T(0)$ from $T(0)=T(0+0)=T(0)+T(0)$. Second, a finite linear combination can be mapped term by term. Third, dependent inputs have dependent images.

$$
T(0)=0,\qquad
T\left(\sum_{i=1}^s a_i u_i\right)=\sum_{i=1}^s a_iT(u_i).
$$

**中文：** 对第三点，若 $\sum a_i u_i=0$ 且系数不全为零，映射后仍有 $\sum a_iT(u_i)=0$，系数还是那一组非零关系。课件第 5 页这一条中的输入向量应属于定义域 $U$。

**English:** For the third result, a relation $\sum a_i u_i=0$ with coefficients not all zero becomes $\sum a_iT(u_i)=0$ with the same coefficients. In this statement on slide 5, the input vectors belong to the domain $U$.

### 反方向为什么不成立 / Why the converse fails

**中文：** 第 7 页提醒：无关的输入可能变成相关的像。教学补充例子取投影 $T(x,y)=(x,0)$。输入 $e_1,e_2$ 无关，但 $T(e_2)=0$，所以两个像相关。映射把某些方向压成了零。

**English:** Slide 7 warns that independent inputs can have dependent images. As a supplementary example, take the projection $T(x,y)=(x,0)$. The inputs $e_1,e_2$ are independent, but $T(e_2)=0$, so their images are dependent. The map has collapsed a direction to zero.

$$
T(e_1)=e_1,\qquad T(e_2)=0.
$$

**中文：** 如果映射是单射，则不会丢掉非零方向，无关性才会得到保持。这个条件将在 [同构](04_同构.md) 中解释。

**English:** Injectivity prevents nonzero directions from disappearing and therefore preserves independence. This condition is developed in [Isomorphism](04_同构.md).

## 4. 两个常见误判 / Two common mistakes

**中文：** $T(0)=0$ 是线性性的必要条件，不是充分条件。例如 $T(x)=x^2$ 满足 $T(0)=0$，却有 $T(1+1)=4\ne2=T(1)+T(1)$。另一类错误是把仿射映射 $T(x)=Ax+c$ 直接叫作线性映射；当 $c\ne0$ 时，它连零向量都不保持。

**English:** Sending zero to zero is necessary but insufficient for linearity. For example, $T(x)=x^2$ sends zero to zero, yet $T(1+1)=4\ne2=T(1)+T(1)$. Another mistake is calling the affine map $T(x)=Ax+c$ linear: when $c\ne0$, it does not even preserve zero.

**中文：** 自测：$T(p)=p'$ 与 $S(p)=p'+1$，哪个线性？前者线性；后者把零多项式映成 $1$，因此不线性。

**English:** Self-check: which of $T(p)=p'$ and $S(p)=p'+1$ is linear? The first is linear; the second sends the zero polynomial to $1$ and is not linear.
