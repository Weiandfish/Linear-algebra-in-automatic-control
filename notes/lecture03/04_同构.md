# 同构 / Isomorphism

[阅读导航 / Guide](00_阅读导航.md) · [术语与公式 / Reference](08_术语与公式速查.md)

> 讲义内容 / Lecture content: slides 19-22; Definitions 1.25-1.26 and Theorem 1.15.

## 1. 单射、满射和双射 / Injective, surjective, and bijective maps

**中文：** 单射表示不同输入不会得到同一个输出；满射表示输出空间里的每个向量都能由某个输入得到；同时满足二者就是双射。满射定义中的原像属于输入空间 $U$，课件第 19 页把它写在 $V$ 中是笔误。

**English:** Injectivity means distinct inputs never share an output. Surjectivity means every vector of the codomain is reached by an input. A map satisfying both conditions is bijective. A preimage in the surjectivity definition belongs to $U$; its placement in $V$ on slide 19 is a typo.

$$
\begin{aligned}
T\text{ injective}&\iff T(u_1)=T(u_2)\Longrightarrow u_1=u_2,\\
T\text{ surjective}&\iff \forall v\in V,\ \exists u\in U:\ T(u)=v.
\end{aligned}
$$

**中文：** 对线性映射，单射可用核判断，满射可用像判断。两个条件分别检查“有没有丢掉输入方向”和“有没有漏掉输出方向”。

**English:** For a linear map, its kernel tests injectivity and its range tests surjectivity. The two conditions check whether input directions disappear and whether output directions are missed.

$$
\boxed{T\text{ injective}\iff\ker T=\{0\}},\qquad
\boxed{T\text{ surjective}\iff\operatorname{im}T=V}.
$$

**中文：** 单射判据来自 $T(u)=T(v)\iff T(u-v)=0$。若核只有零向量，就只能有 $u-v=0$；反过来，单射使得 $T(u)=T(0)$ 只能来自 $u=0$。

**English:** The injectivity criterion follows from $T(u)=T(v)\iff T(u-v)=0$. A trivial kernel forces $u=v$. Conversely, injectivity makes $T(u)=T(0)$ possible only for $u=0$.

## 2. 同构保留线性结构 / Isomorphism preserves linear structure

**中文：** 同构是一个双射的线性映射。若存在这样的映射，称两个空间同构，记作 $U\cong V$。它意味着每个向量都可被唯一编码和还原，并且线性组合的规则得到保留。

**English:** An isomorphism is a bijective linear map. If one exists, the spaces are isomorphic, written $U\cong V$. Every vector can be uniquely encoded and recovered, and the rules for linear combinations are preserved.

**教学补充例子 / Supplementary example：**

$$
\Phi:\mathbb R_1[\lambda]\to\mathbb R^2,\qquad
\Phi(a+b\lambda)=\begin{bmatrix}a\\b\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** $\Phi$ 线性；每个系数列对应唯一的多项式；逆映射是 $(a,b)^T\mapsto a+b\lambda$。所以多项式空间与数字列空间虽然对象不同，但作为实线性空间同构。

**English:** The map is linear, each coefficient column corresponds to one polynomial, and its inverse is $(a,b)^T\mapsto a+b\lambda$. Thus the polynomial and column-vector spaces have different kinds of elements but are isomorphic as real linear spaces.

### 逆映射为什么仍线性 / Why the inverse is linear

**中文：** 设 $v_i=T(u_i)$。由 $T(au_1+bu_2)=av_1+bv_2$ 和原像唯一性，得到下面的等式。这也解释了同构关系为什么对称。

**English:** Write $v_i=T(u_i)$. The equality $T(au_1+bu_2)=av_1+bv_2$, together with uniqueness of preimages, gives the identity below. It also explains symmetry of isomorphism.

$$
T^{-1}(av_1+bv_2)=aT^{-1}(v_1)+bT^{-1}(v_2).
$$

**中文：** 第 20 页将同构说明为等价关系：恒等映射给出自反性；逆映射给出对称性；两个同构的复合给出传递性。

**English:** Slide 20 treats isomorphism as an equivalence relation: the identity gives reflexivity, inverses give symmetry, and composition of isomorphisms gives transitivity.

## 3. 同构会保留什么 / What an isomorphism preserves

**中文：** 第 21 页强调四点：零向量的原像只有零；无关性在映射前后等价；基映成基，所以维数不变；任何子空间都映成同维数的子空间。

**English:** Slide 21 emphasizes four facts: only zero maps to zero; independence is equivalent before and after mapping; bases map to bases, so dimensions agree; and every subspace maps to a subspace of the same dimension.

$$
u_1,\ldots,u_k\text{ independent}
\iff T(u_1),\ldots,T(u_k)\text{ independent},
$$

$$
W\subseteq U\text{ a subspace}
\Longrightarrow \dim T(W)=\dim W.
$$

**中文：** 无关性证明的关键是：若 $\sum a_iT(u_i)=0$，则 $T(\sum a_i u_i)=0$；单射使 $\sum a_i u_i=0$。注意，仅有单射已经足以保持无关性；还要满射，才能保证整个输入基映成整个输出空间的基。

**English:** For independence, $\sum a_iT(u_i)=0$ implies $T(\sum a_i u_i)=0$. Injectivity then gives $\sum a_i u_i=0$. Injectivity alone preserves independence; surjectivity is also needed for a domain basis to map to a basis of the whole codomain.

## 4. 有限维空间的判定 / The finite-dimensional criterion

**中文：** 定理 1.15：同一域上的两个有限维线性空间同构，当且仅当维数相同。必要性来自基映成基；充分性通过选两组基，逐个配对并线性扩张来构造同构。

**English:** Theorem 1.15: two finite-dimensional spaces over the same field are isomorphic exactly when they have the same dimension. Necessity follows because bases map to bases. For sufficiency, pair two bases and extend the pairing linearly.

$$
\boxed{U\cong V\iff\dim U=\dim V}
\quad\text{(finite-dimensional spaces over the same field)}.
$$

**中文：** 维数相同保证“存在某个同构”，不保证“任意给定映射都是同构”。例如 $\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R^2$ 的零映射不是同构。还必须说明标量域：$\mathbb C$ 在复数域上维数为 1，在实数域上维数为 2。

**English:** Equal dimensions guarantee that some isomorphism exists, not that every specified map is an isomorphism. The zero map $\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R^2$ is a counterexample. The scalar field also matters: $\mathbb C$ has complex dimension one but real dimension two.

## 5. 例题：微分方程解空间的同构 / Example: an ODE solution-space isomorphism

> 教学补充，与 Homework 2 第 2(1) 题相连 / Teaching supplement connected to Homework 2, Problem 2(1).

$$
\dot x=\begin{bmatrix}-1&0\\0&2\end{bmatrix}x,\qquad
x(t)=\begin{bmatrix}c_1e^{-t}\\c_2e^{2t}\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** 所有解在同一区间上定义，并按函数加法、数乘构成空间 $S$。映射 $E(x)=x(0)=(c_1,c_2)^T$ 线性；每个初值对应唯一解；不同初值不会得到同一个解。因此 $E$ 是 $S\to\mathbb F^2$ 的同构，其中函数和初值使用同一域 $\mathbb F=\mathbb R$ 或 $\mathbb C$。

**English:** Solutions on a common interval form a space $S$ under function addition and scalar multiplication. Evaluation $E(x)=x(0)=(c_1,c_2)^T$ is linear; each initial value gives exactly one solution; distinct initial values give distinct solutions. Thus $E:S\to\mathbb F^2$ is an isomorphism, using the same field $\mathbb F=\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$ for both functions and initial values.

$$
S=\operatorname{span}\left\{
\begin{bmatrix}e^{-t}\\0\end{bmatrix},
\begin{bmatrix}0\\e^{2t}\end{bmatrix}\right\},\qquad \dim S=2.
$$

**中文：** 对一般常数矩阵 $A$，同样用初值存在唯一性证明 $x\mapsto x(0)$ 同构，逆映射可写成 $c\mapsto e^{At}c$。这里的维数是独立初值参数的个数，不是函数取值次数的个数。

**English:** For a general constant matrix $A$, existence and uniqueness likewise prove that $x\mapsto x(0)$ is an isomorphism, with inverse $c\mapsto e^{At}c$. Dimension counts independent initial-value parameters, not the number of times at which a function is evaluated.
