# 核与像及秩零度 / Kernel, Range, and Rank-Nullity

[阅读导航 / Guide](00_阅读导航.md) · [术语与公式 / Reference](08_术语与公式速查.md)

> 讲义内容 / Lecture content: slides 23-24; Definition 1.27 and Theorem 1.16.

## 1. 核在输入空间，像在输出空间 / Kernel in the domain, range in the codomain

**中文：** 对线性映射 $T:U\to V$，核记录所有被送到零的输入；像记录所有可以得到的输出。课件用 $\mathcal N(T)$ 表示核，用 $\mathcal R(T)$ 表示像；常见记号还包括 $\ker T$、$\operatorname{im}T$。

**English:** For $T:U\to V$, the kernel consists of inputs mapped to zero, and the range consists of all attainable outputs. The slides use $\mathcal N(T)$ and $\mathcal R(T)$; common alternative notation is $\ker T$ and $\operatorname{im}T$.

$$
\boxed{\ker T=\{u\in U:T(u)=0\}},\qquad
\boxed{\operatorname{im}T=\{T(u):u\in U\}}.
$$

**中文：** 核是 $U$ 的子空间：两个核向量的线性组合仍被映成零。像是 $V$ 的子空间：两个像的线性组合也是某个输入线性组合的像。下面两条等式分别给出封闭性证明。

**English:** The kernel is a subspace of $U$, since a combination of kernel vectors still maps to zero. The range is a subspace of $V$, since a combination of images is the image of the corresponding input combination. The following identities prove closure.

$$
u,v\in\ker T\Longrightarrow T(au+bv)=aT(u)+bT(v)=0,
$$

$$
aT(u)+bT(v)=T(au+bv)\in\operatorname{im}T.
$$

## 2. 从矩阵坐标回到抽象空间 / From matrix coordinates back to the space

**中文：** 若 $A=[T]_{C\leftarrow B}$，核通过 $Ax=0$ 求得，但 $x$ 是输入基 $B$ 下的坐标。像是原矩阵列空间在输出基 $C$ 下的还原，不能把两种空间混为一谈。

**English:** If $A=[T]_{C\leftarrow B}$, solve $Ax=0$ for kernel coordinates in the domain basis $B$. The range is reconstructed from the original matrix's column space in the codomain basis $C$. Coordinate spaces and the original abstract spaces must be distinguished.

$$
\ker T=\{Bx:Ax=0\},\qquad
\operatorname{im}T=\{Cy:y\in\operatorname{col}A\}.
$$

**中文：** 这里 $Bx$ 和 $Cy$ 表示基向量的线性组合。定理 1.16 的第一条说明，像由输入基的全部像生成；可以从中挑出线性无关的一部分作为像的基。

**English:** Here $Bx$ and $Cy$ denote linear combinations of basis vectors. The first statement of Theorem 1.16 says that images of all domain basis vectors span the range; select an independent subset as a range basis.

$$
\operatorname{im}T=\operatorname{span}\{T(b_1),\ldots,T(b_n)\}.
$$

## 3. 秩、零度和维数守恒 / Rank, nullity, and the dimension formula

**中文：** 秩是像的维数，零度是核的维数。若输入空间有限维，定理 1.16 给出秩零度公式。右边是输入维数，不是输出维数；同时映射的秩等于任一基下表示矩阵的秩。

**English:** Rank is the dimension of the range, and nullity is the dimension of the kernel. For a finite-dimensional domain, Theorem 1.16 gives rank-nullity. The sum equals the domain dimension, not the codomain dimension. The rank of a map also equals the rank of its matrix in any bases.

$$
\boxed{\dim\ker T+\dim\operatorname{im}T=\dim U},\qquad
\boxed{\operatorname{rank}T=\operatorname{rank}A}.
$$

### 为什么成立：扩充核的基 / Why it holds: extend a kernel basis

**教学补充证明 / Supplementary proof：** 取核的一组基 $k_1,\ldots,k_s$，扩充为输入空间的基
$k_1,\ldots,k_s,u_{s+1},\ldots,u_n$。核基向量的像都是零，所以剩余 $n-s$ 个像生成整个像空间。

**English:** Choose a kernel basis $k_1,\ldots,k_s$ and extend it to a domain basis
$k_1,\ldots,k_s,u_{s+1},\ldots,u_n$. Kernel basis vectors map to zero, so the remaining $n-s$ images span the range.

**中文：** 若这些像有关系 $\sum_{j=s+1}^n a_jT(u_j)=0$，则 $\sum a_j u_j$ 在核中，也能由 $k_1,\ldots,k_s$ 表示。这样得到整组输入基的线性关系，迫使所有 $a_j=0$。因此这些像无关，像维数为 $n-s$。

**English:** A relation $\sum_{j=s+1}^n a_jT(u_j)=0$ places $\sum a_j u_j$ in the kernel, so it is also a combination of $k_1,\ldots,k_s$. This yields a dependence relation among the complete domain basis and forces every $a_j=0$. The images are independent, giving range dimension $n-s$.

## 4. 完整例题：函数空间中的核与像 / Worked example: kernel and range in a function space

> 教学补充，使用 Homework 2 第 1 题的数据 / Teaching supplement using Homework 2, Problem 1.

$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}1&1&-1\\0&1&2\\1&2&1\end{bmatrix},\qquad
\operatorname{rref}(A)=\begin{bmatrix}1&0&-3\\0&1&2\\0&0&0\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** 由行最简形得 $x_1=3x_3$、$x_2=-2x_3$。选 $x_3=1$，核的坐标基为 $(3,-2,1)^T$，还原成原空间的函数如下。

**English:** The reduced form gives $x_1=3x_3$ and $x_2=-2x_3$. Taking $x_3=1$ produces the kernel coordinate basis $(3,-2,1)^T$, which reconstructs the following function in the original space.

$$
\ker\mathcal A=\operatorname{span}\{3\alpha_1(t)-2\alpha_2(t)+\alpha_3(t)\}.
$$

**中文：** 主元在第 1、2 列，选的是原矩阵的第 1、2 列 $(1,0,1)^T$ 与 $(1,1,2)^T$，不是行最简形的列。它们在给定输出基下还原后，得到像的一组基。

**English:** Pivots occur in columns 1 and 2. Choose columns $(1,0,1)^T$ and $(1,1,2)^T$ of the original matrix, not the reduced matrix. Reconstructing them in the specified codomain basis gives a range basis.

$$
\operatorname{im}\mathcal A
=\operatorname{span}\{\alpha_1(t)+\alpha_3(t),\,
\alpha_1(t)+\alpha_2(t)+2\alpha_3(t)\}.
$$

$$
\dim\ker\mathcal A=1,\quad
\dim\operatorname{im}\mathcal A=2,\quad 1+2=3.
$$

**中文：** 若再使用题中的节点列 $\alpha_1=(1,1,1)^T$、$\alpha_2=(1,1,0)^T$、$\alpha_3=(1,0,0)^T$，核函数的节点列为 $(2,1,3)^T$。它与坐标列 $(3,-2,1)^T$ 不同，进一步说明“基下坐标”和“函数节点值”不是同一件事。

**English:** Using the given nodal columns $\alpha_1=(1,1,1)^T$, $\alpha_2=(1,1,0)^T$, and $\alpha_3=(1,0,0)^T$, the kernel function has nodal column $(2,1,3)^T$. This differs from its basis-coordinate column $(3,-2,1)^T$, emphasizing that basis coordinates and function nodal values are different descriptions.

## 5. 做题步骤与易错点 / Procedure and pitfalls

| 中文 | English |
| --- | --- |
| 写清输入基、输出基及矩阵大小。 | Record both bases and the matrix size. |
| 解 $Ax=0$，为每个自由变量找一个坐标基向量。 | Solve $Ax=0$ and obtain one coordinate basis vector per free variable. |
| 找主元列编号，再取原矩阵相应列。 | Find pivot indices, then take those columns of the original matrix. |
| 用输入基还原核，用输出基还原像。 | Reconstruct the kernel in the input basis and the range in the output basis. |
| 用零度加秩等于列数检验。 | Check that nullity plus rank equals the number of columns. |

**中文：** 行变换保留齐次方程的解，因此适合求核；它通常改变列空间，因此不能直接用行最简形的列作为原映射的像。核、像的维数不依赖换基，但它们的坐标列会改变。

**English:** Row operations preserve solutions of homogeneous equations, so they are suitable for finding the kernel. They generally change the column space, so reduced columns cannot directly represent the original map's range. Kernel and range dimensions are independent of basis choice, although their coordinate columns change.
