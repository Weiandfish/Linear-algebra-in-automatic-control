# 换基与矩阵等价 / Basis Changes and Matrix Equivalence

[阅读导航 / Guide](00_阅读导航.md) · [术语与公式 / Reference](08_术语与公式速查.md)

> 讲义内容 / Lecture content: slides 15-18; Theorem 1.13 and Definition 1.24.

## 1. 先固定过渡矩阵的方向 / Fix the transition convention first

**中文：** 将输入基由 $B$ 换成 $B'=BP$，输出基由 $C$ 换成 $C'=CQ$。这里 $P$ 的列是新输入基在旧输入基下的坐标，$Q$ 的列是新输出基在旧输出基下的坐标。因此旧坐标与新坐标满足 $x=Px'$、$y=Qy'$。

**English:** Change the domain basis from $B$ to $B'=BP$ and the codomain basis from $C$ to $C'=CQ$. Columns of $P$ give the new domain basis in old domain coordinates; columns of $Q$ do the same for the codomain. Thus old and new coordinates satisfy $x=Px'$ and $y=Qy'$.

$$
B'=BP,\quad C'=CQ,\quad x=Px',\quad y=Qy'.
$$

**中文：** 设旧矩阵为 $A$，由 $y=Ax$ 代入坐标关系，得到定理 1.13。按从右到左的顺序理解：先把新输入坐标转为旧输入坐标，再应用旧矩阵，最后把旧输出坐标转为新输出坐标。

**English:** Let the old matrix be $A$. Substituting these coordinate relations into $y=Ax$ gives Theorem 1.13. Read the operations from right to left: convert new input coordinates to old ones, apply the old matrix, then convert the output to its new coordinates.

$$
Qy'=APx'\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\boxed{A'=Q^{-1}AP}.
$$

**中文：** 输入和输出可以独立换基；$P$ 是 $n\times n$，$Q$ 是 $m\times m$，$A$ 和 $A'$ 都是 $m\times n$。只有变换 $T:V\to V$ 且两端使用同一换基时，才可令 $Q=P$。

**English:** Domain and codomain bases may change independently. The sizes are $n\times n$ for $P$, $m\times m$ for $Q$, and $m\times n$ for $A,A'$. Set $Q=P$ only for an endomorphism $T:V\to V$ when the same change of basis is used on both sides.

## 2. 矩阵等价 / Matrix equivalence

**中文：** 定义 1.24 说：两个同型矩阵 $A,M\in\mathbb F^{m\times n}$ 等价，是指存在可逆矩阵 $L\in\mathbb F^{m\times m}$、$R\in\mathbb F^{n\times n}$，使 $M=LAR$。课件将这两个可逆因子记作 $Q,P$；这里改用 $L,R$，以免和上一节的基过渡矩阵混淆。

**English:** Definition 1.24 says that matrices $A,M\in\mathbb F^{m\times n}$ are equivalent if $M=LAR$ for invertible $L\in\mathbb F^{m\times m}$ and $R\in\mathbb F^{n\times n}$. The slides call the factors $Q,P$; here they are called $L,R$ to avoid confusion with the preceding basis-transition matrices.

$$
\boxed{M\cong A\iff M=LAR\ \text{for invertible }L,R}.
$$

**中文：** $Q^{-1}$ 也是可逆矩阵，所以换基公式与这个定义相容。等价矩阵可以描述同一映射在不同输入、输出基下的表示。它们的秩相同，但数值元素通常不同。

**English:** Since $Q^{-1}$ is invertible, the basis-change formula fits this definition. Equivalent matrices can represent one map in different domain and codomain bases. Their ranks agree, although their entries generally differ.

### 等价关系的三条性质 / The three equivalence properties

**中文：** 自反性用单位矩阵；对称性用逆矩阵；传递性把两个变换合起来。第 17 页传递性的最后一个结论有笔误，正确结论是 $N\cong A$。

**English:** Reflexivity uses identity matrices, symmetry uses inverses, and transitivity combines two transformations. The final transitivity conclusion on slide 17 has a typo; the correct conclusion is $N\cong A$ in the notation below.

$$
A=I_mAI_n,
$$

$$
M=LAR\Longrightarrow A=L^{-1}MR^{-1},
$$

$$
M=LAR,\quad N=L_2MR_2
\Longrightarrow N=(L_2L)A(RR_2).
$$

## 3. 初等行列变换的含义 / Meaning of elementary operations

**中文：** 左乘初等矩阵对应行变换，右乘初等矩阵对应列变换。可逆矩阵能够写成初等矩阵的乘积，因此一串初等行、列变换产生等价矩阵。列变换允许改变输入坐标，行变换允许改变输出坐标。

**English:** Left multiplication by an elementary matrix performs a row operation; right multiplication performs a column operation. An invertible matrix is a product of elementary matrices, so a sequence of row and column operations produces an equivalent matrix. Column operations change input coordinates; row operations change output coordinates.

**教学补充例题 / Supplementary example：**

$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}1&2&3\\2&4&6\end{bmatrix},\quad
L=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\-2&1\end{bmatrix},\quad
R=\begin{bmatrix}1&-2&-3\\0&1&0\\0&0&1\end{bmatrix},
$$

$$
LAR=\begin{bmatrix}1&0&0\\0&0&0\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** $R$ 将第 2、3 列分别减去第 1 列的 2、3 倍；$L$ 再将第 2 行减去第 1 行的 2 倍。两因子都可逆，变换前后秩均为 1。进一步的教学补充结论是：同型矩阵等价，当且仅当秩相同，因为都可用行列变换化为同一个含 $I_r$ 的标准形。

**English:** The factor $R$ subtracts two and three times column 1 from columns 2 and 3; $L$ subtracts twice row 1 from row 2. Both factors are invertible, and both matrices have rank one. A further supplementary result is that matrices of the same size are equivalent exactly when they have equal rank, since row and column operations reduce them to the same canonical form containing $I_r$.

## 4. 相似是更特殊的换基 / Similarity is a more specific basis change

> 教学补充，与 Homework 2 第 3、5 题相连 / Teaching supplement connected to Homework 2, Problems 3 and 5.

**中文：** 若 $T:V\to V$ 的输入、输出使用同一基，并同步换为同一新基，则
$A'=P^{-1}AP$，称为相似。相似要求左右因子互为逆矩阵；一般等价没有这个限制。

**English:** For $T:V\to V$, if domain and codomain use the same basis and change together to the same new basis, the formula is $A'=P^{-1}AP$, called similarity. Its left and right factors are inverses; general equivalence has no such restriction.

$$
\boxed{A'=P^{-1}AP}.
$$

**中文：** 例如 $I_2$ 与 $\operatorname{diag}(1,2)$ 都可逆，所以等价；但 $P^{-1}I_2P=I_2$ 对任意可逆 $P$ 都成立，所以两者不相似。不能仅凭秩相同判定相似。

**English:** For example, $I_2$ and $\operatorname{diag}(1,2)$ are invertible and hence equivalent. They are not similar, because $P^{-1}I_2P=I_2$ for every invertible $P$. Equal rank alone does not establish similarity.

### 作业第 3 题为何两组基下矩阵相同 / Why Problem 3 gives the same matrix in both bases

**中文：** 题中映射满足 $\mathcal A\epsilon_i=\eta_i$。把过渡矩阵记作 $P$，由 $H=EP$，可知映射的旧矩阵为 $P$，新矩阵为 $P^{-1}PP=P$。这个相同结果来自题目特殊的基像关系，不表示所有换基都保持矩阵不变。

**English:** In Problem 3, the map sends each $\epsilon_i$ to $\eta_i$. To avoid using the same symbol for the map and transition matrix, write $H=EP$. The old map matrix is then $P$, and the new one is $P^{-1}PP=P$. Equality here follows from the special basis-image relation; matrices generally change under a change of basis.

**中文：** 做题时先写“新基 = 旧基 × $P$”，再用 $AP=PA'$ 检验结果。这个等式避免显式求逆，也能有效发现过渡矩阵方向写反的问题。

**English:** Start with “new basis = old basis × $P$,” then check the result with $AP=PA'$. This identity avoids explicitly computing an inverse and helps catch a reversed transition convention.
