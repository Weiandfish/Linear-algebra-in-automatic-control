# 复合映射 / Composition of Linear Mappings

[阅读导航 / Guide](00_阅读导航.md) · [术语与公式 / Reference](08_术语与公式速查.md)

> 讲义内容 / Lecture content: slides 25-27; Theorem 1.17.

## 1. 先做右边的映射 / Apply the rightmost map first

**中文：** 设 $S:U\to W$，$T:W\to V$，先把输入经过 $S$，再把结果经过 $T$，得到复合映射 $T\circ S:U\to V$。右边的 $S$ 先作用，因为它最先接触输入向量。

**English:** Let $S:U\to W$ and $T:W\to V$. Apply $S$ to the input, then apply $T$ to the result. The composition is $T\circ S:U\to V$. The rightmost map $S$ acts first because it meets the input first.

$$
U\xrightarrow{S}W\xrightarrow{T}V,\qquad
\boxed{(T\circ S)(u)=T(S(u))}.
$$

**中文：** 能否复合取决于空间是否衔接。$T\circ S$ 有定义不代表 $S\circ T$ 有定义；即使都能定义，结果也通常不同。

**English:** Composition requires compatible spaces. The existence of $T\circ S$ does not guarantee that $S\circ T$ is defined; even when both exist, the results generally differ.

## 2. 复合后仍线性 / Composition remains linear

**中文：** 第 26 页分别证明加法性和齐次性。也可一次证明它保持任意线性组合：先使用 $S$ 的线性性，再使用 $T$ 的线性性。

**English:** Slide 26 proves additivity and homogeneity separately. They can also be proved together by first using the linearity of $S$, then the linearity of $T$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(T\circ S)(au+bv)
&=T\big(aS(u)+bS(v)\big)\\
&=aT(S(u))+bT(S(v))\\
&=a(T\circ S)(u)+b(T\circ S)(v).
\end{aligned}
$$

## 3. 复合对应矩阵乘法 / Composition corresponds to matrix multiplication

**中文：** 令 $U,W,V$ 的基分别为 $B,C,D$，维数分别为 $n,p,m$。设 $S$ 的矩阵为 $M$，$T$ 的矩阵为 $A$。输入坐标依次经历 $x\mapsto Mx\mapsto AMx$，所以复合矩阵是 $AM$。

**English:** Choose bases $B,C,D$ for $U,W,V$, whose dimensions are $n,p,m$. Let $M$ represent $S$ and $A$ represent $T$. Coordinates pass through $x\mapsto Mx\mapsto AMx$, so the composition matrix is $AM$.

$$
M=[S]_{C\leftarrow B}\in\mathbb F^{p\times n},\quad
A=[T]_{D\leftarrow C}\in\mathbb F^{m\times p},
$$

$$
\boxed{[T\circ S]_{D\leftarrow B}=AM\in\mathbb F^{m\times n}}.
$$

**中文：** 第 27 页定理 1.17 把 $T:W\to V$ 的矩阵维数印成了 $p\times m$；正确应为 $m\times p$，才能与 $p\times n$ 的右侧矩阵相乘。做题时先检查矩阵的输入、输出维数，就能发现这类问题。

**English:** Theorem 1.17 on slide 27 prints the matrix size for $T:W\to V$ as $p\times m$. It should be $m\times p$, allowing multiplication by the $p\times n$ matrix on the right. Checking domain and codomain dimensions first catches this error.

## 4. 完整例题 / Worked example

> 教学补充 / Teaching supplement.

$$
S:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R^3,\quad S(x,y)=(x,y,x+y)^T,
$$

$$
T:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R^2,\quad T(a,b,c)=(a+c,b-c)^T.
$$

**中文：** 全部采用标准基，分别写出矩阵后相乘。先做 $S$，再做 $T$，所以 $T$ 的矩阵在左。

**English:** Using standard bases throughout, write the two matrices and multiply. Since $S$ acts first and $T$ second, the matrix of $T$ is on the left.

$$
M=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\0&1\\1&1\end{bmatrix},\qquad
A=\begin{bmatrix}1&0&1\\0&1&-1\end{bmatrix},\qquad
AM=\begin{bmatrix}2&1\\-1&0\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文：** 直接代入也得到 $T(S(x,y))=(2x+y,-x)^T$。对 $(x,y)=(1,2)$，先得到 $(1,2,3)^T$，再得到 $(4,-1)^T$，与矩阵乘法一致。反向复合 $S\circ T$ 则作用于 $\mathbb R^3$，矩阵是 $3\times3$ 的 $MA$，不能把它当成同一个映射。

**English:** Direct substitution gives $T(S(x,y))=(2x+y,-x)^T$. For $(1,2)$, the intermediate result is $(1,2,3)^T$ and the final result is $(4,-1)^T$, agreeing with matrix multiplication. The reverse composition $S\circ T$ acts on $\mathbb R^3$ and has the $3\times3$ matrix $MA$; it is a different map.

## 5. 中间空间必须使用同一基 / The middle coordinate basis must match

**中文：** 教学补充：直接相乘还要求 $S$ 的输出坐标和 $T$ 的输入坐标使用同一组中间基。如果前者用 $C$，后者用 $C'$，即使维数相同，也要先转换坐标。

**English:** Teaching supplement: direct multiplication also requires the output coordinates of $S$ and the input coordinates of $T$ to use the same middle basis. If the first uses $C$ and the second uses $C'$, a coordinate conversion is needed even when the dimensions match.

$$
C'=CP\Longrightarrow [w]_{C'}=P^{-1}[w]_C,
$$

$$
[S]_{C\leftarrow B}=M,\quad [T]_{D\leftarrow C'}=A'
\Longrightarrow
\boxed{[T\circ S]_{D\leftarrow B}=A'P^{-1}M}.
$$

**中文：** 例如中间空间一维，旧基为 $(1)$，新基为 $(2)$，$P=(2)$。若 $S$ 的旧输出坐标为 $3x$，而 $T$ 读取新输入坐标 $z$ 并输出 $4z$，复合输出是 $4(3x/2)=6x$，不是 $12x$。

**English:** For a one-dimensional middle space, let the old basis be $(1)$ and the new basis $(2)$, so $P=(2)$. If $S$ outputs old coordinates $3x$ and $T$ reads new coordinates $z$ and outputs $4z$, the composition is $4(3x/2)=6x$, not $12x$.

## 6. 与核、像和逆映射的联系 / Connections to kernel, range, and inverses

**教学补充 / Teaching supplement：** 被 $S$ 送到零的向量当然也被复合送到零；复合的输出都在 $T$ 的像内。有限维时，复合的秩不可能超过任一阶段的秩。

**English:** Anything sent to zero by $S$ is also sent to zero by the composition, and every composed output lies in the range of $T$. In finite dimensions, the rank of the composition cannot exceed either stage's rank.

$$
\ker S\subseteq\ker(T\circ S),\qquad
\operatorname{im}(T\circ S)\subseteq\operatorname{im}T,
$$

$$
\operatorname{rank}(T\circ S)
\le\min\{\operatorname{rank}T,\operatorname{rank}S\}.
$$

**中文：** 如果两阶段都是同构，复合也同构。撤销时必须倒序：先撤销最后的 $T$，再撤销最先的 $S$。

**English:** If both stages are isomorphisms, so is their composition. Undoing them reverses the order: first undo the final stage $T$, then undo the initial stage $S$.

$$
(T\circ S)^{-1}=S^{-1}\circ T^{-1},\qquad
(AM)^{-1}=M^{-1}A^{-1}.
$$
