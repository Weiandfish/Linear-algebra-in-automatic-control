# 矩阵表示与映射空间 / Matrix Representations and the Space of Maps

[阅读导航 / Guide](00_阅读导航.md) · [术语与公式 / Reference](08_术语与公式速查.md)

> 讲义内容 / Lecture content: slides 8-14; Theorem 1.12 and Definition 1.23.

## 1. 一列就是一个基向量的像坐标 / Each column records one basis image

**中文：** 设 $T:U\to V$，输入空间的有序基为 $B=(b_1,\ldots,b_n)$，输出空间的有序基为 $C=(c_1,\ldots,c_m)$。先计算 $T(b_j)$，再用输出基 $C$ 表示它。系数列 $[T(b_j)]_C$ 就是矩阵的第 $j$ 列。

**English:** Let $T:U\to V$, with ordered domain basis $B=(b_1,\ldots,b_n)$ and ordered codomain basis $C=(c_1,\ldots,c_m)$. Compute $T(b_j)$, then express it in basis $C$. The coefficient column $[T(b_j)]_C$ is column $j$ of the matrix.

$$
T(b_j)=\sum_{i=1}^m a_{ij}c_i,\qquad
\boxed{[T]_{C\leftarrow B}=
\begin{bmatrix}[T(b_1)]_C&\cdots&[T(b_n)]_C\end{bmatrix}}.
$$

**中文：** 行数 $m$ 来自输出空间，列数 $n$ 来自输入空间。“列是像坐标”中的“坐标”不能省略：像是抽象向量，矩阵的列是指定输出基下的数字列。若输出基不是标准基，直接把像的标准坐标填进去就会出错。

**English:** The $m$ rows come from the codomain, and the $n$ columns from the domain. The word “coordinates” is essential: an image is an abstract vector, while a matrix column is a numerical coordinate column in the specified output basis. Standard coordinates are unsuitable when that basis is nonstandard.

## 2. 为什么得到 $y=Ax$ / Deriving the coordinate equation

**中文：** 若 $u=\sum_j x_jb_j$，线性性允许逐项映射，再展开每个基向量的像。因为输出基下的表示唯一，最后得到 $y_i=\sum_j a_{ij}x_j$。这就是课件第 10-11 页的推导。

**English:** If $u=\sum_j x_jb_j$, linearity maps the combination term by term. Expand each basis image in the output basis; uniqueness of coordinates then gives $y_i=\sum_j a_{ij}x_j$. This is the derivation on slides 10-11.

$$
\begin{aligned}
T(u)&=\sum_j x_jT(b_j)
=\sum_jx_j\sum_i a_{ij}c_i\\
&=\sum_i\left(\sum_j a_{ij}x_j\right)c_i.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boxed{[T(u)]_C=A[u]_B},\qquad A=[T]_{C\leftarrow B}.
$$

**中文：** 这里没有把向量 $u$ 本身变成数字列；是选定基后，用 $[u]_B$ 描述它。Homework 2 第 1 题中，核的坐标 $(3,-2,1)^T$ 应还原成函数 $3\alpha_1(t)-2\alpha_2(t)+\alpha_3(t)$。

**English:** The vector $u$ itself has not become a numerical column; it is described by $[u]_B$ after a basis is chosen. In Homework 2, Problem 1, kernel coordinates $(3,-2,1)^T$ must be reconstructed as the function $3\alpha_1(t)-2\alpha_2(t)+\alpha_3(t)$.

## 3. 固定基后，矩阵和映射一一对应 / Fixed bases give a unique correspondence

**中文：** 定理 1.12 说明：给定两组基后，每个 $m\times n$ 矩阵对应唯一的线性映射。构造方法是先规定 $T(b_j)=\sum_i a_{ij}c_i$，再用线性性定义一般向量的像。基展开唯一，因此这个定义不会产生歧义。

**English:** Theorem 1.12 states that, once both bases are fixed, each $m\times n$ matrix determines a unique linear map. Set $T(b_j)=\sum_i a_{ij}c_i$, then define all other images by linearity. Unique basis expansions make the definition unambiguous.

$$
T\left(\sum_j x_jb_j\right)
\mathrel{:=}\sum_jx_j\sum_i a_{ij}c_i.
$$

**中文：** 反过来，若两个线性映射在每个基向量上的像相同，那么它们在任何向量上的像都相同。所以无需逐个检查空间里的所有向量；检查一组基即可。

**English:** Conversely, if two linear maps agree on every basis vector, they agree on every vector. A basis therefore suffices to determine or compare linear maps.

## 4. 映射本身也组成线性空间 / Linear maps themselves form a linear space

**中文：** 记 $\mathcal L(U\to V)$ 为所有从 $U$ 到 $V$ 的线性映射。定义加法和数乘时，对同一个输入分别求像，再在 $V$ 中做运算。输入空间和输出空间必须一致，才能相加。

**English:** Let $\mathcal L(U\to V)$ be the set of all linear maps from $U$ to $V$. Addition and scalar multiplication are defined by applying the maps to the same input and operating on their outputs in $V$. Maps must have the same domain and codomain to be added this way.

$$
(T+S)(u)=T(u)+S(u),\qquad (aT)(u)=aT(u).
$$

**中文：** 零元素是零映射，$T$ 的加法逆元是 $-T$。在固定输入、输出基下，加法和数乘与矩阵运算完全对应。这是第 13-14 页的结论。

**English:** The zero element is the zero map, and the additive inverse of $T$ is $-T$. With fixed input and output bases, these operations correspond exactly to matrix addition and scalar multiplication, as stated on slides 13-14.

$$
[aT+bS]_{C\leftarrow B}
=a[T]_{C\leftarrow B}+b[S]_{C\leftarrow B}.
$$

**教学补充 / Teaching supplement：** 因此矩阵表示是线性同构，且映射空间的维数为 $mn$。可把“只将 $b_j$ 映成 $c_i$、其余输入基向量映成零”的 $mn$ 个映射作为基。

**English:** Matrix representation is therefore a linear isomorphism, and the map space has dimension $mn$. A basis consists of the $mn$ maps that send one $b_j$ to one $c_i$ and all other domain basis vectors to zero.

$$
\mathcal L(U\to V)\cong\mathbb F^{m\times n},\qquad
\dim\mathcal L(U\to V)=mn.
$$

## 5. 例题：非标准多项式基下的求导 / Example: differentiation in a nonstandard polynomial basis

> 教学补充，使用 Homework 2 第 4 题的基 / Teaching supplement using the basis in Homework 2, Problem 4.

$$
B=(1+\lambda+\lambda^2,\ 1+\lambda,\ 1+2\lambda)
=(\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3),\quad D(p)=p'.
$$

**中文：** 输入和输出都在次数不超过 2 的多项式空间中，并使用同一基 $B$。先求导，再在 $B$ 中展开。利用 $1=2\alpha_2-\alpha_3$，得到三列坐标。

**English:** Both domain and codomain are the polynomials of degree at most two, using the same basis $B$. Differentiate first, then expand in $B$. The identity $1=2\alpha_2-\alpha_3$ gives the three coordinate columns.

$$
D\alpha_1=\alpha_3,\quad
D\alpha_2=2\alpha_2-\alpha_3,\quad
D\alpha_3=4\alpha_2-2\alpha_3,
$$

$$
\boxed{[D]_B=\begin{bmatrix}0&0&0\\0&2&4\\1&-1&-2\end{bmatrix}}.
$$

**中文：** 检验取 $p=2\alpha_1+\alpha_2-\alpha_3=2+\lambda+2\lambda^2$。矩阵输出坐标 $(0,-2,3)^T$，还原后为 $-2\alpha_2+3\alpha_3=1+4\lambda$，与直接求导一致。零行或非零行的位置取决于所用的基，不能凭标准单项式基的习惯猜矩阵。

**English:** Check $p=2\alpha_1+\alpha_2-\alpha_3=2+\lambda+2\lambda^2$. Matrix multiplication gives $(0,-2,3)^T$, reconstructing $-2\alpha_2+3\alpha_3=1+4\lambda$, exactly the derivative. The locations of zero and nonzero rows depend on the chosen basis; a familiar monomial-basis pattern cannot be assumed.

$$
[D]_B\begin{bmatrix}2\\1\\-1\end{bmatrix}
=\begin{bmatrix}0\\-2\\3\end{bmatrix}.
$$
