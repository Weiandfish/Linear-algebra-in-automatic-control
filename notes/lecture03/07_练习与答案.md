# 练习与答案 / Exercises and Answers

[阅读导航 / Guide](00_阅读导航.md) · [术语与公式 / Reference](08_术语与公式速查.md)

**中文：** 以下 8 道题是教学补充，覆盖第三讲主要概念。先独立写出定义、矩阵或证明，再看答案。数学计算共用一套公式，题目与解释均有中英文对照。

**English:** These eight supplementary exercises cover the main concepts of Lecture 3. Write your own definitions, matrices, or proofs before checking the answers. Mathematical calculations are shared; questions and explanations are bilingual.

## E1. 判断线性性 / Testing linearity

**题目：** 判断 $T,S:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R^2$ 是否线性，并说明理由。

**Question:** Decide whether the following maps are linear and justify your answers.

$$
T(x,y)=(x+y,x-2y)^T,\qquad S(x,y)=(x+y+1,y)^T.
$$

**中文答案：** $T$ 线性，因为它是固定矩阵乘法；$S$ 不线性，因为 $S(0,0)=(1,0)^T\ne0$。

**English answer:** The map $T$ is linear because it is multiplication by a fixed matrix. The map $S$ is not linear because $S(0,0)=(1,0)^T\ne0$.

$$
T(x,y)=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\1&-2\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}x\\y\end{bmatrix}.
$$

## E2. 求导矩阵 / Differentiation matrix

**题目：** 对 $D:\mathbb R_2[\lambda]\to\mathbb R_2[\lambda]$、$D(p)=p'$，用基 $B=(1,\lambda,\lambda^2)$ 求矩阵、核与像。

**Question:** Find the matrix, kernel, and range of differentiation on polynomials of degree at most two, using $B=(1,\lambda,\lambda^2)$.

**中文答案：** 三个基向量的像依次为 $0,1,2\lambda$，它们的坐标组成矩阵的三列。常数的导数为零，所有一次及常数多项式都有二次以内的原函数。

**English answer:** The basis images are $0,1,2\lambda$; their coordinates give the three columns. Constants have zero derivative, and every polynomial of degree at most one has an antiderivative of degree at most two.

$$
[D]_B=\begin{bmatrix}0&1&0\\0&0&2\\0&0&0\end{bmatrix},\quad
\ker D=\operatorname{span}\{1\},\quad
\operatorname{im}D=\operatorname{span}\{1,\lambda\}.
$$

## E3. 恒等映射也会有非单位矩阵 / A nonidentity matrix for the identity map

**题目：** 恒等映射 $I:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R^2$ 的输入基为 $B=(e_1,e_2)$，输出基为 $C=(e_1,e_1+e_2)$。求矩阵，并解释它为何不是单位矩阵。

**Question:** Find the identity map's matrix with domain basis $B=(e_1,e_2)$ and codomain basis $C=(e_1,e_1+e_2)$. Explain why it is not the identity matrix.

**中文答案：** $e_1=1c_1+0c_2$，$e_2=-c_1+c_2$，所以两列分别为 $(1,0)^T$、$(-1,1)^T$。映射保留向量，但输入、输出采用不同的坐标。

**English answer:** Since $e_1=c_1$ and $e_2=-c_1+c_2$, the columns are $(1,0)^T$ and $(-1,1)^T$. The map preserves vectors, but its input and output coordinates use different bases.

$$
[I]_{C\leftarrow B}=\begin{bmatrix}1&-1\\0&1\end{bmatrix}.
$$

## E4. 相似换基 / Similarity under a basis change

**题目：** 旧矩阵和基过渡矩阵如下，其中 $B'=BP$。求新矩阵并检查换基方向。

**Question:** With the following old matrix and basis transition $B'=BP$, find the new matrix and check the direction of the change.

$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\0&2\end{bmatrix},\qquad
P=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文答案：** 新矩阵为 $P^{-1}AP$。第二个新基向量为 $e_1+e_2$，其像 $e_1+2e_2$ 的新坐标为 $(-1,2)^T$，验证了第二列。

**English answer:** The new matrix is $P^{-1}AP$. The second new basis vector is $e_1+e_2$, whose image $e_1+2e_2$ has new coordinates $(-1,2)^T$, confirming column 2.

$$
A'=\begin{bmatrix}1&-1\\0&2\end{bmatrix},\qquad AP=PA'.
$$

## E5. 等价与相似 / Equivalence versus similarity

**题目：** $I_2$ 与 $\operatorname{diag}(1,3)$ 等价吗？相似吗？

**Question:** Are $I_2$ and $\operatorname{diag}(1,3)$ equivalent? Are they similar?

**中文答案：** 等价：可取 $L=\operatorname{diag}(1,3)$、$R=I_2$。不相似：任何 $P^{-1}I_2P$ 都等于 $I_2$，无法得到另一个矩阵。

**English answer:** They are equivalent: take $L=\operatorname{diag}(1,3)$ and $R=I_2$. They are not similar, since $P^{-1}I_2P=I_2$ for every invertible $P$.

## E6. 构造同构 / Constructing an isomorphism

**题目：** 证明 $\Phi:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R_1[\lambda]$、$\Phi(a,b)=a+(a+b)\lambda$ 是同构，并写出逆映射。

**Question:** Prove that $\Phi(a,b)=a+(a+b)\lambda$ is an isomorphism from $\mathbb R^2$ to polynomials of degree at most one, and give its inverse.

**中文答案：** 该映射保持线性组合。对任意输出 $c+d\lambda$，唯一原像为 $(c,d-c)^T$，所以它同时单射、满射。

**English answer:** The map preserves linear combinations. Every output $c+d\lambda$ has the unique preimage $(c,d-c)^T$, establishing both injectivity and surjectivity.

$$
\Phi^{-1}(c+d\lambda)=\begin{bmatrix}c\\d-c\end{bmatrix}.
$$

## E7. 核、像和秩零度 / Kernel, range, and rank-nullity

**题目：** 对 $T:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R^2$、$T(x)=Ax$ 求核、像和维数。解释为何核、像维数之和不是 2。

**Question:** Find the kernel, range, and dimensions of $T:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R^2$, $T(x)=Ax$. Explain why nullity plus rank is not two.

$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}1&0&1\\0&1&1\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**中文答案：** $Ax=0$ 给出 $x_1=x_2=-x_3$。前两列独立且生成全部输出空间，所以秩为 2、零度为 1。两者之和等于输入空间的维数 3。

**English answer:** Solving $Ax=0$ gives $x_1=x_2=-x_3$. The first two independent columns span the entire codomain, so rank is two and nullity is one. Their sum equals the domain dimension, three.

$$
\ker T=\operatorname{span}\{(-1,-1,1)^T\},\qquad
\operatorname{im}T=\mathbb R^2,\qquad 1+2=3.
$$

## E8. 复合顺序 / Order of composition

**题目：** 在标准基下，$S(x,y)=(x,x+y)^T$、$T(x,y)=(x+y,y)^T$。分别求 $T\circ S$ 和 $S\circ T$ 的矩阵。若中间坐标采用不同基，还能直接相乘吗？

**Question:** In standard coordinates, let $S(x,y)=(x,x+y)^T$ and $T(x,y)=(x+y,y)^T$. Find matrices for both orders of composition. Can matrices be multiplied directly when the middle coordinate bases differ?

**中文答案：** $T\circ S$ 先做 $S$，矩阵为 $AM$；逆序复合的矩阵为 $MA$，两者不同。若中间基不同，必须插入坐标转换矩阵，不能直接相乘。

**English answer:** The map $T\circ S$ applies $S$ first and has matrix $AM$. Reversing the maps gives $MA$, a different matrix. Different middle bases require an intervening coordinate conversion.

$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{bmatrix},\quad
M=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\1&1\end{bmatrix},\quad
AM=\begin{bmatrix}2&1\\1&1\end{bmatrix},\quad
MA=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\1&2\end{bmatrix}.
$$
