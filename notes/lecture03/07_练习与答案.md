## E1. 判断线性性 / Testing linearity

**题目：** 对以下 $T,S:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R^2$ 判断是否线性。对线性的映射验证任意线性组合；对非线性的映射给出反例。

**Question:** Test the displayed real maps for linearity, verifying arbitrary linear combinations or giving a counterexample.

$$
\begin{gathered}
T(x,y)=(x+y,x-2y)^T,\\
S(x,y)=(x+y+1,y)^T.
\end{gathered}
$$

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** 把 $T$ 写成固定矩阵乘法，再用分配律检验。这里 $a,b$ 是任意实数，$u,v$ 是任意输入列向量。

**Step 1.** Express $T$ as a fixed matrix and use distributivity for arbitrary real scalars and input columns.

$$
M=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-2\end{pmatrix},\quad T(au+bv)=M(au+bv)=aMu+bMv=aT(u)+bT(v).
$$

**第 2 步。** $S$ 在零输入处产生非零常数项；线性映射必把零映到零，因此 $S$ 不线性。

**Step 2.** The constant offset makes $S$ nonzero at the zero input. This contradicts a necessary consequence of linearity.

$$
\begin{gathered}
S(0,0)=(1,0)^T\ne0;\\
\boxed{T\text{ linear},\quad S\text{ nonlinear}.}
\end{gathered}
$$

## E2. 求导矩阵 / Differentiation matrix

**题目：** 记 $V=\mathbb R_2[\lambda]=\{a+b\lambda+c\lambda^2:a,b,c\in\mathbb R\}$。求导映射 $D:V\to V$ 定义为 $D(p)=p'$，输入、输出均使用基 $B=(1,\lambda,\lambda^2)$。求表示矩阵、核与像，并说明每个集合如何得到。

**Question:** Let $V$ be the real polynomials of degree at most two, including zero, and let $D(p)=p'$. Use $B=(1,\lambda,\lambda^2)$ for both input and output. Find the representation matrix, kernel and image, explaining the steps.

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** 空间的定义给出了所有输入的通式，无须题目另给一个具体多项式。系数可以为零。写出一般输入与求导后的输出，就能看见坐标怎样变化。

**Step 1.** The definition of the space supplies a general input with arbitrary, possibly zero, coefficients. Differentiate it to identify the coordinate transformation.

$$
p=a+b\lambda+c\lambda^2,\quad [p]_B=(a,b,c)^T,\quad D(p)=b+2c\lambda,\quad [D(p)]_B=(b,2c,0)^T.
$$

**第 2 步。** 求矩阵就是找固定矩阵 $M$ 使所有输入满足 $[D(p)]_B=M[p]_B$。求三个基函数的像，并按相同的输出基写坐标作为列，得到同一结果。

**Step 2.** A representation matrix must map every input coordinate column to its output coordinate column. The images of the three basis polynomials give its columns.

$$
\begin{gathered}
\begin{aligned}D(1)&=0,&[D(1)]_B&=(0,0,0)^T,\\D(\lambda)&=1,&[D(\lambda)]_B&=(1,0,0)^T,\\D(\lambda^2)&=2\lambda,&[D(\lambda^2)]_B&=(0,2,0)^T.\end{aligned}\\
\boxed{[D]_B=\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0\\0&0&2\\0&0&0\end{pmatrix}},\quad [D(p)]_B=[D]_B[p]_B.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 3 步。** 核是求导后为零的全部输入。零多项式要求每个幂的系数都为零；常数系数 $a$ 不受限制。

**Step 3.** The kernel contains all inputs with zero derivative. Each output coefficient must vanish, leaving the constant coefficient free.

$$
b+2c\lambda\equiv0\iff b=c=0\quad \Longrightarrow\quad \boxed{\ker D=\{a\cdot1:a\in\mathbb R\}=\operatorname{span}\{1\}}.
$$

**第 4 步。** 像是所有可能输出，首先次数至多为一。还要验证每个一次以内的多项式都有原像；取下面的原函数即可。像的基为 $1,\lambda$，核与像的维数分别为 $1,2$。

**Step 4.** The image consists of attainable outputs of degree at most one. Every such polynomial is attained by the displayed antiderivative, proving equality and giving image dimension two.

$$
\begin{gathered}
D\left(u\lambda+\tfrac v2\lambda^2\right)=u+v\lambda\quad (u,v\in\mathbb R),\\
\boxed{\operatorname{im}D=\operatorname{span}\{1,\lambda\}},\quad 1+2=\dim V=3.
\end{gathered}
$$

## E3. 恒等映射也会有非单位矩阵 / A nonidentity matrix for the identity map

**题目：** 在实二维空间中，$e_1=(1,0)^T,e_2=(0,1)^T$。恒等映射 $I:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R^2$ 定义为 $I(v)=v$，输入基 $B=(e_1,e_2)$、输出基 $C=(c_1,c_2)=(e_1,e_1+e_2)$。求 $[I]_{C\leftarrow B}$，解释所求矩阵的含义及其为何不为单位矩阵。

**Question:** Let $I(v)=v$ on real two-space. Its domain basis is standard and its codomain basis is $(e_1,e_1+e_2)$. Find the representation matrix, explain what it computes and why it is not the identity matrix.

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** 所求矩阵接收输入的 $B$ 坐标，给出输出的 $C$ 坐标。恒等映射保持原向量，但这两套坐标未必相同。

**Step 1.** The matrix takes domain $B$-coordinates to codomain $C$-coordinates. The identity map preserves vectors, while their coordinate columns may differ.

$$
\begin{gathered}
\boxed{[I(v)]_C=[I]_{C\leftarrow B}[v]_B},\\
I(v)=v.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 2 步。** 对任意输入 $v=xe_1+ye_2$，用输出基展开同一个向量。两个系数就是输出坐标，不能直接沿用旧坐标。

**Step 2.** Rewrite a general input vector in the output basis. Its two new coefficients are the output coordinates.

$$
\begin{gathered}
v=xe_1+ye_2=(x-y)e_1+y(e_1+e_2)=(x-y)c_1+yc_2,\quad [v]_B=\begin{pmatrix}x\\y\end{pmatrix},\\
[I(v)]_C=\begin{pmatrix}x-y\\y\end{pmatrix}.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 3 步。** 把这个对所有 $x,y$ 成立的坐标规则写成矩阵乘法，即得答案。也可用两个基向量的像逐列核对。

**Step 3.** Write that general coordinate rule as a matrix product. The images of the domain basis provide a column-by-column check.

$$
\begin{gathered}
\begin{pmatrix}x-y\\y\end{pmatrix}=\boxed{\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}}\begin{pmatrix}x\\y\end{pmatrix},\\
[I(e_1)]_C=(1,0)^T,\quad [I(e_2)]_C=(-1,1)^T.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 4 步。** 例如输入 $e_2$ 的坐标由 $(0,1)^T$ 变成 $(-1,1)^T$，但 $-c_1+c_2=e_2$。若错误使用单位矩阵，则会把输出解释成 $c_2=e_1+e_2$，不再等于原向量。

**Step 4.** For input $e_2$, the output coordinates are $(-1,1)^T$, representing the same vector. Using the identity matrix would instead interpret the output as $c_2$, a different vector.

## E4. 相似换基 / Similarity under a basis change

**题目：** 实线性算子在标准旧基 $B=(e_1,e_2)$ 下矩阵为 $A$，新基 $B'=BP$。求 $P^{-1}$ 与新矩阵，逐列解释，并验证 $AP=PA'$。

**Question:** An operator has standard-basis matrix $A$ and new basis $B'=BP$. Compute the inverse transition and new matrix, interpret the columns and verify the intertwining equation.

$$
\begin{gathered}
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&2\end{pmatrix},\\
P=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.
\end{gathered}
$$

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** 新基为 $b'_1=e_1,b'_2=e_1+e_2$。从新输入坐标到旧坐标乘 $P$，计算后转回新坐标乘 $P^{-1}$，故变换次序如下。

**Step 1.** The new basis vectors are the columns of $P$. Convert new input coordinates to old ones, apply the old matrix and then convert back.

$$
P^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\quad AP=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&2\end{pmatrix},\quad \boxed{A'=P^{-1}AP=\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\0&2\end{pmatrix}}.
$$

**第 2 步。** 第一新基向量的像就是它本身；第二个像需用新基重新展开，因此第二列含 $-1$。再直接检验矩阵等式。

**Step 2.** The first basis vector is fixed. Re-expanding the second image in the new basis explains the negative entry. Direct multiplication checks the result.

$$
\begin{gathered}
T(b'_1)=b'_1,\quad T(b'_2)=e_1+2e_2=-b'_1+2b'_2,\\
PA'=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&2\end{pmatrix}=AP.
\end{gathered}
$$

## E5. 等价与相似 / Equivalence versus similarity

**题目：** 对实常数矩阵 $A=I_2$ 与 $B=\operatorname{diag}(1,3)$，判断等价性和相似性。这里等价指存在可逆 $L,R$ 使 $B=LAR$；相似指存在可逆 $P$ 使 $B=P^{-1}AP$。

**Question:** Determine equivalence and similarity of $I_2$ and $\operatorname{diag}(1,3)$ over the reals. Equivalence permits independent invertible left and right factors; similarity requires inverse factors from the same basis change.

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** 等价允许两边分别选择可逆矩阵。下面给出满足定义的具体因子，并核对它们可逆。

**Step 1.** For equivalence, choose explicit invertible factors satisfying the defining equation.

$$
L=\operatorname{diag}(1,3),\quad R=I_2,\quad \det L=3\ne0,\quad L I_2 R=\operatorname{diag}(1,3)=B.
$$

**第 2 步。** 相似的左右因子被限制为 $P^{-1},P$。中间是单位矩阵时，两者必相消，所以不可能得到 $B$。

**Step 2.** Similarity forces inverse factors. With the identity in the middle they always cancel, making the required matrix impossible.

$$
\begin{gathered}
P^{-1}I_2P=P^{-1}P=I_2\ne B\quad (\forall P\text{ invertible}).\\
\boxed{A,B\text{ equivalent but not similar}.}
\end{gathered}
$$

## E6. 构造同构 / Constructing an isomorphism

**题目：** 记 $\mathbb R_1[\lambda]=\{c+d\lambda:c,d\in\mathbb R\}$。对 $\Phi:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R_1[\lambda]$、$\Phi(a,b)=a+(a+b)\lambda$，证明其线性且双射，并写逆映射。

**Question:** Prove that the displayed map from real coordinate pairs to real polynomials of degree at most one is linear and bijective, and give its inverse.

$$
\begin{gathered}
\Phi:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R_1[\lambda],\\
\Phi((a,b)^T)=a+(a+b)\lambda.
\end{gathered}
$$

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** 同构指线性且双射。先用一般输入和标量检验线性；多项式相等按相同次幂的系数比较。

**Step 1.** An isomorphism is a linear bijection. Verify linearity on generic inputs and scalars, comparing polynomial coefficients.

$$
u=(a,b)^T,\ v=(c,d)^T:\quad \Phi(ru+sv)=(ra+sc)+[r(a+b)+s(c+d)]\lambda=r\Phi(u)+s\Phi(v).
$$

**第 2 步。** 给任意目标多项式 $c+d\lambda$，令其等于 $\Phi(a,b)$。常数项先决定 $a$，一次项再决定 $b$，存在且唯一的解同时证明满射与单射。

**Step 2.** For an arbitrary target polynomial, comparing the constant and linear coefficients determines a unique preimage. Existence proves surjectivity and uniqueness proves injectivity.

$$
\begin{gathered}
a=c,\quad a+b=d\Longrightarrow b=d-c,\\
\boxed{\Phi^{-1}(c+d\lambda)=(c,d-c)^T}.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 3 步。** 最后检查两个复合都还原原对象；输入可以是数对，输出可以是多项式，并不妨碍它们作为线性空间同构。

**Step 3.** Check both compositions. Coordinate pairs and polynomials are different kinds of objects but can still form isomorphic vector spaces.

$$
\begin{gathered}
\Phi(c,d-c)=c+d\lambda,\\
\Phi^{-1}(a+(a+b)\lambda)=(a,b)^T.
\end{gathered}
$$

## E7. 核、像和秩零度 / Kernel, range, and rank-nullity

**题目：** 给定标准基下 $T:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R^2$、$T(x)=Ax$。求核与像的集合、基、维数，核对秩–零度公式，并解释为何维数之和使用 $3$ 而非 $2$。

**Question:** Find the kernel and image, their bases and dimensions, and check rank-nullity for this standard-coordinate map. Explain why the dimensions sum to three rather than two.

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&1\\0&1&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** 写一般输入并令输出两个分量为零，第三个输入分量可以自由取值。

**Step 1.** Solve the two zero-output equations with the third input component free.

$$
Ax=(x_1+x_3,x_2+x_3)^T=0\iff x=(-t,-t,t)^T,\quad \boxed{\ker T=\operatorname{span}\{(-1,-1,1)^T\}}.
$$

**第 2 步。** 对于任意输出 $(u,v)^T$，输入 $(u,v,0)^T$ 都映到它，故像是全部 $\mathbb R^2$。可取标准基作为像的基。

**Step 2.** Every output has preimage $(u,v,0)^T$, so the image is the full codomain with its standard basis.

$$
\begin{gathered}
T\big((u,v,0)^T\big)=(u,v)^T,\\
\operatorname{im}T=\operatorname{span}\{(1,0)^T,(0,1)^T\}=\mathbb R^2.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 3 步。** 零度为核的基向量个数，秩为像的基向量个数。秩–零度公式划分的是输入空间的维数；输出空间维数只限制秩不能超过它。

**Step 3.** Nullity counts kernel basis vectors and rank counts image basis vectors. Rank-nullity partitions the domain dimension; codomain dimension only bounds rank.

$$
\dim\ker T=1,\quad \dim\operatorname{im}T=2,\quad \boxed{1+2=3=\dim\mathbb R^3}.
$$

## E8. 复合顺序 / Order of composition

**题目：** 实线性映射 $S,T:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R^2$ 如下，所有已给坐标均为标准坐标。分别求 $T\circ S$、$S\circ T$ 的作用公式及矩阵；若 $S$ 的输出基和 $T$ 的输入基不同，说明如何衔接。

**Question:** Find both composition formulas and standard matrices for the displayed maps. Explain how to compose representations when the intermediate bases differ.

$$
\begin{gathered}
S(x,y)=(x,x+y)^T,\\
T(x,y)=(x+y,y)^T.
\end{gathered}
$$

**解答 / Solution：**

**第 1 步。** $T\circ S$ 表示先做 $S$ 再做 $T$，按内层到外层逐次代入；反向复合也单独计算。

**Step 1.** Composition applies the inner map first. Substitute sequentially for each order.

$$
\begin{gathered}
T(S(x,y))=T(x,x+y)=(2x+y,x+y)^T,\\
S(T(x,y))=S(x+y,y)=(x+y,x+2y)^T.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 2 步。** 矩阵乘法的右因子先作用。先按各映射的两个标准基像求矩阵，再依次相乘；两个结果确实不同。

**Step 2.** The rightmost matrix acts first. Form the two standard matrices and multiply in the corresponding orders.

$$
\begin{gathered}
A=[T]=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\quad M=[S]=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\1&1\end{pmatrix},\quad \boxed{[T\circ S]=AM=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}},\\
\boxed{[S\circ T]=MA=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix}}.
\end{gathered}
$$

**第 3 步。** 若中间基不同，$S$ 输出的坐标列必须先改写为 $T$ 所接收的坐标。设 $S$ 输出基为 $C$、$T$ 输入基为 $D$，定义 $K$ 满足 $[w]_D=K[w]_C$，则应插入 $K$。

**Step 3.** For different intermediate bases, convert the output coordinates of $S$ into the coordinates expected by $T$. Define $K$ by the displayed conversion and insert it between the map matrices.

$$
M_C=[S]_{C\leftarrow B},\quad A_D=[T]_{E\leftarrow D},\quad [T(S(v))]_E=A_DK M_C[v]_B.
$$
