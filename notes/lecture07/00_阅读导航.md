## 符号与必要条件 / Notation and conditions

- **中文：** $A$ 是常数矩阵；$\lambda I-A$ 是特征矩阵，其不变多项式和初等因子决定 $A$ 的标准形。复数情形取 $A\in\mathbb C^{n\times n}$；讨论实 Jordan 形时取 $A\in\mathbb R^{n\times n}$。

  **English:** $A$ is constant, while $\lambda I-A$ is its characteristic matrix. Its invariant polynomials and elementary divisors determine the forms of $A$. Work over $\mathbb C$ for complex Jordan form and with real $A$ for real Jordan form.

- **中文：** 作业的 $j$ 表示虚数单位，$j^2=-1$。因子 $\lambda+1+j$ 的根是 $-1-j$，不是 $-1+j$。每个正指数初等因子对应一个块，相同因子的多次出现要分别保留。

  **English:** The homework uses $j^2=-1$. The root of $\lambda+1+j$ is $-1-j$. Each positive-power elementary divisor gives one block; repeated occurrences remain separate blocks.

- **中文：** 本套采用课件的上次对角线为 $1$ 的 $J_m(\rho)$。实二阶块采用 $R(a,b)=\begin{bmatrix}a&b\\-b&a\end{bmatrix}$，$b>0$；共轭对的实块大小是原复块大小的两倍。

  **English:** Use the lecture's Jordan convention with ones on the superdiagonal. Real blocks use $R(a,b)=\begin{bmatrix}a&b\\-b&a\end{bmatrix}$, $b>0$, and have twice the dimension of one complex block in the conjugate pair.

- **中文：** 广义特征向量的确切阶数 $k$ 要求 $(A-\rho I)^kx=0$ 且 $(A-\rho I)^{k-1}x\ne0$。Jordan 链按“普通特征向量在前、较高阶向量在后”的顺序排列。

  **English:** Exact generalized-eigenvector order $k$ requires both $(A-\rho I)^kx=0$ and $(A-\rho I)^{k-1}x\ne0$. Order a chain from its ordinary eigenvector to its higher-order vectors.

## 讲义表述与阅读线索 / Clarifications and reading pointers

**中文：** 第 7 页把不变多项式与行列式写在一起，应理解为“特征矩阵的不变多项式”；行列式只是它们的乘积。第 8—9 页指数为零的因子不产生 Jordan 块，也不写负阶单位矩阵。第 13、14 页内容重复，笔记合并讲解。

**English:** The wording on slide 7 refers to invariant polynomials of the characteristic matrix; its determinant is only their product. Zero exponents on slides 8-9 contribute no Jordan block and do not call for negative-sized identity matrices. Slides 13 and 14 repeat the same result and are combined here.

**中文：** 第 12 页的链定义额外要求链尾不属于 $\operatorname{im}(A-\rho I)$，即不可继续延长。本笔记先说明基本链关系，再区分不可延长链；所有链头均明确要求非零。构造完整 Jordan 基还须检查链头独立、长度与初等因子一致、总列数为 $n$。

**English:** Slide 12 additionally requires the terminal vector to lie outside $\operatorname{im}(A-\rho I)$, making its chain nonextendible. These notes explain the basic chain relation first and then this extra condition, always requiring a nonzero head. A complete Jordan basis must also have independent heads, correct elementary-divisor lengths and exactly $n$ columns.

**中文：** 课件第 10 页引用教材第 103 页例 3.5，但没有给出数值题设；第 3—5、21 页仅引用教材第 96、115 页。这里保留阅读线索，不编造教材原例。新增证明和数值计算均标为教学补充或作业例题。

**English:** Slide 10 refers to textbook Example 3.5 on page 103 without providing its numerical statement. Slides 3-5 and 21 refer to pages 96 and 115. These remain reading pointers; added proofs and numerical calculations are marked as teaching supplements or homework examples.
