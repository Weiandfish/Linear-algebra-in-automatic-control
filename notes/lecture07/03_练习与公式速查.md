# 练习与公式速查 / Exercises and Formula Reference

[阅读导航 / Guide](00_阅读导航.md) · [Jordan 标准形 / Jordan forms](01_复数与实数Jordan标准形.md) · [链与子空间 / Chains and subspaces](02_Jordan链与子空间分解.md)

**中文：** 以下 8 题为教学补充。复数情形默认在 $\mathbb C$ 上，实 Jordan 形的问题假设原矩阵为实矩阵。先核对块长与总维数，再核对链与坐标。

**English:** These eight exercises are teaching supplements over $\mathbb C$, with real matrices assumed when asking for real Jordan form. Check block sizes and total dimension before checking chains and coordinates.

## 练习 / Exercises

### 1. 初等因子与块长 / Elementary divisors and block sizes

**题目：** 初等因子为 $(\lambda-2)^3$、$\lambda-2$、$(\lambda+1)^2$，求复 Jordan 形和各特征值的两种重数。

**Question:** Given elementary divisors $(\lambda-2)^3$, $\lambda-2$ and $(\lambda+1)^2$, find the complex Jordan form and both multiplicities at each eigenvalue.

$$
J=\operatorname{diag}(J_3(2),[2],J_2(-1)).
$$

**解：** 大小为 $6$。特征值 $2$ 的代数、几何重数为 $4,2$；$-1$ 的两种重数为 $2,1$。重复根的指数必须按块分别保留。

**Answer:** The total size is six. At two, algebraic and geometric multiplicities are four and two; at minus one they are two and one. Keep the block exponents separate.

### 2. Smith 形与实共轭配对 / Smith form and real conjugate pairing

**题目：** 某实矩阵的复初等因子为 $(\lambda-1)^2$、$\lambda+2-i$、$\lambda+2+i$。求特征矩阵的 Smith 形、自然标准形与两种 Jordan 形。

**Question:** A real matrix has complex elementary divisors $(\lambda-1)^2$, $\lambda+2-i$ and $\lambda+2+i$. Find its characteristic-matrix Smith form, natural form and both Jordan forms.

**解：** 每个根只有一个初等因子，因此只有一个非常数不变多项式

**Answer:** There is one elementary divisor at each root, giving one nonconstant invariant polynomial:

$$
p=(\lambda-1)^2((\lambda+2)^2+1),\qquad S=\operatorname{diag}(I_3,p),\quad N=C(p).
$$

$$
J_{\rm complex}=\operatorname{diag}(J_2(1),[-2+i],[-2-i]),\qquad
J_{\rm real}=\operatorname{diag}(J_2(1),R(-2,1)).
$$

**中文：** 总大小为 $4$，不是初等因子的个数 $3$。 / **English:** The total size is four, not the number of elementary divisors, three.

### 3. 确切阶数与截短链 / Exact order and truncated chains

**题目：** 令 $N=J_3(0)$、$x=e_2$。$x$ 是几阶广义特征向量？$(e_1,e_2)$ 是否已不可延长？

**Question:** For $N=J_3(0)$ and $x=e_2$, what is its exact generalized-eigenvector order? Is the sequence $(e_1,e_2)$ nonextendible?

**解：** $Nx=e_1\ne0$、$N^2x=0$，所以确切阶数为 $2$，不是 $3$。递推链可用 $e_3$ 继续延长，因为 $Ne_3=e_2$，所以它尚不满足课件的不可延长条件。

**Answer:** Its exact order is two because $Nx=e_1\ne0$ and $N^2x=0$. The recurrence extends through $e_3$, so the truncated sequence does not satisfy the lecture's nonextendibility condition.

### 4. 只验 AX=XJ 是否足够 / Is the intertwining equation enough?

**题目：** 对 $A=\operatorname{diag}(J_2(0),[0])$，取两条序列 $(e_1,e_2)$ 与 $(e_1)$，拼成 $X=(e_1,e_2,e_1)$。虽满足 $AX=X\operatorname{diag}(J_2(0),[0])$，是否得到换基矩阵？

**Question:** For $A=\operatorname{diag}(J_2(0),[0])$, combine $(e_1,e_2)$ and $(e_1)$ into $X=(e_1,e_2,e_1)$. Does the intertwining equation make $X$ a change-of-basis matrix?

**解：** 不成立，$X$ 的秩为 $2$，两条序列的链头重复。应把单向量序列换为 $(e_3)$，此时得到完整独立基。还须注意，单独的 $(e_1)$ 本身可延长。

**Answer:** No. The rank is two and the heads are repeated. Replace the one-vector sequence by $(e_3)$ to obtain a full basis. The sequence $(e_1)$ is also extendible by itself.

### 5. 求一条链与换基矩阵 / Finding a chain and a transition matrix

**题目：** 对 $A=\begin{bmatrix}2&3\\0&2\end{bmatrix}$，求 $X$ 使 $X^{-1}AX=J_2(2)$。

**Question:** For $A=\begin{bmatrix}2&3\\0&2\end{bmatrix}$, find $X$ such that $X^{-1}AX=J_2(2)$.

$$
x_1=3e_1,\quad x_2=e_2,\qquad
X=\begin{bmatrix}3&0\\0&1\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**解：** $(A-2I)x_2=x_1$、$(A-2I)x_1=0$，且 $\det X=3\ne0$，故 $AX=XJ_2(2)$。

**Answer:** The chain relations hold and $\det X=3\ne0$, giving $AX=XJ_2(2)$.

### 6. 共轭二阶复块变成多大的实块 / The size of a real block

**题目：** 一对复块 $J_2(2i)$、$J_2(-2i)$ 对应哪个实 Jordan 块？

**Question:** Which real Jordan block corresponds to the pair $J_2(2i)$ and $J_2(-2i)$?

$$
\mathcal R_2(0,2)=\begin{bmatrix}
0&2&1&0\\-2&0&0&1\\0&0&0&2\\0&0&-2&0
\end{bmatrix}.
$$

**解：** 是四阶实块；对角放 $R(0,2)$，上次块对角线放 $I_2$。对应的实不可约因子幂为 $(\lambda^2+4)^2$。

**Answer:** It is a real block of order four, with $R(0,2)$ on its diagonal and $I_2$ on its block superdiagonal. The real irreducible power is $(\lambda^2+4)^2$.

### 7. 从核维数推块长 / Recovering sizes from nullities

**题目：** 对某特征值 $\rho$，已知 $h_0,h_1,h_2,h_3,h_4=0,3,5,6,6$，求该根的 Jordan 块大小。

**Question:** At eigenvalue $\rho$, nullities are $h_0,h_1,h_2,h_3,h_4=0,3,5,6,6$. Determine its Jordan block sizes.

**解：** 增量为 $3,2,1,0$，即至少一阶、二阶、三阶、四阶的块数分别为 $3,2,1,0$。相邻再相减，恰好一阶、二阶、三阶各一个。故块长为 $3,2,1$，代数重数为 $6$，几何重数为 $3$。

**Answer:** The increments $3,2,1,0$ count blocks of sizes at least one, two, three and four. Their consecutive differences give one block of each size one, two and three. Thus the sizes are $3,2,1$, algebraic multiplicity six and geometric multiplicity three.

### 8. 普通特征空间、Jordan 子空间与抽象向量 / Eigenspaces, Jordan subspaces and abstract vectors

**题目：** 算子 $T$ 在基 $(\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3)$ 下的矩阵是 $\operatorname{diag}(J_2(1),[3])$。写出特征值 $1$ 的普通特征空间与 Jordan 子空间。

**Question:** An operator has matrix $\operatorname{diag}(J_2(1),[3])$ in basis $(\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3)$. Find its ordinary eigenspace and Jordan subspace at one.

$$
E_1(T)=\operatorname{span}\{\alpha_1\},\qquad
G_1(T)=\operatorname{span}\{\alpha_1,\alpha_2\}.
$$

**解：** $(T-I)\alpha_2=\alpha_1$，所以 $\alpha_2$ 是二阶广义特征向量，不能作为普通特征向量。另一分量为 $G_3(T)=\operatorname{span}\{\alpha_3\}$，故 $V=G_1(T)\oplus G_3(T)$。

**Answer:** Since $(T-I)\alpha_2=\alpha_1$, the vector $\alpha_2$ is generalized of order two, not ordinary. The other component is $G_3(T)=\operatorname{span}\{\alpha_3\}$, and $V=G_1(T)\oplus G_3(T)$.

## 核心公式 / Core formulas

| 内容 / Topic | 公式与条件 / Formula and conditions |
| --- | --- |
| 初等因子与块 / Divisor and block | $(\lambda-\rho)^m\leftrightarrow J_m(\rho)$，每次出现分别计数 / Count each occurrence |
| 重数 / Multiplicities | 代数重数为块长之和，几何重数为块数 / Sum of sizes versus number of blocks |
| Smith 单位项 / Smith units | $\lambda I-A$ 有 $n$ 个非零不变多项式；$\ell$ 个非常数项外还有 $n-\ell$ 个 $1$ / Retain unit entries |
| 实共轭块 / Real paired block | 两个 $m$ 阶共轭复块变为一个 $2m$ 阶实块 / A pair becomes one block of order $2m$ |
| 广义向量确切阶 / Exact order | $(A-\rho I)^kx=0$，$(A-\rho I)^{k-1}x\ne0$ |
| 链 / Chain | $(A-\rho I)x_1=0$，$x_1\ne0$；$(A-\rho I)x_{r+1}=x_r$ |
| 换基验证 / Basis verification | $AX=XJ$ 且 $\det X\ne0$ / Both conditions are required |
| Jordan 子空间 / Jordan subspace | $G_\rho=\ker(A-\rho I)^n$，维数为代数重数 / Dimension equals algebraic multiplicity |
| 直和 / Direct sum | $\mathbb C^n=\bigoplus_\rho G_\rho$ |
| 核维数增量 / Nullity increments | 长度至少 $k$ 的块数为 $h_k-h_{k-1}$ / Number of blocks of size at least $k$ |

**中文：** 自查：根的符号是否正确；相同初等因子是否分别保留；总块大小是否等于维数；实共轭块是否配对；链头是否独立；换基矩阵是否可逆；坐标列是否已正确还原为抽象向量。

**English:** Check root signs, repeated elementary divisors, total block size, conjugate pairing, independent heads, invertibility of the transition matrix and correct conversion from coordinates to abstract vectors.
